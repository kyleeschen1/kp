# Subtract parts of the same size

Subtracting an amount requires keeping track of which quantity is removed.
Changing its expression must preserve both that quantity and the minus sign.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Express the third as two sixths, then subtract its count from five. Follow the
minus sign into the numerator before the arithmetic is evaluated.
:::

The remaining three sixths can also be written as one half.
