# Give both fractions a shared unit

Neither sixths nor eighths can directly name the parts in both fractions.
Twenty-fourths can. Each addend needs its own equivalent expression.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Watch each numerator and denominator take the same factor: four on the left,
three on the right. The plus sign keeps the two quantities separate until
their parts share a size.
:::

Four twenty-fourths and three twenty-fourths make seven twenty-fourths.
