# Count the same-sized parts

Adding fractions means counting parts of a common size. Changing the name of
the parts, adding their counts, and simplifying the result are different moves.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Follow what changes and what stays fixed. First express both quantities in
sixths. Then count the sixths. Finally recognize the result as one half.
:::

The denominator names the size of a part; the numerator counts those parts.
During addition the size stays fixed. During simplification the size and count
change together, preserving the same quantity.
