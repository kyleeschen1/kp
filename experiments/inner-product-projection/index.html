Why divide by b · b?

Write the parallel part as p = αb. The remainder is r = a − αb. Requiring r · b = 0 gives a · b − α(b · b) = 0. Solving for α gives the projection formula, provided b is nonzero.

Try reversing b. The scalar α changes sign, but αb—and therefore the projected vector—stays the same.
