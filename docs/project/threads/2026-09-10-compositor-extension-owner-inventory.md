`prepareKpCanonicalNativeKatexScene` reconciles/measures tracks, compiles successor
plans, applies semantic and operation motion, projects tracks, partitions
successor/factoring ownership, then routes the remaining tracks. Protected-plan
compilation inspects those routed tracks and separately invokes factoring's
inspection. Renderer-ready assembly subsequently combines successor material with
factoring material; playback combines that material with sampled ordinary tracks.
This ordering is why an ordinary-track certificate is not a final-scene audit.

`KpNativeKatexRendererReadyScenePlan` in `native-katex-base-scene-plan.ts` carries
an optional callback and an independent protected-transit certificate. The live
plan issuer validates stages, track IDs and dwell, but not callback participation.
The lower-level renderer constructor also accepts the callback directly. Both
must be considered; changing only a host does not seal the shared boundary.

## Bounded migration inventory

Production search for `supplementalMaterialOwners`, `sampleMaterialOwners` and
`inspectTransit` identifies the ready-plan/renderer seam plus two producer owners:
factoring and successor synthesis. Successor synthesis itself covers materially
different evaluation and identity-transfer programs; one successful factoring
case cannot certify them. The bounded second mechanism should be existing
evaluation source/catalyst/result material, subject to s04 actual paint evidence.
M1b's product variant is factoring shape/orientation pressure, not a different
ownership mechanism. Existing identity-transfer callers remain in the migration
inventory and must retain truthful coverage boundaries.

Not new extension producers: ordinary track projection, reorder/fan-in routing,
native typography/handoff and the shared material DOM painter. They remain
preservation boundaries, not justification for another engine. Cross-domain
graph/code/WebGL mechanisms are outside this native-equation migration.

`KpEquationMaterialLayerOwnerFrame.expectedPaintRect` and `.paintAlignmentRect`
are optional in the general paint contract. The measured contribution seam
must therefore establish its own required measured-frame invariant; a cast or
copied flag cannot supply it. Do not globally force every unrelated material
consumer to adopt the new contract. Native observations carry font/style and
stage-coordinate evidence; cached pure plans already compare input signature and
geometry. Extend those responsible boundaries instead of duplicating measurement.

## What must be retired or coupled

- Independent callback plus independent inspection metadata at ready-plan and
  renderer-session construction.
- Factoring-only publication authorization as a substitute for final-scene
  coverage (retain motif-specific fusion checks and diagnostics).
- Any path where an extension's actual sampler changes while old evidence is
  still treated as current.

Retain motif contact policies. Required fusion contact is not a collision error;
transit intersections remain reported diagnostics. Sampling evidence is finite,
not proof that unsampled motion is clear. Actual native paint and exclusive
ownership remain separate executed checks.
