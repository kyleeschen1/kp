## Goal

Make KP smaller to understand, cheaper to extend, and harder for humans or LLMs
to route through obsolete behavior. Preserve the semantic compiler and proven
publication capabilities while reducing representations, compatibility,
implicit dependencies, vocabulary drift, and project-memory retrieval cost.

## Current Decision

The project is in a bounded convergence phase governed by
`../decisions/2026-08-12-kp-convergence-and-architecture-compression.md`.
Architecture work must be complexity-negative and tied to an observable caller,
production route, compatibility retirement, or generation-quality measure.

The bottleneck to a public product is product convergence: a small set of
finished explanations, one release-worthy default projection, navigation and
responsive fit, and coherent packaging. Layout selection is one required gate,
not a mandate to encode one universal layout in Article or animation semantics.

## Accepted Scope

- documentation and terminology compression;
- production-reachability and dependency analysis;
- removal of rejected product representations;
- compatibility retirement adjacent to migrations;
- narrow public APIs and import-boundary enforcement;
- TypeScript/Python program-animation pressure;
- generation fixtures, typed diagnostics, and quality measures; and
- one bounded public-product projection decision after the proof portfolio.

## Preservation Boundary

- `kp.article.v1` and canonical article source;
- versioned vignette imports;
- semantic identity, provenance, operations, correspondence, and lineage;
- one deterministic clock and direct seek/rewind;
- native settled KaTeX, SVG graph semantics, static publication, and
  accessibility;
- approved economics, algebra, physics, vector, and catalogue evidence;
- the bounded Scheme factorial semantic trace and current checkpoint; and
- route and active-frame budgets unless deliberately tightened.

## Architecture ROI Gate

A tightening or new-caller slice succeeds when it reduces or holds flat:

- production render/runtime paths;
- compatibility entries;
- implicit registration;
- caller-specific branches;
- visual exceptions;
- initial route closure; and
- time from semantic input to human-reviewable output.

A caller that needs a new clock, scheduler, paint owner, lesson-local semantic
store, or geometry authored by the model fails the gate and must simplify or
remain local.

## LLM Performance And Generation

Future model sessions should receive the roadmap, this thread, the system
vocabulary, the generation entry point, and only the selected task's source
refs. Historical reviews are retrieved on demand.

Generation quality is measured by:

- valid governed draft on first attempt or after one typed repair;
- correct canonical operation and motif selection;
- exact semantic-role binding;
- absence of paint, geometry, timing, or framework authority in generated
  output;
- deterministic compilation and direct seeking;
- no silent generic fallback; and
- marginal implementation cost for the second caller.

## Out Of Scope

- a repository-wide namespace or package move before dependency boundaries are
  enforced;
- another general animation engine or scene graph;
- a universal lesson layout;
- broad Graph3D, Canvas, or WebGL promotion;
- arbitrary executable model output;
- live LLM wording during playback;
- full curriculum, Public Editor, accounts, or learner modeling; and
- polishing every historical experiment before pruning it.
