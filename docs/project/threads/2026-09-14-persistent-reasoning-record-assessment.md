## Product thesis

KP publishes durable technical explanations whose consequential transformations
can be locally inspected. The noninteractive reading experience must stand on
its own. Prose, equations, meaningful graph snapshots, code and other records
remain readable, printable and addressable without animation or JavaScript.

**Inspection may change emphasis, but must never erase the argument being
inspected.** The desired behavior is confusion → inspect → clarification →
continue reading, not admiration of animation or successful control operation.

The permanent document is the authoritative *reading surface*. It is not a new
semantic authority: domain-owned checked sources, transformations, assumptions
and provenance remain upstream of both static and interactive projections.
Generate both from those sources rather than maintaining competing truths.

Stable states and named transitions describe the conceptual model. A page
presents an authored route through reasoning and dependencies, not merely a
list of animated equations. Transitions may name substitution, execution,
specialization, inference, differentiation or parameter change, but these are
not all the same logical relation. Preserve equivalence, implication, physical
evolution, approximation and information loss. Reversible inspection does not
establish mathematical invertibility, nor is every intermediate paint frame a
valid standalone mathematical statement.

## Highest-value refinements from the proposal

- Separate document geometry, local inspection coordinate, and visual geometry.
  A long prose interval can provide more drag distance without requiring a long
  glyph flight. Normalize each transition to local progress; the relevant
  presentation determines spatial motion. Playback duration remains intentionally
  designed and independent of prose height. Physical time and parameter variation
  retain their own domain meanings.
- Animate the smallest semantically complete explanatory group, not the smallest
  changed token and not whatever the renderer happens to clone. Persistent objects
  may be crucial participants. Keep full-expression, extracted-subexpression and
  fixed-frame inspection as candidate treatments rather than universal defaults.
- Share interaction expectations across media, not a universal renderer or a
  requirement that everything move downward. Graphs can retain one coordinate
  frame; code can act on a block or execution state; prose may own a conceptual
  inference without animation.
- Separate four explanatory functions: what changed, why it is valid, why it is
  useful here, and what deserves attention. They may be omitted or combined in
  visible prose when sensible, but authoring should not accidentally conflate
  them. This is first an editorial/authoring discipline, not permission for four
  mandatory boxes or a new universal runtime schema.
- Make expandable granularity and provenance/exact return explicit design goals.
  A coarse transition and its finer account should represent the same reasoning,
  with preserved outer endpoints and assumptions. Equal endpoints alone do not
  verify the intervening steps or establish shared provenance.
- Keep the calm reading measure primary. Inline inspection, adjacent inspection
  and full-width prose are layout candidates, not a compulsory three-mode engine.
  The current fixed narrow prose lane is an exemplar compromise, not a standard.

## Qualifications and safeguards

1. Replace “every state appears exactly once” as a universal rule with one
   canonical identity and unambiguous record ownership per occurrence. Recaps,
   comparisons and provenance excerpts may intentionally repeat representations;
   they must remain references, not forked sources of truth. Persistent also does
   not mean everything must be on screen or mounted at once.
2. A useful static graph record need not reproduce every sampled configuration.
   Preserve the claims, significant states and explanatory correspondence needed
   to read the argument; dynamic exploration is optional additional capability.
3. Endpoint resting zones are not permission to snap every interior release.
   Preserve deliberate held inspection. A shared equation boundary may belong to
   either adjacent transition; logical edge identity must survive that ambiguity.
4. Exact return should use stable article/revision, transition and entity anchors,
   expansion state and local progress, with a relative viewport anchor. Raw scroll
   pixels alone are insufficient after reflow. Define an explicit revision-change
   recovery behavior rather than promising impossible pixel-identical restoration.
5. Mobile inspection entry is a hypothesis, not an accepted replacement control.
   Ordinary scrolling and accessible alternatives are non-negotiable; the exact
   affordance can differ from desktop. Do not postpone basic phone readability,
   keyboard access, reduced motion or print until after desktop aesthetics.
6. “Most users” and “at least some users” are discovery prompts, not release
   thresholds or statistical evidence. Specify sample, prerequisites, tasks and
   measures before collecting evidence. Non-use can reflect no genuine confusion,
   poor discoverability, adequate prose or an unhelpful inspection; distinguish
   these explanations before declaring the product thesis disproved.
7. Competitive labels in the supplied text are positioning hypotheses, not a
   researched market audit or proof of uniqueness. Defensibility must come from
   the integrated reading/authoring experience, quality, reuse and outcomes—not
   from claiming competitors cannot add transitions or provenance.

## Acceptance ledger for a future approved prototype

These are outcome requirements to allocate across small experiments, not a new
executable slice list or a requirement to build everything before learning.

### Deterministic integrity and interaction checks

- Complete meaningful static record in logical order without JS; coherent print;
  no required argument hidden solely in animation or optional detail.
- Source/destination agreement at local 0/1; no doubled endpoint ink; identifiable
  permanent records throughout inspection; no meaning change on expansion.
- Reverse samples the same transformation; interior release holds; resting zones,
  keyboard and explicit buttons reach identical logical checkpoints.
- Page scroll never advances inspection; prose height does not change semantic
  endpoints or button playback duration. Rail belongs visually to the derivation.
- Disclosure remeasures layout without resetting progress. Long prose has a
  comfortable measure; narrow layouts do not cause horizontal page overflow.
- Color is not the only state cue; keyboard, reduced motion and non-fine-motor
  navigation remain available. Mobile input preserves normal scrolling and reading
  position; any explicit inspection entrance must preserve exact return.
- One verified coarse transition can expose individually inspectable substeps and
  collapse to the original outer endpoints. Preserve logical location, provenance
  and orientation; do not invent a bijection between coarse and fine progress.
- One local recall shows an earlier result, assumptions and source link; return
  restores active transition, progress, expansion, focus and reading location.
  No decorative multi-screen flying expressions.
- At least one local/fixed-frame transformation demonstrates that long document
  distance need not mean long visual travel. Reuse domain renderers and semantic
  sources when changing inspection treatment.

### Heterogeneous content pressure

Eventually test roughly 6–10 major states containing substitution, cancellation,
structural rearrangement, a subexpression-scale move, a move where whole-expression
carry is inappropriate, an earlier-result recall with assumptions, a conceptual
step explained primarily in prose, expandable verified substeps and validity
disclosure. Choose a meaningful argument first; do not manufacture steps to meet
a quota. Four similar rewrites alone cannot validate the general interaction model.

### Human evidence

Separate static comprehension, control usability and incremental learning benefit.
Use readers who did not build the system and have the declared prerequisites.
Ask for reconstruction/changed-case explanation rather than satisfaction alone.
Observe unprompted behavior before teaching the control. Include deliberately
non-obvious transitions, but do not weaken the static explanation to force use.
Compare equivalent static and interactive content; use matched tasks or ordering
controls so prior exposure is not mistaken for an animation benefit.

Look for deliberate forward/reverse use, recovery from held interiors, useful
depth expansion, successful provenance return, absence of routine duplicate-ink
confusion and attention competition, and repeated spontaneous confusion → inspect
→ clarification → continue. If inspection is only used when prompted, investigate
discoverability, need and utility separately. Small formative studies guide
iteration; they do not establish generalized learning effects.

### Authoring and operational economics

Track time to a coherent static argument and to its supported inspected version;
source edits versus renderer/engine edits; maintained code added per transition;
compile/review/repair effort; initial versus activated browser payload and work.
Reuse and retroactive repair must be demonstrated, not inferred from shared names.
Prefer a cheaper clear static explanation when interaction adds no demonstrated
benefit. Do not make an elaborate engine a prerequisite for content trials.

## Recommended sequence, subject to approval

First finish the current visual checkpoint. Then prioritize one earlier-result
recall with exact return and one coarse-to-fine expansion on the current argument,
in separately reviewable changes. Start with the source fact p = mv and an explicit
account of the mass-factor simplification; use existing supported operations or
surface a bounded repair gap instead of faking new semantic capability.

Only then grow a coherent 6–10-state mechanics argument when it genuinely needs
the heterogeneous cases above. A work–energy derivation is a candidate, not chosen
scope: differentiation/integration support and prerequisite prose must be checked
before committing to it. Run formative static/interactive comparisons before
general-purpose graph infrastructure. Next pressure the interaction model with
one compact fixed-frame graph or code case; extract shared contracts only from
demonstrated common needs. Improve authoring economics alongside each stage.

Non-goals: universal renderer or solver, general mathematical graph database,
automatic generation of every move, full proof verification, sophisticated
cross-document provenance, collaboration, courses, assessment, gamification,
accounts/telemetry, complete author GUI, or exhaustive mobile polish before the
basic product hypothesis is tested. Existing safety and correctness boundaries
remain mandatory. No old loop is resumed or cancelled by recording this brief.
