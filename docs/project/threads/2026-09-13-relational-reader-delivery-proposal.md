## Mathematical scope

Constant-mass Newtonian point particle in a fixed inertial frame. One shared state
supplies position, velocity, net force, momentum and kinetic energy. Check
`dp/dt = F` and `dK/dt = F dot v` in this scope; work requires the actual motion,
not force alone. Work is an interval quantity, not another particle state variable.

Initial fixtures from handoff section 14:

- Straight line: m=1, F=(2,0), initial r=v=0, 0<=t<=2; r=(t²,0), v=p=(2t,0),
  K=2t². End displacement=4, momentum change=(4,0), net work=energy change=8.
- Quarter circle: m=R=omega=1, 0<=t<=pi/2; r=(cos t,sin t),
  v=(-sin t,cos t), F=(-cos t,-sin t). Momentum changes by (-1,-1),
  K=1/2, instantaneous power and net work are zero. Avoid a full-period endpoint
  that hides the net momentum distinction.

The two quantities within one episode share physical time. Different episodes
need not finish together or share a normalized percentage; hold a completed
branch if an explicit comparison coordinate requires it. Narrative reading and
derivation progress remain separate. Presentation easing must not alter physical
truth. Declare numerical tolerances for trigonometric display separately from
analytical identities; do not label sampled numerical evidence a general proof.

cost. The visible comparison comes next because it tests the medium. Return,
