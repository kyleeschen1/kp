## Outcome

Help the reader describe a one-dimensional trip and read its position–time
record. Motivation: another person cannot reconstruct motion from a destination
alone. Choose a point model, reference, length unit and timestamps. Distinguish
the physical path from its graph, physical motion from relabelling coordinates,
and net displacement from total travel. No force, acceleration or calculus lesson
is required yet. A slope/rate invitation connects to lesson 2 without pretending
to complete it here.

The opening example follows a marked object along a tabletop: outward movement,
a pause, and a return. A recorded table and graph describe the same checked
history. Between observations, any interpolated motion is explicitly a model,
not information magically supplied by sparse measurements. Origin change is a
supporting comparison, not the headline achievement.
