## Goal

Make the internal Animation Catalogue the simplest honest view of KP's
executable library: one searchable row per animation asset, one continuously
visible stage, a small universal player, progressive disclosure for machinery,
and review capture that records the exact observed state.

Use catalogue breadth to discover and pressure shared semantic, runtime,
hosting, renderer, control, and review seams without multiplying
representations or designing a universal renderer in advance.

## Accepted Scope

- `/` as the default catalogue route and `/?artifact=<id>` selection;
- a flat fuzzy asset index with advanced metadata query tokens;
- one row per concrete loadable animation asset ID;
- one shared host contract over domain-specific surface adapters;
- a frameless center stage with Play/Pause and scrubber by default;
- keyboard step/reset and stage-native direct manipulation;
- one linear Details, Parameters, or Tuning inspector;
- compact artifact-local bespoke parameters and review-only tuning;
- lower-left review capture with automatic state and screenshot attachment;
- inline KaTeX and `h3`-scale visible headings with semantic structure;
- derived `Ready`, `Review`, and `Broken` health;
- human `Unreviewed`, `Keep`, `Repair`, `Canonical port`, `Merge`, or `Retire`
  disposition;
- an approved solve-x exemplar, then catalogue-wide hosting and inventory;
- retention of useful old routes as hidden diagnostics during migration.
- typed, compiler-owned candidate symbol-motion invariants exercised through
  quotient, distribution, and cancellation exemplars;
- one nominal compiled authority consumed after endpoint measurement and
  before path planning and paint;
- a separately proposed canonical compiler tranche after the active
  three-caller promotion decision, with one clean-path pressure caller before
  further symbolic-family expansion;
- exhaustive supported equation-surface migration after the clean-path human
  checkpoint, with one generated disposition per asset and adjacent
  compatibility retirement;

## Out Of Scope

- roadmap, planned-only, Theseus, or abstract ontology rows in the catalogue;
- domain tabs, ontology trees, nested catalogues, peer representation pickers,
  iframe fallbacks, card grids, or a large masthead;
- a universal renderer or speculative shared surface category;
- canonicalizing temporary presentation tuning without explicit promotion;
- deciding canonical port order before the hosting inventory;
- economics, physics, programming, or other domain implementation merely to
  make the catalogue look complete;
- public website polish, phone-first design, accounts, teacher tools, full
  curriculum, or live LLM editorial generation;
- making Svelte components part of animation, semantic, clock, sampled-frame,
  or renderer-port authority;
- using catalogue state as roadmap priority, approval, or execution authority.
- forcing static, unsupported, diagnostic, graph, code, diagram, or 3D assets
  through equation-specific presentation recipes;
- fixed paths, timing constants, opacity values, or KaTeX geometry as semantic
  authoring authority.
