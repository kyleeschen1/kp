# Inset equation fenceposts

User-approved experiment following acceptance of the equation rail. The user
means visually inset equations, not physical downward motion.

Canonical artifact: `examples/physics/momentum-energy.article.md`, hosted at
`http://localhost:8000/experiments/mechanics-relations/#energy-from-momentum`.
The checked energy derivation remains semantic authority; native KaTeX owns
equation paint, and the existing reader clock owns inspection position.

## Outcome and scope

Make permanent equations feel recessed and distinguish a crisp landing from
the moving expression. Current near-dock fading paints a moving copy while the
record grows darker, which can read as doubled or blurred ink. The candidate
keeps the moving copy crisp and withdraws the nearby stationary paint during
overlap, restoring one native record at exact docking. Other fenceposts remain
legible and subdued, with a small sharp highlight suggesting inset ink.

One package: `fencepost.exemplar`, energy derivation only. Preserve accepted
rail geometry, prose, algebra, native endpoints, font-size recovery, dragging,
edge scrolling, print/static reading and accessibility. No translation, scale,
blur filter, new clock, semantic model, graph/code rollout or catalogue seam.
Rollback unit: the energy-only record projection and stylesheet treatment.

Use a pure overlap/presence check plus one Chromium near-dock capture at source,
interior and destination, including reverse and font-size pressure. Run affected
types/build and existing endpoint/drag regressions. Reserve the final quarter of
a two-hour ceiling for closeout. Stop for human judgment of inset depth, context
legibility and landing clarity before generalizing.

Theseus owns execution and evidence in `run-contract.kp.inset-fenceposts-v1`.

## Candidate evidence and review

The energy desktop record now uses subdued cool ink with a sharp one-pixel
light edge; a docked live equation uses normal dark ink without that edge.
No blur, translation or scale is applied. The moving scene stays fully opaque
between docks. Source/target record presence is zero within the measured row
overlap distance, then returns smoothly only after that distance clears. At an
exact dock the stage yields to the native record. Prose, accessibility content
and record layout remain present. Print overrides transient record suppression.
The provisional phone-local inspection retains its prior appearance.

The pure projection test pressures multiple row distances and font-height scales,
both docks, reversal and invalid geometry. The Chromium candidate checks near
departure, midpoint, near landing, exact landing, reverse, larger text and print;
accepted rail geometry, endpoint ink and cross-step dragging are preservation
checks. Native compositor choreography is unchanged; these checks establish the
bounded reader handoff, not a new renderer-wide compositor certification.

Review the same energy URL: approach the second equation slowly, dock, then
reverse. Judge whether one crisp expression appears to settle into the page,
whether the inset suggestion is perceptible, and whether contextual equations
remain readable. Color/depth and the recovery distance remain provisional.
No new observer, clock or dependency is added; the existing projection writes
one presence property per record from cached geometry.
