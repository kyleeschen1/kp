## Later recommendation

The accepted governing authority is
`../principles/motif-composition-contracts.md`. Preserve the motif's visual
character; transit overlap is diagnostic, not a demand for larger arcs. This
supersedes the initial zero-clearance interpretation of this follow-up.

Make the final assembled native scene the occupancy authority for every extension:

1. Inventory specialized factoring, successor synthesis and supplemental material
   owners against the ordinary scene tracks. Identify which actual painted
   participants are and are not covered by each existing certificate.
2. Use one required extension contribution contract coupling actual material
   sampling to measured occupancy. Preserve operation-owned open registries;
   do not add a central chain of operation-name conditionals.
3. Validate the assembled scene after ownership partitioning, routing and
   specialized contributions. A supplemental owner must not bypass the audit.
   Declare required fusion contact and motif-owned transit contact policy.
   Report factor/context intersections rather than silently ignoring them or
   automatically distorting motion to eliminate them. Endpoint and ownership
   invariants remain hard failures.
4. Bind issued inspection evidence to the exact motion sampler, participants,
   geometry/font revision and coordinate space. Recheck at invalidation and
   cache reuse; copied or stale metadata must not confer authority. Inspection
   coverage is not a collision-free certificate or aesthetic approval.
5. Add negative type fixtures for omitted occupancy and runtime rejection tests
   for unissued/stale/incomplete evidence. Types establish participation and
   provenance, not geometric truth.
6. Pressure the seam with a materially different existing specialized mechanism
   through the real native-to-material-to-native path. Preserve endpoint ownership,
   ink continuity, direct seek, reversal, interruption and reduced motion.
7. State the assurance boundary precisely: finite sampled clearance is not a
   continuous-time proof. Evaluate conservative swept bounds or bounded analytic
   subdivision before claiming unsampled trajectories are guaranteed safe.

## Preservation and promotion

Retain semantic models, authoring source, canonical motifs, native paint, the
shared clock and Focus Card hosts. No new universal scene engine, generic fades,
notation-specific offsets or blanket exemptions. Promote only after an accepted
exemplar and a different caller demonstrate the boundary; use supported-browser
coverage at that promotion point, not as a discovery matrix.

The immediate factoring repair is narrower. It must not be reported as a
catalogue-wide extension certificate or a formal proof for all continuous motion.
The successor proposal must budget migration and verification explicitly and
respect the existing non-TypeScript source/payload cost gates.
