# Compositor participation: assurance boundary

The issued assembly authenticates its exact tracks, contribution samplers,
participant identities and measured endpoint context. Its typed assurance is
`finite-samples-not-continuous-proof`; contact policy is `diagnostic-only`.
Neither a copied audit nor an ordinary-track certificate authorizes extra paint.

The current inspection uses 100 equal progress intervals: interior positions
1/100 through 99/100, plus separately evaluated native endpoint samples. This
does not establish continuous-time separation. A narrow continuous trajectory
can contact another participant entirely between inspected positions. The
executable `finite contact inspection cannot claim safety between its sample
positions` test constructs that case: an empty audit and actual coincident
occupancy at progress 0.005 are both correct observations.

At every requested sample—not only the inspection grid—the contribution seam
requires the exact participant set and finite, nonnegative measured rectangles
with valid opacity. The companion unsampled-progress test proves malformed
paint still fails before rendering and does not contaminate the sample cache.
These hard frame checks do not prove that a supplied rectangle perfectly
describes every raster pixel, or that a stateful callback is deterministic.
Real native/material/native and repeated-seek browser representatives provide
separate bounded evidence for the retained mechanisms.

No arc inflation, collision optimizer, dense universal matrix, or new timing
policy follows from this audit. Motif-owned contacts remain permitted. A future
continuous clearance claim would require an explicit trajectory class and
sound bounds (including clipping, transforms, ownership transitions and font
measurement), with its own approved scope; increasing sample density alone
cannot supply it. The present product does not require that claim.
