## Outcome and boundaries

Give the author a curriculum-shaped map of independently useful reasoning moves
and their visual needs, with granular practical algebra and a systematic first
pass across applied mathematics, economics, classical mechanics and programming.
This is an inventory and implementation audit, not lesson production. It makes
the next explanation or machinery experiment easier to choose. Coverage remains
reviewable and revisable, not a claim that a finite list exhausts mathematics.

Canonical artifact: `docs/project/repertoire/` Markdown; host:
`/experiments/repertoire/`; renderer: existing static Vite projection plus native
discipline selection. Curriculum references justify inclusion; source and tests
justify implementation checks. Preserve existing animations, semantic owners,
reader interactions, and the simple topic/two-list dashboard. No new salience
treatment, renderer, curriculum lesson, paid calls, service, deployment or agents.
Old run contracts remain parked. This task does not execute equation transfer.

Rows name independently demonstrable operations, example inputs/outputs or
observable relationships, necessary assumptions, and bounded implementation
evidence. Distinguish audited gaps from unaudited support; existing semantic-only
or hand-authored support must not silently become a complete authorable move.
Maintain shared algebra/motifs once where possible; domain-specific interpretation
remains distinct. Keep small source files and stable references. No scores,
percentages, dependency graph UI, or new mandatory full-repository audit per edit.

## Required packages and dependencies

1. `curriculum.scope`: cross-check open curriculum sources, define inclusion and
   exclusions, row conventions and honest check semantics. Beneficiary: authors
   deciding what remains unknown. Focused verification: source provenance,
   parse/format checks. Low risk; rollback: conventions and source index.
2. `curriculum.algebra`: detailed arithmetic/algebra inventory; audit relevant
   existing canonical implementations, split logs/fraction variants and direction
   where reasoning differs. Depends on scope. Beneficiary: practical explanation
   authors. Standard verification: content/evidence checks, bounded source/test
   inspection. Main risk is overclaiming support; rollback: algebra topic files
   and its evidence notes, with compatible parser changes if required.
3. `curriculum.domains`: applied mathematics, economics, mechanics, programming
   inventories with examples and bounded audit. Depends on conventions; independent
   of aesthetic review. Required areas are not optional reserves. Beneficiary:
   authors beyond algebra. Standard content checks; one coherent commit per useful
   discipline group is allowed. Main risk: uneven depth; document exclusions and
   unaudited regions. Rollback: individual discipline/topic content.
4. `curriculum.traces`: representative worked-problem step traces across all five
   areas, stable row references, audit holes and consequential next gaps. Depends
   on inventories. Beneficiary: authors needing real derivation coverage rather
   than a catalogue of isolated rules. Focused link/trace validation plus manual
   mathematical reasoning. Report uncertainty and composition gaps explicitly.
5. `curriculum.integration`: readable dashboard at expanded scale, source-only
   update validation, source/evidence links, responsive/no-script navigation,
   review packet and final integration. Depends on inventories and traces.
   Broad boundary: repertoire tests/browser checks, architecture, typecheck/build,
   Theseus validation. Rollback: dashboard projection changes; content remains
   independently readable. Preserve existing visual language; if a new aesthetic
   is necessary, isolate it and park its promotion for review.

Order reflects immediate author value: algebra first, then breadth, then actual
problem-step coverage and final integration. No optional reserves or unrelated
cleanup. Independent approved domain packages may proceed when a local review
dependency is parked. Content granularity and subjective usefulness are reviewed
in one final packet; do not block ordinary inventory authoring on repeated reviews.
