product law. Integral substitution required an exponential antiderivative and
endpoint evaluation, not just a derivative-power motif. These missing rows were
added; adjacent integral-linearity and trigonometric-antiderivative needs were
made explicit. No check was awarded by analogy.

abstraction/data-processing patterns. Their inclusion does not expand this run
into specialized continuum mechanics, database systems or compiler lessons.
