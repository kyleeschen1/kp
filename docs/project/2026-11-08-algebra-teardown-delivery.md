# Algebra reader teardown repair

Approved by the user's “excellent. go as long as possible” continuation of the
bounded Firefox teardown repair recommended after maintenance. This is a new
run, not an extension of the completed maintenance or deferred annotation run.

## Outcome and boundary

Readers and authors can leave, replace, or restore an algebra card without a
remaining browser callback commanding a disposed timeline. Firefox reported
this failure during the maintenance browser checks; the clock correctly rejects
commands after disposal. Establish the actual failing ownership path before
changing it, and retain that strict clock invariant.

Canonical hosts are the reusable-reasoning algebra experiments and their
interactive editions. Their shared algebra card mounts the canonical native
equation session through `src/reader/app/algebra-presentation-mount.ts`;
checked composed-algebra presentations remain semantic authority. Preserve
source/revision identity, semantic playhead, native endpoints, paint, controls,
practice behavior and retained-page restoration. No visual treatment is added.

## Work and checks

1. Reproduce departure/replacement failure through the real card; establish the
   violated lifetime invariant and repair its owning boundary. Include regression
   coverage for visibility before/after final teardown and retained-page pause.
2. Integrate across complete affected card/edition consumers. Run focused
   deterministic tests, Chromium and Firefox algebra mount/edition suites,
   typecheck, architecture and production build checks. Record failures honestly
   and repair related failures within scope. Keep production payload gates.

Implementation and focused evidence form the first reversible commit; integration
evidence and durable context form the second. Theseus owns execution status.
Use existing browser entrypoints, extending their scoped coverage as needed.
An unrelated failing gate may be diagnosed and reported but not waived.

## Authority, limits and finish

Allowed: proportional lifecycle repair, regression checks, related measured
engineering-budget repair and source-linked closeout. Excluded: editor lane,
CSS redesign, new renderer or lifecycle framework, reader annotation rollout,
publication, merging and unrelated maintenance. No human visual approval is
needed for deterministic preservation; changed aesthetics would stop this run.

Ceiling: six hours from the continuation start, reserving the final 45 minutes
for verification and backup. Finish earlier when the failure is reproduced,
the owning invariant repaired, required checks pass, and evidence is committed
and backed up. Stop for a genuine scope/authority blocker, user pause, or ceiling;
unused time does not authorize new work.
