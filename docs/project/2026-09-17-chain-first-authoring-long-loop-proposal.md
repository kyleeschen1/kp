## Outcome and why now

An author supplies a short mathematical chain, prose and sparse assumptions or
intent; KP resolves supported operations, verifies their semantics and projects
the appropriate existing motifs into a persistent reasoning passage. Invalid,
ambiguous and unsupported steps produce specific repairs rather than plausible
animation. The first new end-to-end family is rational fraction addition and
subtraction, after the saved nonterminal equation/scalar transfer.

The audit recovered 12 bounded examples without engine changes. It did not prove
cheap independent authoring or composition. This run addresses that limitation,
following the [accepted target](decisions/2026-09-16-chain-first-semantic-authoring-target.md)
and [saved transfer scope](decisions/2026-09-16-machinery-transfer-and-simple-coverage.md).

complete coverage or high measured LLM precision from a few fixtures.

## Limits, resources and done rule

Initial supported family: explicit rational addition/subtraction chains with
integer numerators and positive integer denominators, exact bounded arithmetic
and authored stops. Negative numerators are semantic pressure, not automatic
visual support. Symbolic denominators, arbitrary algebra, arbitrary chain length,
new log/root laws, a universal scene graph, economics/model inference, new code or
graph renderers, catalogue-wide rollout, deployment and paid model APIs are out.
Existing code/text coordination remains the following medium, not another build
programme inside this loop. No subagents are authorized by this proposal.

Inference here means a bounded proposal-to-checker workflow and a documented
current-session trial. It is not an independently blinded LLM benchmark, training
run or statistical high-precision claim. Generalization across subjects remains
the product trajectory; domain-boundary probes do not certify those curricula.

Estimate: roughly 12–20 active engineering hours across review pauses; uncertain,
especially at fraction presentation boundaries. Proposed ceiling: 24 active hours,
with the final three reserved for verification/closeout. Human waiting does not
consume that execution estimate. Stop earlier when complete; at the ceiling,
record incomplete slices and exact resume state rather than mark them done.
Use brief context packets and one relevant authoring/renderer owner at a time;
working context only for missing authority. No full corpus dumps or token budget.

Allow responsible-boundary bug fixes and standing evidence-backed engineering
budget repairs. Stop for an unresolvable blocker, new product scope, unsafe
authority, user pause or required visual judgment. Do not turn a difficult gap
into an unrelated generic transition merely to finish a slice.
