# Economics

## Macroeconomic accounting, growth and stabilization

### Semantic moves

- [ ] `econ.gdp` Distinguish final output, value added and double counting.
  Example: Count a final good once rather than also adding all intermediate sales
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.real-nominal` Separate nominal change from price-adjusted change.
  Example: Real output uses a stated price-index convention
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.inflation` Compute inflation from an index and distinguish it from the price level.
  Example: π=(P_t-P_(t-1))/P_(t-1)
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.labor` Distinguish unemployment from nonparticipation.
  Example: Unemployment rate divides unemployed people by the labor force
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.accounts` Use national-accounting identities without making them causal theories.
  Example: Y=C+I+G+NX is an accounting relationship
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.saving` Relate saving and investment under stated openness/accounting conventions.
  Example: In a closed economy, national saving equals investment ex post
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.growth-accounting` Separate input accumulation from productivity growth.
  Example: More capital per worker differs from a higher productivity parameter
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.solow` Compare investment and depreciation in a simple growth model.
  Example: k changes according to s f(k)-(δ+n)k under the chosen model
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.ad-as` Separate demand and supply disturbances in an aggregate model.
  Example: An adverse supply shock can raise prices while reducing output
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.multiplier` Derive a simple expenditure multiplier under restrictive assumptions.
  Example: With constant prices and C=cY, an autonomous increment is amplified by 1/(1-c), 0<c<1
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.money` Separate money stocks, bank balance sheets and lending constraints.
  Example: A loan creates matching balance-sheet entries
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.interest` Distinguish nominal and real interest rates.
  Example: 1+r=(1+i)/(1+π) for a realized one-period comparison
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.monetary` Trace a proposed monetary-policy channel with explicit assumptions.
  Example: A policy-rate change may affect financing costs and expenditure
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.fiscal` Distinguish a deficit flow from the accumulated debt stock.
  Example: Debt_t = Debt_(t-1) + deficit_t under a simple accounting convention
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.debt` Compare interest, growth and primary balance in debt dynamics.
  Example: Debt/GDP can rise despite a stable nominal deficit
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.expectations` Separate anticipated from unanticipated changes in a model.
  Example: Expected inflation affects nominal contracts differently from a surprise
  Audit: unaudited

### Visual motifs

- [ ] `motif.econ.stock-flow` Keep stocks and flows visually distinct across periods.
  Example: A deficit adds to a debt stock; they are not interchangeable bars
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.econ.accounting` Trace paired balance-sheet entries without inventing net resources.
  Example: An asset for one party can be a liability for another
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.econ.shock` Retain assumptions while comparing model responses to different shocks.
  Example: The same observed inflation need not imply the same cause
  Audit: unaudited
