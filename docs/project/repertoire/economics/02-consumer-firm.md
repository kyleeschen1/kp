# Economics

## Consumers, firms and market structure

### Semantic moves

- [ ] `econ.budget` Construct a budget constraint with prices and income.
  Example: p_x x+p_y y≤m; quantities nonnegative
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.preferences` Represent preferences without treating utility as directly measurable welfare.
  Example: A monotone utility transformation preserves ordinal choices
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.mrs` Interpret marginal rate of substitution and its sign convention.
  Example: Along u(x,y)=constant, dy/dx=-u_x/u_y when u_y≠0
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.consumer-optimum` Compare interior tangency with corners and affordability.
  Example: u_x/u_y=p_x/p_y is an interior condition, not a universal answer
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.income-substitution` Separate compensated substitution from an income effect.
  Example: A price change alters relative incentives and purchasing power
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.demand-derive` Derive individual demand from constrained choice.
  Example: Choose the utility-maximizing affordable bundle for each price
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.production` Distinguish marginal product from returns to scale.
  Example: Adding one input differs from scaling every input
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.cost` Derive cost minimization for a required output.
  Example: Choose an input bundle on an isoquant subject to input prices
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.profit` Choose output by comparing marginal revenue and marginal cost.
  Example: MR=MC is a candidate condition with shutdown/boundary checks
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.shutdown` Distinguish short-run shutdown from long-run exit.
  Example: Fixed sunk commitments differ from avoidable variable costs
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.competition` Connect price-taking behavior to the relevant marginal condition.
  Example: A competitive firm has MR=P under the model
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.monopoly` Relate market power to demand and marginal revenue.
  Example: For inverse demand P=a-bQ, MR=a-2bQ
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.price-discrimination` Separate group pricing from a change in production cost.
  Example: Different prices can redistribute surplus without changing marginal cost
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.game-best-response` Identify a best response to another player's action.
  Example: Compare payoffs within the opponent's fixed strategy
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.game-nash` Find a mutually consistent set of best responses.
  Example: Neither player can gain by unilateral deviation at a Nash equilibrium
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `econ.game-sequential` Reason backward through a sequential game.
  Example: A later credible choice constrains the earlier decision
  Audit: unaudited

### Visual motifs

- [ ] `motif.econ.constraints` Coordinate a budget or technology constraint with the chosen point.
  Example: An attractive infeasible choice remains visibly infeasible
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.econ.best-response` Highlight one player's comparison while holding the other fixed.
  Example: A row/column comparison does not alter the payoff table
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.econ.marginal-total` Relate marginal increments to accumulated totals.
  Example: Area under marginal cost recovers variable cost under the model
  Audit: unaudited
