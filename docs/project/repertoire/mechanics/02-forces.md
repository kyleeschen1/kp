# Classical mechanics

## Forces, constraints and Newtonian models

### Semantic moves

- [ ] `mech.free-body` Choose a body and identify forces acting on it.
  Example: Normal force, weight and friction belong to the selected body
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.newton` Relate net external force to momentum change.
  Example: ΣF=dp/dt; constant mass gives ma
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.action-reaction` Keep action-reaction forces on different bodies.
  Example: Forces in a third-law pair do not cancel in one body's free-body equation
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.equilibrium` Distinguish zero net force from zero velocity.
  Example: ΣF=0 implies constant momentum, not necessarily rest
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.friction` Distinguish static friction's bound from kinetic friction's model.
  Example: |f_s|≤μ_s N; equality is not always attained
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.incline` Resolve force components along a chosen incline basis.
  Example: Weight components are mg sin θ and mg cos θ
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.tension` Couple bodies through idealized string/pulley constraints.
  Example: Equal tension requires specified idealizations
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.spring` Use a linear restoring-force model within its regime.
  Example: F=-kx relative to equilibrium
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.drag` Compare linear and quadratic drag assumptions.
  Example: -bv and -c|v|v give different speed dependence
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.terminal` Identify terminal speed from balanced forces under drag.
  Example: For downward positive motion with linear drag, mg-bv=0
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.noninertial` Introduce inertial-force terms only with a noninertial frame.
  Example: A rotating-frame centrifugal term is frame-dependent
  Audit: unaudited

### Visual motifs

- [ ] `motif.mech.force-owner` Attach each force to its body and interaction source.
  Example: Third-law partners share an interaction but have different recipients
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.mech.constraint` Retain a geometric constraint while inspecting force balance.
  Example: A rope constraint relates accelerations without becoming a force law
  Audit: unaudited
