# Classical mechanics

## Elementary fluid mechanics

### Semantic moves

- [ ] `mech.fluid.pressure` Relate pressure to normal force per area.
  Example: For uniform pressure, F=pA on a planar surface
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.fluid.hydrostatic` Relate pressure change to depth in a static incompressible fluid.
  Example: p=p₀+ρgh with constant density and gravity
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.fluid.buoyancy` Relate buoyant force to displaced fluid weight.
  Example: F_b=ρ_fluid g V_displaced
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.fluid.continuity` Use conservation of mass in a steady flow.
  Example: ρAv is constant along a stream tube under the stated assumptions
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.fluid.bernoulli` Compare pressure, speed and elevation under ideal-flow assumptions.
  Example: p+ρv²/2+ρgz is constant along a streamline for steady inviscid incompressible flow
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.fluid.regime` Distinguish viscous dissipation from an ideal Bernoulli model.
  Example: A pressure drop in a real pipe can supply dissipative loss
  Audit: unaudited

### Visual motifs

- [ ] `motif.mech.fluid-balance` Track fluxes through a retained control volume.
  Example: Mass entering and leaving determines accumulation
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.mech.pressure` Distinguish local pressure from the net force it produces.
  Example: Opposing pressure forces can largely cancel
  Audit: unaudited
