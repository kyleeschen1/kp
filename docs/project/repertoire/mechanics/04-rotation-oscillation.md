# Classical mechanics

## Rotation, equilibrium, gravitation and oscillation

### Semantic moves

- [ ] `mech.torque` Relate force, lever arm and torque about a chosen origin.
  Example: τ=r×F
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.angular-momentum` Distinguish angular momentum from angular velocity.
  Example: L=r×p for a particle about the chosen origin
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.inertia` Compute moment of inertia relative to an axis.
  Example: I=∫r_perp² dm; changing the axis changes I
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.parallel-axis` Relate parallel-axis moments through the center of mass.
  Example: I=I_cm+Md²
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.rotation-dynamics` Use the fixed-axis rigid-body torque equation within its scope.
  Example: τ_axis=I_axis α for a fixed axis and constant I
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.rolling` Relate translation and rotation under no-slip constraints.
  Example: v_cm=Rω for rolling without slipping
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.static-equilibrium` Require both force and torque balance for a rigid body at rest.
  Example: ΣF=0 and Στ=0
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.angular-conserve` Conserve angular momentum when external torque vanishes.
  Example: Changing I can change ω while L remains fixed
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.gravity` Use central inverse-square gravitation with vector direction.
  Example: F=-GMm r/||r||³ for separation vector r
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.orbit` Connect centripetal acceleration and gravity for a circular orbit.
  Example: v²=GM/r in the point-mass model
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.escape` Use energy to distinguish bound and escaping motion.
  Example: v_escape=√(2GM/r) ignoring drag and other bodies
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.shm` Connect a restoring linear force to harmonic motion.
  Example: m x″=-kx → ω=√(k/m)
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.small-angle` State the approximation behind the small-angle pendulum.
  Example: sin θ≈θ for small θ in radians
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.damping` Distinguish underdamped, critically damped and overdamped motion.
  Example: m x″+b x′+kx=0 depends on b²-4mk
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.driven` Separate driving frequency, natural frequency and resonance.
  Example: Steady response depends on damping and detuning
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.wave` Distinguish propagation of a disturbance from material transport.
  Example: A traveling transverse wave does not carry each material point with its phase
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `mech.wave-superposition` Superpose linear waves and identify standing-wave conditions.
  Example: Opposite traveling waves can produce fixed nodes
  Audit: unaudited

### Visual motifs

- [ ] `motif.mech.rotation` Keep axis, lever arm and force direction coordinated.
  Example: The same force has different torque about different origins
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.mech.oscillation` Coordinate phase, displacement, velocity and energy exchange.
  Example: Turning points have zero speed but nonzero acceleration
  Audit: unaudited
- [ ] `motif.mech.wave` Separate particle motion from phase propagation.
  Example: A marked particle oscillates while a crest travels
  Audit: unaudited
