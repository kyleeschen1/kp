# Author complete explanations in the two preferred formats

Approved direction and execution: the user selected near-term authoring,
long-term mathematical exploration, selective exceptional publications, equation
rails, and horizontal focus cards with text above the stage, then requested a
Theseus plan and execution. This plan owns rationale and scope; Theseus owns
slice order, live status and evidence.

## Outcome

An author can write a complete explanation, bind supported mathematical steps,
preview the exact selected source, revise it, and retain a readable publication
without editing renderer code. First use the accepted mechanics rail to isolate
the authoring problem; this is an enabling trial, not a new mathematical frontier.
Then pressure the path with top-text horizontal cards and one connected argument
about how changes propagate through composition. No claim of general mathematical
authoring, learning effectiveness or exceptional publication quality follows from
passing implementation tests.

## Packages and dependencies

1. **Boundary:** reconcile the accepted affine run, identify exact rail/card
   references, and preserve the new strategic direction. Focused documentation
   and Theseus validation. No visual change.
2. **Rail source:** a short Article selects the existing checked energy/power
   derivations without copying their equation chains. Author-owned prose remains
   distinct from checked mathematics. A local command checks selected bytes and
   prepares an explicit preview/static reading; invalid drafts return located
   repairs and cannot replace the last valid selection. Focused compiler, source
   identity and invalid-selection tests. No new physics or mathematical motion.
3. **Rail exemplar:** integrate the selected source through the existing native
   rail reader, retain source identity, static access, keyboard/drag/reversal and
   refinement behavior. Standard checks plus a small Chromium smoke cohort.
   **Mandatory human checkpoint:** does the source express a whole useful
   passage, and does the preview retain the preferred interaction? Stop before
   card generalization. Commit the reviewable candidate first.
4. **Card caller:** after acceptance, support an authored passage through the
   accepted horizontal focus-card treatment, text above the persistent stage.
   Reuse semantic and playback owners; no global scaffold reordering. Review the
   first source-driven card before promoting this across families.
5. **Connected publication:** after the format checkpoints, author a bounded
   change-and-composition argument. Establish reader question, exact versus
   approximate claims, and available mathematical authority first. Reuse the two
   formats where each has an explanatory job. Unsupported semantic/motion work
   must be reported as a gap; no generic animation substitute. Review one complete
   argument before adding chapters or calling it an exceptional publication.
6. **Reuse and closeout:** after that review, demonstrate a meaningful source-only
   editorial/structural variation, document the author workflow and limitations,
   verify final integration and delivery costs. Independent-human authorship and
   learner outcomes remain unmeasured unless actually observed.

## Execution and limits

Use `run-contract.kp.explanation-authoring-v1`. Commit coherent checked packages
with evidence and back up the feature branch according to standing policy.
Interactive mode; no minimum task count. Initial pre-review ceiling about three
active hours, uncertain, with the final quarter reserved for verification and
handoff. Later packages remain deliberately coarse until prerequisite reviews.
No broad editor, universal proof system, new rendering framework, catalogue
migration, deployment, paid API, or curriculum expansion. No mathematical topic
is selected merely to fill a presentation slot.

Stop for mandatory human review, completion, user pause, the time ceiling, or an
unresolvable scope/authority/safety blocker. Routine repairs and source/bundle/type
budget diagnoses inside this scope remain authorized. Use supported Theseus
commands; preserve failed checks and distinguish a review candidate from acceptance.
