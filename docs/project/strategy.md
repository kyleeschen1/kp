# KP product direction

Last Updated: 2026-11-08
Direction: `authoring-expert-attention-v1`
Authority: [accepted direction](decisions/2026-11-08-authoring-expert-attention.md).
Current commitment and execution boundary: [roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Destination

**KP helps authors create extraordinary explanations that teach people what to
notice. Learners can question, adapt, practice and revisit that material while
preserving its important reasoning and relationships.**

KP remains a nonprofit creating media and tools for thought. The educational
aim is domain literacy: learners acquire an expert's way of seeing authentic
representations, then use that ability independently. Mathematical practice is
the first proving ground; physics, music, code and other domains remain part of
the ambition, with their own meanings and validity rules.

## First product center

Build a responsive authoring and revision studio. Human and LLM authors should
be able to develop rich reasoning, examples, contrasts and precise attention
choreography without engineering each new explanation. Useful drafts and
revisions should arrive promptly. Exceptional material can take thoughtful
editorial work; implementation delays should not dominate that work.

A small collection of exceptional featured content demonstrates the medium,
sets a quality standard and may attract authors and learners. Marketing value,
adoption and educational effectiveness are hypotheses to test, not established
advantages. Early examples develop the system; this direction does not start
public asset production or publication.

Learners adapt material with context intact: ask about an exact part, change
pace or depth, request another example, and return to the argument. Authors can
preserve essential reasoning while designing room for adaptation. Conversation,
pointing, grouping and prediction can reveal what a learner currently perceives.

## Product progression

1. **Create and revise.** An author improves one excellent explanation and its
   attention choreography through a repeatable product workflow.
2. **Adapt and interpret.** A learner receives a responsive, situated variation
   and demonstrates their own interpretation without losing the authored context.
3. **Practice and retrieve.** Related problems, feedback and flashcards develop
   and revisit the same instructional distinctions.
4. **Grow the library.** Curated adaptive material becomes easier to create,
   reuse, embed and maintain across more topics and domains.

These are capability horizons, not four approved engineering campaigns.
The roadmap selects one finite next proof. Existing capabilities must be inspected
before proposing their reconstruction.

## Quality and learning

Preserve motivation, prerequisite meaning, justified transitions, examples,
counterexamples and meaningful questions. Prose and annotations receive their
own moments of attention. Choreography makes relevant structure perceptible;
guidance should be able to withdraw as understanding develops.

The desired learning evidence is recognition and use of important structure in
an unfamiliar case without our cues. Visual acceptance, correct mathematics and
passing tests each matter, but none establishes learning transfer.

Problems and flashcards need instructional design. Changing numbers or trimming
prose is insufficient. Preserve links to explanatory context and distinguish
authored intent, observed learner behavior and model hypotheses.

## Evidence that earns investment

Measure time to useful text, checked symbolic material and substantive revision;
record engineering intervention and remaining author effort. Keep authoring
efficiency separate from learner understanding and retention. A showcase should
both demonstrate quality and pressure reusable capability; report when it merely
revalidates something that already worked.

Infrastructure earns priority by removing an observed obstacle to the selected
product outcome. Prefer a small working loop and a structurally different caller
over speculative generality. Importing or enhancing existing material may offer
a lower-cost author entry point than demanding a complete rewrite.

## Stable boundaries and open tensions

Domain owners establish correctness and semantic identity. Reuse existing
authoring, runtime, renderer and attention boundaries; unsupported generation
returns an explicit typed repair gap. Preserve accessible static truth and exact
references. The product vision does not prescribe a universal semantic model or
a single layout for every domain.

Author control versus learner flexibility, immediate responsiveness versus deeper
generation, and exceptional craft versus repeatable cost remain design tensions.
Resolve them through concrete material and evidence, not automatic global rules.

Broad curriculum/platform construction, automatic learner models, scheduling,
public deployment and universal authoring languages are outside the current
commitment. Later scope must be selected explicitly.
