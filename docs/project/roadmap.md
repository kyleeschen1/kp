## Executive Direction

> **Kinetic Press turns explanations into reusable mental machinery.**

KP is a semantic medium for acquiring, manipulating, retaining, and reusing
ways of seeing. The verified semantic-to-interactive compiler is the machinery
that preserves meaning and identity; it is not the complete product thesis.
The durable center is:

```text
verified meaning
-> semantic objects, operations, identity, and lineage
-> attentional beats and renderer-neutral presentation planning
-> one deterministic sampled timeline
-> prose, notation, diagram, code, motion, or learner-action focus
-> retrieval, variation, compression, and reuse
```

The engine is no longer the project's largest unknown. KP has canonical
semantic traces, deterministic seek and rewind, native KaTeX and SVG renderers,
an interpreter-grounded program trace, static publication, Article v1,
whole-file editing, semantic navigation, review capture, accessibility, route
budgets, and a native catalogue.

Educator product discovery remains the next product-validation gate and is
being carried forward outside this Codex thread. The repository's active
working lane has returned to the internal Animation Catalogue: build durable
semantic animation capabilities that remain useful under any later product
layout. This does not validate demand or reopen learner-facing layout work.

The catalogue contains a mixture of teaching animations, fixtures, and
diagnostic proofs. The supported equation domain has completed its narrow-core
convergence: typed declarations, recipes, motif invocations, generated closed
dispatch, preservation evidence, governed authoring metadata, and a
cross-domain host boundary now replace the earlier caller-by-caller authority.
The current decision is to prove that this architecture is cheaper to author
against, attribute its remaining compiler and verification cost, and retire
only redundancy with exact reachability evidence. Matrix-to-linear-map remains
tabled; no stable promotion rank has been silently changed.

## Architecture And Authoring Laws

- Semantic identity is never inferred from glyph equality.
- The interpreter or solver supplies causal truth; a typed pedagogical score
  may reorder disclosure but may not contradict that truth.
- Authors and LLMs specify semantic intent and role bindings, not DOM, geometry,
  keyframes, renderer selection, or timing tables.
- Unsupported generated work produces a typed repair gap; it never silently
  falls back to an unrelated motif or generic fade.
- A new exemplar should primarily add semantic content and bindings. A new
  clock, scheduler, paint owner, or caller-specific renderer branch fails the
  architecture ROI gate.
- One exemplar establishes a candidate; a structurally different second caller
  justifies a shared seam; only then does broad promotion begin.
- Layout is a projection. Article syntax and animation semantics do not encode
  desktop geometry.
- Static prose, math, SVG, accessibility, and direct-link truth exist before
  optional interaction enhancement.

## Measures Of Convergence

Track outcomes rather than infrastructure volume:

- production representations and compatibility paths retired;
- renderer or runtime files changed per new exemplar;
- caller-specific presentation branches and visual exceptions;
- time from semantic fixture to human-ready animation;
- first-pass and one-repair success for LLM drafts;
- typed repair-gap rate and silent-fallback count;
- recurrence of previously closed regression classes;
- initial route closure, active-frame cost, layout shift, and long tasks;
- percentage of human feedback about pedagogy versus engine correctness; and
- whether the second and third callers are materially cheaper than the first.

The 2026-08-13 pre-expansion closeout establishes the first bounded benchmark:
existing-operation variation succeeds after one typed repair, governed
multi-operation composition succeeds first pass, and an unsupported operation
returns a typed repair gap without fallback. Treat model-reported provenance as
untrusted metadata and keep typecheck plus executable laws authoritative.
