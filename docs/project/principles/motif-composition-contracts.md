# Motif-preserving composition

Accepted 2026-09-09 after the composed-factoring clearance repair produced
oversized arcs. This direction supersedes treating zero bounding-box overlap
as the objective of motif composition. It does not authorize a catalogue-wide
implementation or replace existing operation-owned registries.

## Ownership boundary

The compositor preserves correctness and coordinates interactions. The motif
preserves the visual explanation. Neither may silently rewrite the other.

Semantic sources declare identities, grouping and mathematical transformations.
Operation-owned motif contracts declare contributor/receiver/context/artifact
roles, their dependencies, permissible contact, handoffs and adaptation limits.
Renderer measurements locate those roles; they do not infer mathematical intent.
Hosts consume the canonical binding rather than inventing local choreography.

## Shared laws

1. **Identity continuity:** persistent mathematical objects remain trackable.
   A renderer ownership exchange must not look like object replacement.
2. **Semantic grouping:** a declared unit stays coherent until an operation
   explicitly decomposes it. Do not split a fraction or parenthesized expression
   merely because the renderer observes several glyphs.
3. **Explicit paint ownership:** every visible contribution has an owner.
   Duplication requires lineage; fusion requires a declared ownership handoff.
4. **Readable checkpoints:** reading stops reproduce exact native expressions,
   without unintended occlusion, leftover material or duplicated owners.
5. **Deterministic reversal:** direct seek, reverse and interruption sample one
   plan. Do not introduce a second clock or replay-dependent state machine.
6. **Bounded motion:** adaptation stays within the motif's reviewed character.
   Occupancy inspection cannot silently enlarge arcs, add detours, replace
   easing, shrink content or fade continuants until a geometric score passes.

## Contact is not a Boolean visual verdict

Required contact includes fusion and branching origins. Permitted transit
crossings may briefly occlude paint while preserving object tracking. Unintended
interference obscures identity, implies false grouping, or spoils a reading stop.
An axis-aligned rectangle intersection is diagnostic evidence, not sufficient
proof of the third category: compound bounds include empty space.

Inspect the complete assembled scene, including specialized material. Preserve
coverage and report contacts even when the motif's transit policy is diagnostic.
Do not mislabel an inspected scene as collision-free. Keep strict lifecycle,
ownership, handoff and endpoint invariants separate from transit diagnostics.

For meaningful conflicts, coordinate context and reception within the existing
motif's limits. If that is insufficient, expose the conflict for review. Do not
invent an aesthetically different route or accumulate equation-specific waivers.
Persistent ambiguity and required readability need perceptual assessment; zero
sampled intersections is neither necessary nor sufficient for good choreography.

## Reusable dependencies, not token exceptions

Receiver space may be prepared by moving persistent context. The rule is not
"move the 3 left": use declared roles and their measured destinations.

For fusion, contributors resolve to a shared position, scale, baseline and
emphasis before paint ownership transfers. Resolve identity before releasing
attention. Arrival, material settlement and emphasis release are distinct
dependencies; their exact timing remains motif-owned and visually reviewable.

Distribution keeps branching lineage and semantic units legible. Factoring
resolves equivalent contributors to one identity. Evaluation retains its
established ink-glyph mechanism. Enclosure reception preserves the intelligible
order of contents and syntax. Do not flatten these into one generic animation.

## Enforcement and promotion

Types require complete roles, ownership, contact policy and compositor
participation through supported APIs. They do not prove geometric or aesthetic
truth. Runtime checks validate actual participants, compatible handoffs,
native endpoints, geometry revisions and declared adaptation limits. Finite
sampling is not a continuous-time proof. Human review selects the visual
language; focused regressions preserve accepted choices afterward.

Current bounded implementation: the compiler-issued factoring choreography owns
its composition policy; its native binding must inspect all factor/context paint
before publishing evidence. Transit intersections are reported, not repaired by
arc inflation. Contributors and receiver share emphasis at fusion, with release
after material settlement. This is not a universal extension contract or
catalogue-wide certification.
