# Fluent reasoning without lost explanation

Status: accepted authoring policy, 2026-09-15; not catalogue-wide visual promotion.

**Fluent presentation may suppress routine intermediate states, but must preserve
consequential lineage, assumptions and access to a faithful expansion.**

Keep three decisions separate: checked mathematical reasoning, the granularity
of the written record, and the presentation of its inspection. A compact written
step need not be an opaque whole-expression replacement. Fluent motion is not
explanatory motion played faster.

Choose the default from the teaching purpose and explicit prerequisites, not
from a guessed learner identity or subject label. The momentum/energy exemplar
assumes routine scalar cancellation and unit-exponent notation; its cancellation
may be fluent. The scalar lesson teaches why a factor remains, so it stays
explanatory. A lesson about exponent laws could instead expose the exponent
arithmetic. None of these choices changes the mathematical authority.

Offer a local “Inspect smaller steps” route, retaining the same outer endpoints,
assumptions and exact return. Do not require a global beginner/expert mode or a
special popup to justify every omitted identity. “Why is this allowed?” remains
independent of animation depth. Do not omit an identity when it is the learning
target, when its removal obscures an exception, or when no checked refinement is
available.

Cancellation must retain the surviving factor's lineage. Exponent one is
conventionally unwritten but its base remains; exponent zero removes the factor
only under the relevant assumptions. A glyph disappearance is never itself
proof of either operation. Preserve nonzero assumptions and reject unsupported
inputs rather than silently falling back to a generic transition.

LLMs author purpose, prerequisites and a supported presentation choice. They
must not invent timings, geometry, inferred mastery or unverified decompositions.
Reuse existing motifs and paint owners. Review one exemplar before broader
promotion; keep the explanatory alternative accessible even after acceptance.
