# Decision 2026-08-15: Symbol-Motion Invariant Promotion Result

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: accepted

## Decision

The quotient-fusion, distribution-fan-out, and additive-cancellation pressure
gate is complete. Promote the following operation-independent requirements for
the next canonical semantic-motion compiler:

1. exact semantic endpoints and an explicit bounded rewrite frontier;
2. total correspondence, explicit provenance, and false-identity rejection;
3. semantic material lifecycle independent from salience and paint;
4. exhaustive typed operation cohorts, role bundles, and attachment closure;
5. an acyclic semantic precedence graph lowered into presentation-local time;
6. one nominal compiled authority between semantic validation and rendering;
7. deterministic direct sampling, native endpoint settlement, and exact
   historical rewind through the same semantic record.

Promotion establishes compiler requirements, not one universal motion motif.
The next tranche may introduce only the smallest shared types and entry point
needed to route the three approved callers through this boundary.

## Retained As Local Policy

Do not promote any of the following into semantic authoring authority:

- trajectories, normalized timing windows, easing, or collision geometry;
- opacity as a proxy for semantic presence;
- a universal rigid-compound or cohesion rule;
- homomorphic fusion as the root grammar for all symbolic operations;
- structural-shell lifecycle when an operation has no shell;
- counter-orbit contact outside cancellation;
- the distribution connector-axis constraint outside distribution;
- fraction optical correction outside its reviewed typography treatment; or
- renderer-authored fallback when semantic authority is incomplete.

These choices remain capability-specific presentation recipes. A future
caller may pressure and promote one of them, but this gate does not.

## Compiler Consequence

The three approved callers currently reach sound laws through different
authority paths. The next bounded run must therefore establish one compiler
front door with a discriminated outcome:

```text
ready | repair-required | explicit-static | human-review
```

It must preserve the existing quotient, distribution, and cancellation
motifs, not merge them. It succeeds only after those callers route through the
same front door and one new animation reaches review mainly through semantic
states, role bindings, lineage, teaching intent, and salience rather than new
renderer machinery.

LLM-authored drafts may connect only after the semantic compiler boundary is
enforced. Models may propose semantic states and pedagogical intent; they may
not select paths, timings, paint behavior, or renderer fallbacks directly.

## Consequences

- The pressure run is complete after its release closeout.
- No new symbolic family starts before a separately reviewed compiler tranche.
- Existing assets are not migrated automatically.
- Paths, timing, renderer geometry, and optical treatment remain local.
- The future catalogue inventory classifies paths before incremental migration.

## Rejected Alternatives

- **Promote a single choreography:** rejected because the three operations
  require intentionally different spatial behavior.
- **Keep every law family-local:** rejected because the seven promoted
  requirements explain recurring regressions across all three callers.
- **Begin a catalogue-wide migration now:** rejected because a thin-waist
  proof with one new caller must establish lower marginal cost first.
- **Connect generated drafts directly to motifs:** rejected because that would
  recreate competing presentation authorities.
