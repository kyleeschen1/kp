# Revise The Inline Lesson Proof To An Attention Corridor

Date: 2026-08-04
Status: accepted for bounded discovery

Superseded presentation detail: the external padding `P`, anticipatory
approach, and reading shelf are replaced by the direct cue-to-stage depth
handoff in `2026-08-04-kp-depth-handoff-lesson-layout-revision.md`. This file
remains the rationale for the unelevated stage, constant prose measure, and
explicit following runways.

## Decision

Replace the economics proof's raised platform and docked paragraph with an
unelevated sticky stage and a scroll-projected attention corridor. The stage
pins in the upper viewport. Invisible padding `P` sits beneath it. Each
constant-width cue approaches the lower edge of `P`, reaches a brief
full-opacity reading shelf, fades to zero while crossing `P`, and is then
occluded by the stage's page-colored paint plane.

Each cue owns a following runway. A motion cue's semantic animation begins at
the focus line and may continue through that runway after the prose has left
the display area. Context cues use shorter runways and retain the settled
animation frame. Reverse scroll reconstructs cue opacity and animation progress
from the same geometry.

## Reason

The raised proof made the lesson feel like a card system and changed the active
paragraph's geometry. The attention corridor preserves a continuous page,
keeps prose width and wrapping invariant, delivers the instruction immediately
before it is useful, and clears the observation area while motion unfolds.
Position, timing, opacity, and occlusion carry salience without elevation.

## Preservation Boundary

Retain the existing proof's query flag and rollback boundary. Do not change:

- economics truth, animation frames, graph rendering, or stage composition;
- canonical prose, passage identity, controls, semantic URLs, TOC, or review;
- the approved split-layout default;
- shared lesson-shell contracts or another tutorial;
- the paused S-expression execution checkpoint or promotion ledger.

## Proof Criteria

- all cues retain one prose width, font size, indentation, and line wrapping;
- the stage has no shadow, lift transform, rounded card, or paragraph surface;
- cue opacity follows waiting, approach, reading-shelf, receding, and occluded
  states from one deterministic projection;
- the focus line starts the same block-local motion corridor used by manual
  controls, deep links, direct seek, and rewind;
- explicit runways separate successive cues and allow each animation to settle;
- phone text remains readable with no horizontal overflow;
- large text and reduced motion select ordinary fully opaque reading flow;
- the default economics layout and review capture retain their regressions.

## Consequences

This supersedes only the visual lift-and-dock detail of the earlier proof. It
does not authorize a general scroll-scene framework, shared cue abstraction,
second-caller rollout, or default-layout replacement. Human review of this
economics exemplar remains the next gate.
