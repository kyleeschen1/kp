# Adopt An Inline Sticky Lesson Layout Proof

Date: 2026-08-04
Status: accepted for bounded discovery

Presentation revision: the raised lift-and-dock treatment in this record is
superseded by
`2026-08-04-kp-attention-corridor-lesson-layout-revision.md`. The query-selected
exemplar, preservation boundary, readable fallback, and promotion gate remain
in force.

## Decision

Record the inline-sticky lesson standards in
`../principles/inline-sticky-lesson-layout.md` and implement one reversible
proof through the approved economics demand-shift lesson. The proof is selected
by `?layout=inline-sticky`; the approved split presentation remains the default.

The candidate grammar uses one reading axis. The graph begins inside the
lesson, lifts into a square continuous platform, and lets the relevant prose
dock beneath it. Scroll and manual controls project the same reversible motion
state. Phone fit preserves readable text by contracting the stage first and
returning to ordinary flow when a minimum useful stage no longer fits.

## Reason

The existing persistent side stage preserves visual context but requires
horizontal gaze switching and a substantially different fixed-dock phone
projection. The inline-sticky candidate makes proximity carry the attention
relationship, gives each animation a local scene, preserves continuous prose,
and can use the same layout logic on phones and wide screens.

## Preservation Boundary

Do not change:

- economics equations, parameters, equilibrium truth, or exact readouts;
- animation assets, runtime frames, graph renderer, or stage composition;
- canonical Markdown prose or passage identities;
- scrub controls, review capture, semantic URLs, or TOC destinations;
- the current default economics presentation;
- the active animation-promotion rank or the S-expression human checkpoint.

## Proof Criteria

- one graph and one copy of every paragraph exist in the DOM;
- embedded, lifted, docked, released, and reverse-scroll states are
  deterministic;
- primary phone prose remains at least approximately 17px at the default text
  scale with no horizontal overflow;
- a large-text pressure case selects reading flow rather than shrinking or
  clipping text;
- the existing semantic progress and manual-control contracts remain in use;
- the proof has a stable scoped browser-capture command;
- human review occurs before default replacement or shared-shell promotion.

## Consequences

This authorizes a visual comparison, not a rollout. The proof may reveal that
some observation paragraphs need explicit authored runways or that graph/code
stages require distinct minimum sizes. Those are findings for the exemplar and
second caller, not permission for a universal scroll-scene framework.
