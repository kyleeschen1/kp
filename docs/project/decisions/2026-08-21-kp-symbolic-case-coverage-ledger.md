# Symbolic Case Coverage Ledger

Status: accepted and implemented
Accepted: 2026-08-21
Applies to: symbolic animation capability promotion

## Decision

KP will not treat a family-level `Direct` label as evidence that every
important syntactic or semantic shape is handled. Each newly promoted symbolic
family must declare a case ledger derived from its typed vocabulary or corpus.
The generated Transformation Coverage artifact projects that ledger so gaps
cannot disappear into roadmap prose.

The ledger records six separate maturity claims for every case:

1. notation paintability;
2. semantic representability;
3. operation authority;
4. executable exemplar evidence;
5. family promotion evidence; and
6. governed generation evidence.

A case may resolve to an animated transition, a typed gap, or a required
ordered sequence. Refusing an invalid rewrite is coverage, not a missing
animation, when the refusal is typed and governed.

## First Family

Radical inversion is the first tracked family. Its ten rows are derived from
`kpRootRewriteVocabulary`, including closed evaluation, persistent carriers,
domain-qualified cancellation, exponent/index composition, mixed evaluation,
partial extraction, nested roots, a blocked rewrite, and a factoring-first
derivation. Adding or removing a root operation class now fails TypeScript
until its title and maturity evidence are classified.

The family-level `Direct` status and the case-level maturity claims remain
different truths. Root authoring can be Direct while individual visual
exemplars or generation cases remain pressure or missing; the dashboard must
show both.

## Promotion Gate

- A new Direct symbolic capability without a case ledger fails compilation.
- Existing Direct families are listed as explicit migration exemptions rather
  than being silently treated as covered.
- Non-Direct equation families show that a ledger is required before Direct
  promotion.
- The generated JSON and internal coverage view are projections only. Typed
  vocabularies, registries, evidence, and capability readiness remain the
  authorities.

This gate applies to future family promotion. It does not require a historical
catalogue migration before work can proceed, and it does not certify subjective
choreography without a reviewed exemplar.

## Maintenance Rule

When a symbolic family gains a semantic edge case, add it to the family's
typed vocabulary or corpus first. Its coverage row must then declare the
required evidence and current maturity. Do not maintain a parallel prose-only
checklist.

The smallest rollback unit is the case-coverage schema and its generated
projection. Existing semantic operations, animation assets, renderers, and
choreography are outside this decision's mutation boundary.
