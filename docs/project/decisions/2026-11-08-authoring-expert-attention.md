# Authoring expert ways of seeing

Status: accepted direction; orientation infrastructure approved
Date: 2026-11-08

## User direction

The user wants ChatGPT-level responsiveness for robust custom explanations,
authors who can develop extraordinary attention choreography, and libraries of
adaptive material connected to problems and flashcards. They endorse an
authoring-first product center and note that exceptional featured content may
help marketing. They then identify getting lost as their main blocker and
authorize the proposed orientation infrastructure to proceed.

## Decision and approval boundary

The [charter](../strategy.md) is the durable product thesis. Authoring and revision
come first, with learner adaptation, practice/retrieval and a curated library as
successive capability horizons. Featured content is a quality/discovery vehicle;
marketing advantage and learning transfer remain hypotheses.

The [orientation delivery](../2026-11-08-project-orientation-delivery.md) is approved
implementation. The next live authoring/revision proof is proposed and needs a
bounded scope informed by the independent editor lane. Neither the direction
endorsement nor “go as long as possible” approves all horizon implementation,
paid model use, publication or an unrestricted content-production campaign.

## Disposition of previous work

- The finite explanation-first progression closes at its actual scope: accepted
  content, local reading reuse, scripted exact-context return and physics pressure.
  Its physics compiler/rail already existed; revalidation was disproportionately
  expensive and did not prove rapid conversational generation.
- Physics-reader convergence was an agent recommendation, never an approved
  successor. It is deferred unless it removes a demonstrated product blocker.
- Co-creative practice and spaced retrieval remain in the destination. Their
  former first-product priority gives way to authoring exceptional reusable
  material, then learner adaptation and practice.
- Earlier exclusions of library production described the previous stage. A
  curated adaptive library is now accepted as a long-term ambition; no public
  catalogue or publishing work starts here.
- Accepted readers, domain-owned semantics, native renderers, architecture
  boundaries, finished repairs and independent editor ownership remain intact.
- Historical run gates and approvals remain evidence at their original scope.
  Archiving narrative does not resume, discard or complete those runs.

## Continuity

Keep the roadmap short and current. Preserve previous narrative in explicitly
historical files; retrieve it only for a named question. The dashboard projects
direction and source-backed outcome evidence. Theseus retains execution status.
The parallel agent may maintain tasks; one designated owner reconciles strategic
records. Brainstorming stays exploratory until selected, and each accepted pivot
records the reason and disposition of displaced commitments.
