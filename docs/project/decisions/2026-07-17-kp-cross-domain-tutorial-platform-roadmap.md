# 1. Adopt The Cross-domain Tutorial Platform Roadmap

Date: 2026-07-17  
Status: accepted

## Decision

Finish the active semantic-material-motion loop, freeze a small gold equation
baseline, and shift the next product lane to a cross-domain tutorial platform.
Build that platform through capability-opening exemplars in this order:

1. an interactive Fundamental Theorem of Calculus equation/graph tutorial;
2. a BFS or Dijkstra code/network/data-structure tutorial;
3. a supply, demand, tax, and subsidy parametric economics laboratory;
4. a work-energy equation/diagram/graph physics derivation;
5. governed LLM prompt and upload authoring across the promoted vocabulary.

Human approval attaches to novelty rather than content count. Compositions of
promoted vocabulary may pass automated gates; new combinations receive cohort
review; new primitives and gestalt styles require a canonical exemplar review.

## Reason

Incrementally approving every animation protects quality but does not scale.
KP already has equation, graph, DiagramScene, SourceFile, shared-clock, layout,
export, and LLM compiler seams. The next leverage comes from composing those
seams into explanatory, interactive modules while preserving exemplar-first
review for genuinely new visual language.

## Consequences

- Equation motion becomes a bounded conformance lane after the active loop,
  not the only active product lane.
- A minimal tutorial storyboard and interaction contract becomes platform work;
  full curriculum, learner modeling, and course generation remain deferred.
- Tutorial modules reuse animation assets and the shared runtime rather than
  creating a separate clock or renderer architecture.
- Domain logic enters through deterministic, validated ports with provenance.
- LLMs propose semantic models, claims, storyboards, and approved operations;
  they do not emit DOM, pixels, arbitrary keyframes, or self-certified claims.

## Alternatives Considered

- Perfect every equation animation before expanding: rejected because it makes
  visual review an unbounded prerequisite for product breadth.
- Build a universal scene graph and every domain abstraction first: rejected
  because it front-loads speculative complexity without vertical evidence.
- Build the economics or physics application directly: rejected because the
  tutorial, interaction, and cross-view contracts should be proven first.

## Follow-ups

- Complete promotion-matrix and closeout slices 29–30.
- Add novelty and maturity facets to the catalog/promotion model.
- Grill and accept the cross-domain tutorial/interaction contract.
- Materialize an exemplar-first FTC run contract.
- Expand the governed vocabulary one flagship domain at a time.
