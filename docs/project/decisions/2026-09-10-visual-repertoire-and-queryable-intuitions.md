# Visual repertoire and queryable intuitions

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: accepted product direction; continued brainstorm, not implementation approval

## User direction

The user accepted the capability-led visual-repertoire vision, then clarified:

> When I think of the "memetic unit" of KP, I think of something like "an
> intuition for the fundamental theorem of calculus" or "intuition for the
> process of singular valeu decomposition", at least on the longer end.

> When I am trying to learn and retain, I need micro-intuitions, easily
> queryable and composed.

The user values long-form explanations such as 3Blue1Brown for gestalt, but
does not want documentary-length presentation to be the required access path
for learning, retrieval or retention. This is a stated product preference, not
an empirical claim that short explanations universally teach better.

## Accepted repertoire direction

Build a language of reusable mathematical ways of seeing, not merely moving
equations or isolated animation demos. Combine reliable symbolic authoring with
conceptual explanation quality exemplified by surface-to-contour, Bayes and tax.

The explanatory families are preserve/reorganize, constrain/select, connect
representations, vary/compare, approach/approximate and decompose/accumulate.
These organize pedagogy and capability selection; they do not mandate six
universal software types or erase domain-owned semantics and renderers.

Accepted high-level path:

1. A richer mixed-operation algebra explanation through the shared authoring
   path, improving author-start guidance within delivery rather than as a gate.
2. A geometric flagship adjacent to the contour exemplar; the recommended
   candidate is gradient/contour reasoning and greatest increase.
3. Derivative/local-linearization reasoning, followed by accumulation.
4. Connected applications across trig, calculus, linear algebra, probability,
   statistics and MVC, selected by useful content and actual dependencies.
5. Increasingly normal human/LLM assembly from deductions, assumptions and
   instructional intent into checked explanations and coherent projections.

Exact examples, dependencies, boundaries and loop scope remain to be proposed.
Algebra need not be complete before bounded geometric/calculus expansion.
Preserve and reuse existing families rather than rebuilding them. No tabled
asset, including the matrix promotion item, is implicitly reactivated.

Each addition proceeds from a comprehension question to one canonical exemplar,
early human visual review, a structurally different second example, and a
bounded authoring/delivery path. Distinguish exemplar, reusable family and
authorable capability. Reuse established verification; do not repeat the
twenty-slice authoring-trial ceremony for routine variants. Name future loops
for visible capabilities and justify infrastructure by the explanation it enables.

## The product unit

An intuition is not necessarily one mathematical operation, motion beat, card,
fixed duration or full lesson. FTC and SVD are examples of larger intuition
units; smaller intuitions should be independently findable and composable into
them. The medium should support both local recovery and whole-argument reading.
Focus Cards remain a canonical presentation format, not the definition of an
intuition's semantic boundary. Flashcards and readings are projections rather
than separately maintained truths.

Retain the existing semantic/revision/reference foundations. This direction
does not authorize a new ontology, universal intuition schema, renderer,
search backend, scheduler, curriculum, or claims of comprehension efficacy.

## Open design questions for continued brainstorming

- What makes a micro-intuition independently useful without losing assumptions,
  referents, prerequisites or the reason it matters?
- How should a learner find a missing idea by question or confusion, not only
  by formal topic name?
- How should immediate recall, a worked explanation and prerequisite expansion
  share a source without becoming a compulsory linear video?
- Which links express prerequisite, reason, representation, application,
  contrast or composition, and which existing contracts already cover them?
- How should reconstruction/prediction test recovered intuition rather than
  recognition of a familiar animation?
