# Table Linear Algebra Frontier

Date: 2026-08-02
Status: accepted pause; replacement frontier not yet selected

## Decision

Table the matrix-to-linear-map promotion and the remaining slices of
`run-contract.kp.matrix-linear-map-rank6-v2`. Linear algebra is a distant
learner target relative to the transformation families KP should explore next.

Preserve the current matrix exemplar, its slice-17 visual checkpoint, tests,
captures, framework-neutral semantic objects, operation-bank experiment, and
open review questions. Do not promote its schematic Pour treatment, pressure
standalone dot-product or matrix-matrix callers, generalize the motif, or run
its release slices while the thread is tabled.

The stable promotion ledger is not silently reranked by this pause. Its
matrix row remains the first unresolved ledger entry until a replacement is
explicitly selected and the ranks are revised in one bounded decision. In the
interim, `next` means unresolved ledger position, not active implementation
authority.

## Candidate Direction

Prefer nearer transformations that expose reusable laws without requiring
linear algebra:

- exact repartition and equivalence;
- inverse exchange and regrouping;
- distribution and recombination;
- proportional rescaling with units;
- function translation, scaling, and reflection;
- branching and reintegration;
- refinement and accumulation;
- geometric dissection and rearrangement;
- periodic correspondence; and
- weighted branching and conditioning.

The accompanying next-step review recommends place-value subtraction as the
strongest new architectural pressure, fraction equivalence as the fastest
harvest, and function-coordinate transformations as the strongest graph-led
alternative. No candidate is authorized for implementation by this decision.

## Preservation And Revisit Boundary

- Leave the matrix asset visible in the internal catalogue and retain its
  review state.
- Keep the completed 17 slices and commit history intact.
- Treat the remaining nine slices as deferred, not failed or complete.
- Revisit only when the user deliberately returns to linear algebra or when a
  nearer approved exemplar proves that the matrix seam is the smallest useful
  second caller.
- Replace the matrix-dependent SvelteKit pressure gate explicitly rather than
  assuming another animation provides equivalent evidence.

## Consequences

- The next task is selection among non-linear-algebra transformation families,
  not slice 18 of the matrix run.
- Internal Studio and SvelteKit remain durable goals, but their prior
  matrix-specific pressure ordering must be reconsidered before execution.
- The current operation-bank treatment remains an open visual experiment; it
  does not become a shared Pour motif.
