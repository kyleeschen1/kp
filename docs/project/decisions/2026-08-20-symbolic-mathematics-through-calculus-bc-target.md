# Symbolic Mathematics Through Calculus BC Is The Near-Term Coverage Horizon

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: accepted

## Decision

Make common symbolic transformations from prerequisite algebra through AP
Calculus BC a near-term capability horizon for Kinetic Press. "Covered" does
not mean that KP is a complete computer algebra system, textbook, or proof
assistant. It means that a requested transformation can be:

1. represented as typed source and target semantic states;
2. assigned stable roles, identity, correspondence, scope, domain, and branch
   evidence;
3. validated by a registered semantic operation or rejected with an exact
   typed gap;
4. compiled through canonical motif recipes into deterministic, seekable,
   reversible presentation; and
5. discovered through natural-language or ordered-LaTeX authoring once its
   exemplar and pressure caller pass review.

Keep maturity visible per capability. Static KaTeX rendering, semantic
representability, registered operation authority, an executable exemplar,
narrow promotion, and governed generation are different claims.

## Coverage Envelope

The envelope includes the symbolic prerequisites and transformations used in
typical algebra, precalculus, and Calculus BC work:

- arithmetic, fractions, powers, roots, logarithms, and balanced equations;
- factoring, collection, substitution, identities, and function composition;
- inequalities, absolute values, piecewise expressions, and domain/branch
  restrictions;
- polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric functions;
- inverse and reciprocal functions with syntax-aware disambiguation;
- sequences, finite and infinite series, sigma/product notation, and
  convergence statements;
- limits, one-sided limits, infinity, and indeterminate-form transformations;
- differentiation, including higher derivatives, implicit forms, and the
  common algebraic rules;
- integration, including bounds, differentials, substitution, accumulation,
  improper forms, and constants of integration;
- parametric, polar, and vector-valued symbolic forms used by Calculus BC; and
- first-order separable differential-equation transformations and Taylor or
  Maclaurin series operations used by the course.

Graph interpretation, geometric intuition, and curriculum sequencing remain
separate projections over this symbolic authority. The target does not imply
that every listed operation receives unique choreography.

## Operation, Motif, And Projection

Specific transformations are executable semantic truth and coverage cases.
Motifs are reusable attentional and motion mechanisms selected by registered
recipes. State-retention projections decide whether the source is replaced,
retained in an equivalence frame, or accumulated in a derivation trail.

One operation may compose several motifs. One motif may serve several
operations. Neither a motif name nor a visual gesture is mathematical
authority. An LLM may propose an operation and instructional intent; KP owns
validation, role binding, recipe selection, geometry, timing, paint ownership,
and typed refusal.

## Syntax Hazards Are Semantic Work

Many function and trigonometric transformations can reuse existing wrapping,
distribution, transfer, cancellation, fusion, and enclosure motifs, but their
surface syntax requires explicit normalization. In particular:

- `sin^2(x)` means the square of a function value;
- textbook `sin^-1(x)` normally denotes inverse sine, not the reciprocal of
  sine;
- `1 / sin(x)` and `csc(x)` denote reciprocal relationships;
- `f^-1(x)` may denote an inverse function while `(f(x))^-1` denotes a
  reciprocal;
- prime notation, Leibniz notation, higher derivatives, differentials,
  integral bounds, summation limits, and one-sided limit markers carry roles
  that cannot be inferred from glyph adjacency alone.

Preserve original notation as representation provenance while normalizing to
unambiguous semantic forms. Ambiguity produces a clarification or typed gap,
not a guessed animation.

## Accepted Order

Retain the approved breadth order:

1. exponential/logarithmic duality;
2. root inversion with branch and domain truth;
3. the accepted persistent equivalence/derivation projection tranche;
4. TypeScript and Python generation frontends;
5. finite summation/product binders;
6. one bounded Graph3D semantic transformation;
7. distinct differentiation and integration coverage; and
8. subsequent symbolic gaps ranked from the generated Calculus BC coverage
   atlas rather than intuition alone.

The persistent-state work is orthogonal to the mathematical family order. It
is inserted after root pressure because log equivalence and multi-step root
solving provide two contrasting real callers.

## Guardrails

- Do not build a general CAS or claim arbitrary equivalence checking.
- Do not create one handcrafted motif per textbook identity.
- Do not mark a capability direct because KaTeX can render its notation.
- Do not flatten inverse functions, reciprocals, powers, roots, binders, or
  branches into token movement without semantic evidence.
- Do not place several unreviewed visual families in one promotion cohort.
- Keep the coverage atlas generated from declarations and evidence; do not
  create another manually maintained dashboard ontology.
- Preserve the accepted cross-domain order even while the Calculus BC horizon
  makes the eventual symbolic gaps explicit.
