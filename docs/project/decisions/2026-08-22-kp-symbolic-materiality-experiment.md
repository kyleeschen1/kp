# Decision 2026-08-22: Symbolic Materiality Experiment

Date: 2026-08-22
Status: accepted as an exemplar experiment; not promoted globally

## Direction

Test **symbolic materiality** as a possible Kinetic Press visual signature.
Flat, searchable semantic glyphs should feel physically alive while they act:
they may lift, settle, pass through structure, split into generated streams,
compress, expand, align, dock, or withdraw. They remain still while the learner
is inspecting a stable mathematical state.

The objective is embodied intuition rather than decorative dimensionality. A
motion earns its place only when it reveals structure, preserves identity, or
communicates causality.

## Provisional physical laws

1. **Conservation of symbolic material.** A glyph does not appear, disappear,
   split, or merge without a semantic reason.
2. **Semantic gravity.** Native notation is the resting surface. Transit may
   use active or subsurface planes, but stable endpoints settle into exact
   native typography.
3. **Causal contact.** One object's arrival or release initiates the next
   response; unrelated cohorts do not merely animate at the same timestamp.
4. **Structural rigidity.** A semantic subtree moves as one body until an
   operation explicitly opens or transforms its interior.
5. **Operation-specific topology.** Evaluation compresses, distribution
   branches, factoring extracts, cancellation annihilates, function
   application receives, and homomorphism acts through structure.
6. **Calm settlement.** Transit paint, depth, and shadow fully yield to crisp
   native endpoint ownership without a terminal correction.

These are instructional laws, not pixel APIs. Semantic plans name roles and
causal phases; renderer-local presentation owns elevation, shadow, occlusion,
and measured geometry.

## Depth vocabulary

The experiment begins with three constrained planes rather than unrestricted
3D space:

- `surface`: stable native notation;
- `active`: material currently performing or receiving an operation; and
- `subsurface`: latent influence or transit that must remain distinct from a
  persistent glyph occurrence.

Color continues to express identity or semantic family. Depth expresses
agency, containment, and causal transit. Opacity remains presence paint and
must not become the sole carrier of meaning.

## Canonical experiment

Use `animation.algebra.log-product.product-to-sum` as the only tuning surface:

```text
ln(xy) -> ln(x) + ln(y)
```

Preserve the approved law and identities:

- `x` and `y` are persistent continuants;
- the source logarithm application withdraws;
- target logarithm applications are generated successors, not copies of the
  source occurrence; and
- the source product and target sum are distinct structural relationships.

The material-depth variant is selected only through the existing development
`focus=elevated` mode. `focus=flat` remains the canonical comparator and
`focus=no-depth` must preserve the same semantic states and x/y choreography.
Test the treatment at local inline KaTeX scale with generous stage space.

## Promotion boundary

Do not create a universal material-semantics type family, published gestalt
style, cross-renderer promise, or catalogue rollout from this decision. First
perfect the binary log-product exemplar and stop for human visual review. Only
an approved exemplar plus a structurally different caller may justify shared
infrastructure.

## Performance and access

- Preserve native KaTeX at stable endpoints and use inert transit paint.
- Keep projection deterministic for direct seek, reverse, interruption, and
  URL restoration.
- Avoid per-frame layout reads and perpetual clocks.
- Prefer compositor-friendly transform and opacity changes; do not let a
  costly animated blur become the visual signature.
- Preserve reduced-motion, no-depth, high-contrast, searchable, selectable,
  and accessible representations of the same semantic state.
