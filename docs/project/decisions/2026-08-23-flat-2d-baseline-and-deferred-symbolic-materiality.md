# Flat 2D Baseline and Deferred Symbolic Materiality

Status: accepted
Accepted: 2026-08-23
Applies to: symbolic animation presentation, motif promotion, and future depth work

## Decision

Flat 2D remains the canonical production presentation for symbolic animation.
The elevated log-product material-depth treatment remains a bounded internal
experiment and is not promoted across the catalogue or equation families.

The experiment established useful contracts: semantic roles and phases remain
independent of presentation; foreground KaTeX can stay on exact native
geometry; light and dark themes require separate optical paint; direct seek,
reverse, resize, reduced motion, and native settlement can survive an optional
depth adapter. It also exposed a visual limit: contour relief amplifies the
apparent swelling and thinning of scale-heavy glyph choreography.

KP should therefore continue building canonical semantic operations and 2D
motifs without making material presentation a prerequisite. The existing
elevated profile is preserved as review evidence, not as a default or shared
visual standard.

## Future Depth Boundary

Later dimensional presentation should decorate shared motif roles such as
`persist`, `transit`, `wrap`, `split`, `fuse`, and `evaluate`. It must not be
implemented separately for every animation or derive meaning from glyph text,
DOM position, or incidental KaTeX structure.

Promotion requires:

1. stable flat 2D choreography for the underlying motifs;
2. one optional renderer-owned material profile over existing semantic roles;
3. at least three contrasting motif mechanisms, including one that changes
   cardinality and one that preserves a carrier;
4. exact native endpoints, deterministic seek and reverse, theme-specific
   optical treatment, and accessibility fallbacks; and
5. human confirmation that depth improves embodied understanding without
   introducing swelling, blur, or attentional noise.

Bespoke callers that bypass canonical motifs may require later migration. This
is another reason to keep new animations on the governed motif pipeline now.

## Scope Clarification

This decision concerns dimensional treatment of flat symbolic glyphs. It does
not reject semantic Graph3D scenes, camera/model separation, or genuinely
spatial mathematical content. Those remain distinct capability work and must
justify themselves pedagogically rather than acting as visual decoration.

## Preservation Boundary

- Preserve the accepted flat log-product choreography and shared motif timing.
- Preserve the material exemplar and its evidence behind explicit elevated
  presentation selection.
- Do not generalize its palette, relief constants, or material-role vocabulary.
- Do not revisit historical animation families merely to prepare them for 3D.
