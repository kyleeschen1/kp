# Return To The Animation Catalogue

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: accepted

## Decision

Return the repository's active working lane to the internal Animation
Catalogue while educator recruitment and product-discovery planning continue
outside this Codex thread. The purpose is to build durable semantic animation
capabilities that remain useful across future layouts and products, not to
resume open-ended layout experimentation or to claim that product demand has
been validated.

The immediate action is to reconcile the existing 40 loadable assets with the
accepted promotion ledger and symbolic-manipulation plans, then select one
missing transformation as a reversible catalogue exemplar.

## Boundaries

- Keep the educator-discovery hypothesis and protocol as current product
  evidence; it is handed off, not rejected.
- Keep learner-facing layout, curriculum, SRS, LLM tutoring, and public-site
  expansion paused.
- Do not silently resume the tabled matrix-to-linear-map sequence.
- Do not equate a loadable equation fixture or diagnostic sample with a
  polished learner animation.
- Perfect one catalogue exemplar and stop for human review before promoting a
  family or shared motif.
- Preserve framework-neutral semantic assets, one deterministic clock, direct
  seek and rewind, native settled content, and the existing catalogue host.

## Selection Gate

The existing evidence recommends place-value subtraction as the strongest
next architecture-pressure exemplar, with fraction equivalence as the fastest
harvest, function-coordinate transformations as the strongest graph-led
candidate, and geometric dissection as the strongest diagram-led candidate.
This decision does not activate any candidate; selection remains the next
human decision.
