# Give annotations their own moment in the reader

Date: 2026-10-31
Status: annotation treatment accepted; reader consolidation and attention direction accepted

## Decision and source

After reviewing the [anchored algebra candidate](../reviews/2026-10-30-anchored-algebra.md),
the user questioned its different format from the accepted fractional document
reader and the resulting sprawl. The proposed correction was to bring the same
explanation and annotations into the accepted reader, demonstrate reuse with
that second content source, and retire the standalone experimental shell once
the replacement works.

The user responded: "yes -- i love the annotation format. It's very powerful"
and said annotations can often be better than the heading text passage. They
should not compete with normal text and deserve their own moment. Eventually,
one comment should be able to point to multiple things at once.

Accept the annotation treatment at its demonstrated local scope. This does not
accept the card interface as a second product default, establish learning
efficacy, or certify a shared annotation system. Preserve the original candidate's
finite completion; consolidation is its successor rather than an expansion of
the completed experiment.

## Attention and explanatory role

An annotation may carry the explanation for a moment instead of supplementing
a heading or narrative passage. Give its text and mathematical referent a clear
shared focus. Ordinary prose must not simultaneously demand competing reading.
The surrounding argument must remain recoverable, with complete searchable,
accessible static reading.

Coordinate the whole scene through the existing semantic playhead and attention
owners. A possible sequence is prose establishing the question, an annotation
moment beside the relevant mathematics, then the surrounding argument continuing.
That sequence is an illustration, not a mandatory template. Exact arrival,
withdrawal, placement and timing still require an integrated exemplar review.
Direct seek, reversal, reload and reduced motion must preserve the same meaning.

## Future annotations with several targets

Retain the ability for one comment to address several distinct things as a
future requirement. For example, "These are the same quantity" can refer to both
occurrences together. This is one explanatory claim with several explicit
referents; duplicating the sentence or pointing between the objects is not
automatically equivalent. Preserve semantic identity and occurrence identity.

The current adapter can combine several selectors into one measured anchor;
that does not establish independently resolved targets or several leaders for
one comment. No general target schema, renderer-wide support or implementation
of this future capability is claimed or added by this decision.

## Next finite outcome and alternatives

Bring `content/authoring/algebra/what-counts.json` and its accepted commentary
into the accepted document-reader experience, preserving the existing checked
mathematics and native compositor. Preserve the fractional reader's generous
spacing, readable prose handoffs, held mathematics and absent scrubber. Repair
the demonstrated document-projection boundary instead of copying that reader's
lesson-specific implementation. Complete the replacement by retiring the
standalone candidate shell while retaining source, evidence and useful guards.

The finish line is one coherent reader hosting both content sources, with an
inspectable annotation moment that does not compete with narrative prose. A
bounded implementation contract must identify the concrete host, conversion
and retirement paths and review checkpoint; none is started by this record.
The existing [projection repair](../reviews/2026-10-29-next-step-review.md)
supplies the earlier preservation and removal goals.

Continuing the card as another unexplained product format leaves the user's
fragmentation concern unresolved. Replacing every passage with annotations
would overgeneralize the feedback. Catalogue-wide layout unification, new math,
new animation machinery, multiple-target implementation and workbench changes
remain outside this immediate outcome.
