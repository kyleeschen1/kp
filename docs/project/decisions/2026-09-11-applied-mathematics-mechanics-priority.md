This records direction and recommendations; it is not a new executable run plan.

## North star and learner

Develop sharper intuition for applied mathematics and modelling by exploring
classical mechanics through KP, with other learning materials alongside it.
The user has strong single-variable calculus, took AP mechanics about 15 years
ago and has forgotten much of it. This is an open-ended hobby, not preparation
for a test or a dated milestone. Experiencing, critiquing and improving KP as a
student is part of the value, not automatically a cost to minimise.

Mathematical understanding of general relativity and quantum field theory is a
long-term aspiration. It guides meaningful connections, not a promise of quick
coverage or a prerequisite checklist that postpones interesting physics.
Vector/matrix fluency remains to be discovered through use.

## Accepted content priorities

Classical mechanics is the first sustained learning and authoring test case.
Its governing question is: how do we turn a physical situation into a mathematical
model, and what does that model let us see? Mathematics grows out of physical
questions and remains independently explorable; completing MVC is not an entrance
requirement for mechanics.

The long-term mathematical backbone is linear algebra, multivariable calculus,
probability/statistics, differential equations and optimisation. These are
interconnected foundations, not an exhaustive taxonomy or five simultaneous
implementation programmes. Mechanics need not artificially include every subject.

Prioritise arguments that:

1. derive predictions from modelling assumptions;
2. explain connections between representations or domains;
3. reveal why a mathematical method works; and
4. establish assumptions, validity limits, counterexamples and failure cases.

Do not equate intuition with reduced rigour. Distinguish modelling assumptions,
mathematical derivations/proofs, approximations and empirical checks. A valid
derivation does not demonstrate that its model describes the world. A suggestive
animation is not itself a proof. More abstract material remains a later interest
and may enter earlier when it clarifies an actual question.

## Reuse: integrated and independently accessible mathematics

Mechanics is one route through mathematical material, not its owner. Maintain
independently navigable domain and mathematics paths linked by explicit
applications. Standalone means explicit, recoverable prerequisites, not no
prerequisites. Shared truth need not imply identical prose, pacing or motivation.

Discover the boundaries among concept, verified example, explanation and
curriculum path through real reuse; these distinctions do not authorise four new
runtime layers. Explanations may vary by context while reusing mathematical
authority, supported visual mechanisms and stable references.

Connections deserve explicit treatment: what corresponds, what is invariant,
what changes meaning, and under which assumptions? For example, conservative
force and gradient descent both involve a negative gradient, but inertial
mechanics is not generally gradient-descent motion.

An eventual linear-algebra path may revisit ideas through mechanics, data science
and graph theory. Domain operators and interpretations must remain distinct;
equal notation alone does not establish semantic correspondence.

## Recommended development approach, not an approved slice list

- Start with spatial motion and circular acceleration; use the pendulum as a
  first recurring system. Return later through forces, energy, constraints,
  differential equations, approximation and phase space. Orbits and coupled
  oscillators are subsequent candidates, not immediate build commitments.
- Maintain two lightweight maps: physical questions and mathematical concepts,
  connected by named applications and prerequisite links. Begin with existing
  documents/authoring seams, not a universal ontology or course platform.
- Prove one reuse triangle: a mechanics question, an independently understandable
  mathematical explanation, and an explicit connection. Gradient/potential/force
  is a promising bridge from current work, while kinematics is a gentler learner
  entry point; authoring order need not equal curriculum order.
- Test reuse by entering the math independently, returning to the exact physical
  question, changing context without altering shared truth, and later adding one
  genuinely different domain. Shared source updates must be coherent within the
  existing revision/publication policy, not silently mutate immutable editions.
- Capture genuine confusion, successful explanations and later reconstruction.
  Use fresh predictions and external problems without turning the hobby into an
  exam. Personal co-authoring is discovery evidence, not an independent efficacy
  trial or proof of market demand.
- Keep exposition, static diagrams and motion available. Not every question needs
  a new animation. New visual capabilities must serve a demonstrated need and
  traverse existing canonical domain, renderer and playback boundaries.
- Reconcile the bounded next delivery before launching another loop: preserve
  reusable gradient work, identify which remaining integration tasks directly
  serve this direction, and explicitly amend rather than silently abandon scope.
