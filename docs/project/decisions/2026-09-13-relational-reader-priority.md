## Accepted direction

KP is a technical reading medium in which important relationships can be
inspected, unfolded, compared and reused without losing the surrounding argument.
Excellent prose is the default carrier of motivation and reasoning. A visual
must earn its cost against a strong static alternative. A card is a projection,
not the compulsory unit of every explanation.

Retain canonical semantic objects, transformations, correspondence, governed
authoring, pedagogical scores, deterministic playback, renderer adapters,
vignettes, Article and publication boundaries. No architectural restart or
universal solver/renderer/ontology is authorized. Relationship records refer to
existing domain authority rather than copying mathematics into a new database.

Persistent means recoverable semantic context, not permanent screen occupancy
or keeping all scenes mounted. Reading position, reasoning progress, physical
time and parameter exploration have distinct meanings and should not share
unconstrained progress authority. Reverse presentation is not mathematical
invertibility. Validity conditions and information loss remain explicit.

Classical mechanics remains the sustained learner/author case. Mathematics stays
independently reusable across curricula. The from-zero curriculum is preserved;
the sharper momentum/energy repair is a development exemplar with prerequisites,
not automatically the beginner's first lesson. The five applied mathematics
domains remain the eventual backbone, not simultaneous development programmes.

## Durable outcome order

1. One complete understanding repair: reading, useful comparison, Show why,

2. Measured source-only reuse and a structurally different caller before shared

4. Productize the authoring path demonstrated by repeated encounters. LLMs draft
   arguments and select supported capabilities; checked compilation rejects
   unsupported requests. No live tutor or universal editor is a prerequisite.

These are selection gates, not four preapproved implementation runs. The active
[thread](../threads/relational-reader.md) routes subsequent loops to this decision
and the one current proposal. Theseus owns execution status after scope approval.

## Delivery and evidence policy

- Prefer one bounded complete experience over another architecture-wide cleanup.
  Admit infrastructure only for a named blocker or observed defect.
- Preserve question-first authoring and shared typography. No new font identity,
  catalogue-wide styling change, page scroll hijacking, forced quizzes or graph UI.
- Keep page scrolling native. Local inspection is explicit and reversible;
  narrow/short/enlarged-text layouts can fall back to in-flow presentation.
- Preserve the full explanatory bridge; required prerequisites cannot disappear
  into optional disclosures. Comparisons align by declared semantic coordinates.
- Measure end-to-end authoring, source-to-preview time, source-only edits versus
  engine intervention, new maintained code and initial/activated browser cost.
  Five minutes to a useful supported preview is an aspiration, not proven capacity.
- Distinguish semantic correctness, usability, learning, reuse and repeat demand.
  Use a strong static baseline and changed-case reconstruction; exposure is not
  mastery. No analytics service or learner-data collection is implicitly approved.
- Use KP delivery policy: focused discovery checks, human exemplar review,
  different-caller pressure, then promotion/release checks. No minimum slice count.
  Nonvisual repairs within approved scope retain standing automatic approval.
