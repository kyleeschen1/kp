# Algebra-grounded relationship orientation

Status: accepted; user approved implementation of the recommendation and caution.

The learner needs a map of force, momentum and energy before the narrower
turning puzzle is meaningful. Algebra is the explanatory spine, not notation
appended to verbal explanations: substitution, differentiation and accumulation
make relationships compact, precise and traversable. Prose supplies motivation
and connecting reasons. Visuals inspect consequences and correspondences.

Let a reader scan a compact argument and recover unfamiliar moves. Distinguish
definitions, physical laws, modeling assumptions and deductions; formal precision
does not erase physical scope or information loss. Keep kinetic energy distinct
from energy in general and physical time distinct from reading/derivation progress.

Implement the existing proposal's r2 amendment on the canonical Article and host.
Preserve checked physics, pinned vignettes, native renderers and input mechanics.
The authored derivation uses build-time KaTeX and native links; this does not
claim an executable symbolic verifier or vector-calculus animation capability.
No generic morph, new renderer or universal ontology is authorized as a shortcut.

The original r1 evidence remains valid for its fixtures. The old narrow opening
is superseded, not the supporting demos. The revised r2 explanation needs human
review before the later reader features; the old mechanics-motion loop stays
parked. Scope and rationale: `../threads/2026-09-13-relational-reader-delivery-proposal.md`.
