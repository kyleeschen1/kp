## Ordered outcomes

Theseus owns live slice state; this list owns rationale and finish conditions.

0. **Saved link repair.** The exact reported URL returns the version it names,
   with its checkpoint and native ink restored after load/reload. Unknown,
   foreign or conflicting versions remain explicit errors. Add unit and browser
   regressions at the reading-resolution boundary; no subjective review needed
   for faithful restoration of accepted behavior.
1. **Real revision.** Address one substantive explanatory question using current
   supported semantics and authoring inputs. Preserve the accepted original,
   show the revised candidate through the actual reader, and record request,
   source changes, checks, preview and remaining intervention. Editorial judgment
   gates adopting a new default; a scripted exchange is not live generation.
2. **Responsive iteration.** Measure model/editor work separately from compile,
   preview and verification. Remove the largest demonstrated local friction;
   check a second revision and last-valid recovery. Do not invent a speedup from
   a warm preview measurement or build a parallel preview framework.
3. **Precise attention authoring.** Revise supported targets, annotation content
   and attention ownership through source. If a several-target note is needed,
   make one reversible exemplar and stop for visual judgment before promotion.
4. **Featured-quality passage.** Develop one consequential explanation with
   reasoning, contrast and a transfer question; review its quality.
5. **Cheaper second explanation.** Use meaningfully different supported material;
   repair only coupling revealed by that caller. Measure marginal engineering.
6. **Domain transfer.** Pressure another supported representation/domain with
   correctness retained by its owner. Review any new presentation before reuse.
7. **Situated learner question.** Answer an actual question at a precise referent,
   preserving the original argument and return context.
8. **Bounded adaptation.** Change a useful learner-facing dimension while
   preserving authored commitments and assumptions; inspect the result.
9. **Learner interpretation.** Exercise one meaningful prediction, grouping or
   selection and a less-guided new case. Human response evidence is required
   for learning claims; do not infer it from automation.
10. **Problem family.** Vary an instructional distinction with checked answers,
    purposeful hints and connected feedback.
11. **Contextual retrieval.** Recognition/reconstruction cards return to the
    source explanation; reuse existing delayed-return mechanisms.
12. **Small collection.** Connect and maintain a bounded set of explanations,
    adaptations, problems and cards. Review coherence; publication is separate.

At loops 3, 6 and 9 reconsider the remaining sequence from evidence. Later scopes
remain coarse until their dependencies are demonstrated. Do not repeat accepted
visual gates, add tasks to fill time, or claim an unexecuted loop complete.
