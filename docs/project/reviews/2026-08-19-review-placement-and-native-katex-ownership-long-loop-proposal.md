## Objective

Make Catalogue review capture reliably visible, then repair the
`1/3 + 1/6` pressure caller's composite-to-leaf jerk through one shared,
immutable Native KaTeX endpoint and hierarchical paint-ownership contract.
Stop for human review of the repaired caller before implementing evaluation-
family exemplars or promoting any fraction capability.

## Acceptance Criteria

- On a wide Catalogue viewport, Review opens in a fully visible inspector-side
  drawer; Save remains reachable and the composer covers neither stage,
  scrubber, nor development dock.
- On a narrow viewport, Review opens as a bounded captured-moment sheet that
  preserves the visible reader region and a reachable Save action.
- Placement changes do not reset a draft or alter exact captured artifact,
  settings, route, or playhead state.
- Every shared Native KaTeX endpoint is rendered and measured once per font
  and viewport revision, then consumed through immutable handles.
- One endpoint observation retains both compound and semantic-leaf ownership;
  adjacent phases select views without clearing DOM annotations, rebinding
  nodes, or remeasuring the same settled state.
- The introduction-to-equivalence seam has an equivalent-pose certificate and
  fails closed when semantic-leaf inventories, geometry, baseline, font
  revision, or viewport revision disagree.
- The `2/2` unit factor shows no positional, scale, or internal-leaf jerk at
  ordinary playback, direct seek, rewind, or interruption.
- Mathematical endpoints, semantic IDs, operation order, the untouched
  `1/6`, existing approved fraction-equivalence choreography, and the shared
  deterministic clock do not change.

## Preservation Boundary

- Preserve verified fraction semantics and authoring contracts.
- Preserve the approved fraction-equivalence exemplar and all unrelated
  equation, graph, code, and 3D assets.
- Preserve Native DOM as settled typography and accessibility authority.
- Do not add a global store, second clock, universal renderer ownership graph,
  caller-local timing mask, or glyph-text identity inference.
- Do not touch the user's unrelated economics and documentation worktree
  changes.

## Rollback Units

1. Review placement and responsive shell changes.
2. Immutable Native KaTeX endpoint-handle primitives.
3. Hierarchical compound/leaf ownership views and seam certificates.
4. Common-denominator pressure integration and focused evidence.

Each unit must remain independently revertible. A failed visual treatment may
roll back the pressure integration without rolling back semantic endpoints or
the shared ownership primitives.

## Promotion Criterion

This run does not promote an evaluation family or fraction capability. Human
approval of the repaired pressure caller permits one structurally different
compound caller to pressure the ownership seam. Only that second caller can
justify shared-family promotion. Evaluation-family recipes retain their own
later exemplar checkpoint.

## Verification Cadence

- Inner loop: `npm run verify:impact -- --path <changed-path>`, then only its
  recommended focused checks.
- Review boundary: focused Review unit/browser checks and one Catalogue visual
  capture.
- Ownership boundary: rendered-scene, paint-continuity, compositor,
  fraction-equivalence, and common-denominator focused suites.
- Final checkpoint: one-worker browser evidence for the canonical caller,
  impact-selected type/build checks, and Theseus validation.
- Broad cross-browser or catalogue-wide certification is deferred until human
  approval and a structurally different second caller.

## Explicit Deferrals

- Evaluation-family recipe types and visual comparisons.
- Selecting or promoting a conservative evaluation default.
- A second compound ownership caller.
- Fraction-equivalence family promotion.
- Like-denominator addition or final arithmetic choreography.
- Public layouts, curriculum, SRS, tutoring, and unrelated asset work.
- Any rewrite of user-owned dirty economics or documentation files.

## Contract Stop Conditions

- The Review change requires restructuring the Catalogue layout.
- Shared endpoint handles alter settled KaTeX output or accessibility markup.
- The repair changes mathematical endpoints, semantic identity, or approved
  fraction-equivalence choreography.
- A seam can pass only through caller-local timing, opacity, offsets, or DOM
  rebinding.
- Direct seek, reverse, font invalidation, or resize requires replay.
- Work expands into evaluation presentation or family promotion before the
  human checkpoint.
- Unrelated user work would need to be overwritten or reformatted.
