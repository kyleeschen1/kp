Status: `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

checkpoint. It records evidence and review questions; it does not approve the
wording, visual language, catalogue disposition, or a reusable generated-session

## Objective evidence

The stable command builds the production application, starts an isolated preview,
captures all seven states, and fails closed on the following facts:

- the catalogue paints the selected artifact natively with no iframe;
- catalogue mathematics is inline KaTeX with no display wrappers;
- no visible `h1` or `h2` crowds the catalogue shell;
- the optional explanation has four `h3` sections and eight cues;
- the static explanation machinery remains closed by default;
- the lower-left development review dock retains exactly one shell slot;
- the narrow reader has no horizontal document overflow and retains six beats;
- reduced motion preserves direct progress `800` in essential-motion mode; and
- the no-JavaScript document retains six beats, MathML, and the verified solution.

The generated route itself remains within its HTML budget. The separate shared
equation-reader closure is still 855 gzip bytes over its existing allowance and
is assigned to slices `s21`–`s25`; this packet does not widen that baseline.

## Visual observations, not decisions

- The catalogue keeps the equation centered and the controls visible without
  scrolling, but leaves intentionally generous negative space around a small
  equation. Whether that balance feels focused or under-scaled is a human choice.
- The subtraction-in-flight frame preserves source and destination identity. Its
  pale structural ghosts are deliberately quiet and need a legibility judgment.
- The explanation spine fits in the inspector without nested navigation and uses
  compact inline math. The density and exact learner wording remain unapproved.
- The narrow reader fits cleanly and keeps the stage above the active prose. Its
  stage occupies a substantial fraction of the first viewport, which is a
  deliberate review point rather than an accepted responsive default.
- The static document is complete and searchable, but its long vertical rhythm is
  optimized for one state per card and still needs a publication/print judgment.

## Consolidated checkpoint questions

1. Is the catalogue equation large enough relative to the open stage, or should
   the exemplar use a larger bounded math scale?
2. Are the moving subtraction tokens and pale structural anchors legible enough
   at the operation midpoint?
3. Is the four-section explanation spine concise and ordered correctly for this
   learner state?
4. Does the narrow reader allocate the right amount of space to the sticky visual
   versus the active prose?
5. Is the static one-state-per-card rhythm acceptable, or should static output be
   denser before public-facing publication work begins?

No answer is inferred here. Generated-session visual and editorial work stops at
this package until the consolidated human checkpoint.
