## What The Exemplar Is Trying To Prove

The product claim is narrower and more useful than “a proof with animation”:

1. complete prose and mathematics remain searchable and useful without
   JavaScript;
2. a learner can recover the meaning of `lambda`, `r`, and `B` without holding
   the entire proof in working memory;
3. motion makes the row/column product correspondence and the unmatched
   `||r||^2` contribution easier to reconstruct;
4. a prompt returns to exact proof context instead of becoming a disconnected
   flashcard; and
5. settled, static, reduced-motion, and direct-link states all preserve the
   same proof truth.

## Seven Checkpoint Questions

1. **Mathematical recovery:** Can you reconstruct the proof from the Article
   without motion, and is every prerequisite available near the point where it
   is needed?
2. **Cycle A correspondence:** Does the row/column inspection make the two
   top-left product entries read as squared row and column norms, rather than
   as arbitrary symbol movement?
3. **Prediction quality:** Before the reveal, does the comparison make
   `||r||^2 = 0` feel earned for the correct reason—one unmatched,
   nonnegative contribution?
4. **Cycle B continuity:** Does `r` retain its identity through comparison,
   zero handoff, block diagonalization, and recursive focus on `B`, without a
   premature disappearance or confusing replacement?
5. **Pacing and control:** Is one continuous scrubber calm and legible at both
   active intervals, including reverse and interruption, or does either cycle
   still crowd or outrun comprehension?
6. **Return value:** Do the prompt reveal, semantic link, static comparison,
   390-pixel view, and day-zero/day-one/day-seven rehearsal make this more
   useful for durable recall than a video or isolated diagram?
7. **Disposition:** Is the result strong enough for local hardening in s26, or
   should it be revised at the exemplar, retained as static-only evidence, or
   stopped before any further investment?
