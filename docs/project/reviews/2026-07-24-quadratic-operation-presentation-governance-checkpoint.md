## What changed after the rejected checkpoint

- Completing the square now exposes ten ordered transitions: balance the
  equation, add to both sides, form a common denominator, evaluate, expose and
  factor the perfect-square pattern, take roots, evaluate the root, isolate,
  and normalize the candidates.
- The quadratic formula now exposes eight ordered transitions: substitute
  coefficients, evaluate the power and product, subtract, prepare the
  denominator, simplify the radical, evaluate the numerators, and divide.
- Each visible transformation carries an operation-presentation certificate
  covering the exact authority operations, material roles, causal phases, and
  registered motifs. Generic whole-state checkpoint motion is rejected.
- Default playback compresses compound work without omitting operations.
  “Show steps” opens the same atomic trace on a nested clock, pauses the parent,
  and restores the exact parent frame when closed.
- The signed candidates now travel directly into their exact graph roots. The
  former visible solution-set reunion has been removed.
- Reader-route conformance now requires either the shared certified runtime or
  a certified custom renderer with operation certificates, browser phase
  gates, and no generic fallback.
