## Recommendation

Keep the next bounded algebra loop and the contour-adjacent geometric successor.
Sharpen the algebra acceptance criteria around useful reasoning and independently
recoverable intuitions. After geometry, propose a small cross-domain reuse trial
before sustained derivative/accumulation expansion. Reuse existing economics or
Bayes capability rather than starting chemistry, another renderer, or a universal
transformation model. Assess the size from actual integration gaps; do not
manufacture a 20-slice maintenance run for a small trial.

The synthesis clarifies product evidence, not a missing architecture tier:
transformations and correspondences are engine concerns; reusable intuitions are
product units. Domain-specific semantic authority, instructional intent and
presentation remain separate. A camera move, algebraic rewrite, counterfactual
comparison and physical evolution are not interchangeable semantic operations.

## Proposed refinements within the 24-slice structure

- Slices 1 and 12: judge whether the reader can identify what changed, what was
  preserved, and why the move is valid/useful. The five-state chain is a useful
  engineering specimen but not inherently a compelling conceptual explanation.
  Its current "why collect before expanding" question needs a concise contrast
  with direct expansion, or a narrower question about preserving repeated-group
  structure. Do not add a second animated branch or new operation family merely
  to supply that contrast.
- Slices 11 and 12: independently entered sub-explanations must establish their
  question, referents and necessary context, not merely start at a timestamp.
- Slices 13, 16 and 20: make source-only adaptation and coherent output visible;
  record observed editing/repair effort without claiming general author economics
  or repeating the completed unfamiliar-author trial.
- Slice 18: use prediction/reconstruction to probe preserved structure or the
  reason for a move, not only recognition of the final expression. This is a
  product acceptance exercise, not evidence of superior learning or retention.

These are proposed acceptance/pedagogical refinements, not additions of a generic
proof inspector, search backend, ontology, scheduler or branching runtime. Preserve
canonical motifs, source-v1 compatibility, typed repair gaps, one early visual
checkpoint and existing release gates. They are not applied by this review.

## Proposed successor horizon

1. Mixed algebra: author a connected explanation and recover useful parts.
2. Contour/gradient: explain a correspondence or relationship across views, not
   only a symbolic sequence; this prevents overfitting to algebra rewrites.
3. Small cross-domain reuse trial: bring the existing approved explanation
   capabilities into one recognizable author/reader workflow. Demonstrate direct
   question entry, context-preserving extraction/return, supported adaptation and
   coherent outputs using existing routes/projections where available. Domain
   semantics remain local. No new scientific capability is assumed.
4. Derivative/local linearization, then accumulation: resume repertoire growth
   with the authoring/reuse requirements already tested.

The third item is a proposed insertion, not currently approved scope. Any real
learner recruitment, external coordination or new services would require separate
authority. The existing algebra loop remains paused; prior completed loops remain
closed. No Theseus execution state or roadmap priority is changed here.
