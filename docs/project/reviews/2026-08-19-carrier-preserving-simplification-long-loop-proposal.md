## Why this loop is current

Catalogue Review relocation and the sequence-level Native KaTeX ownership
repair are complete. Contributor fusion is also promoted across product,
quotient, and sum. Continuing fraction arithmetic now would add depth inside
one syntax family without testing whether the evaluation architecture can
express a genuinely different identity relation.

Carrier-preserving simplification is the highest-leverage contrast. In
`2 × 3 → 6`, no source glyph is the result; contributor fusion is honest. In
`2 × 1 → 2`, one source `2` is semantically the result; fusing every glyph
would be false. The existing generated `x + 0 = 4 → x = 4` artifact then
pressures the same family with a symbolic carrier and stationary context. This
tests breadth while reusing the completed clock, endpoint, ownership,
settlement, hosting, URL, and Review infrastructure.

## Canonical references

- **Approved contrast:** `2 × 3 → 6` contributor fusion.
- **Canonical exemplar:** `2 × 1 → 2` carrier-preserving simplification.
- **Second caller:** `animation.generated.add-zero`, rendering
  `x + 0 = 4 → x = 4`.

The exemplar establishes aesthetics. The second caller establishes the shared
boundary. Neither caller may author geometry, timing, opacity schedules,
renderer nodes, or a new clock.

## Observable acceptance criteria

- The surviving `2` or `x` is selected from verified semantic lineage, never
  from glyph equality.
- The carrier stays continuously meaningful and settles into exact native
  target ink without disappearing, duplicating, snapping, or participating in
  contributor fusion.
- The identity operand and its operator yield as one bounded syntax cohort;
  their withdrawal must not imply that they become the result.
- Every semantic leaf has exactly one dominant paint owner; no blank interval
  and no interval containing two complete competing expressions is allowed.
- Unrelated context (`= 4`) remains stationary and unchanged in the pressure
  caller.
- Ordinary playback, scrub, direct seek, rewind, interruption, remount, font
  settlement, resize, and theme changes sample the same deterministic state.
- Contributor-fusion product, quotient, and sum choreography remains byte-for-
  byte behaviorally unchanged at its registered profile boundary.
- Unsupported or ambiguous carrier evidence produces a typed gap rather than
  a generic fade or inferred identity.
- Human authors and LLMs discover the same capability from one generated
  catalogue with friendly names, aliases, examples, counterexamples, required
  evidence, support state, and exact schema—not by memorizing internal IDs.
- Ordered source states first produce a deterministic eligible-operation
  shortlist. An LLM may interpret informal intent or choose among that bounded
  set; verified binders retain mathematical authority and return typed
  clarification or repair when selection cannot be closed.

## Preservation boundary

- Preserve all verified fraction, balanced-operation, logarithm, graph, code,
  and 3D semantics and animations.
- Preserve the accepted generated add-zero source, semantic IDs,
  correspondence records, static endpoint truth, and direct URL.
- Preserve Native DOM as settled typography and accessibility authority.
- Reuse one sampled clock, existing endpoint handles, equivalent-pose seams,
  presentation registry, Catalogue host, and Review lifecycle.
- Do not reopen learner-facing layout, curriculum, SRS, tutoring, Public Web,
  or cross-domain generation.
- Do not turn carrier preservation into a default for ordinary evaluation.

## Rollback units

1. Typed family, handoff, and carrier-evidence contracts.
2. Renderer-neutral carrier-preserving recipe.
3. Native KaTeX optical realization and `2 × 1 → 2` exemplar.
4. Existing add-zero pressure integration.
5. Promotion, governed authoring, and mixed-family benchmark.

Each unit remains independently revertible. Rejection of the visual treatment
must not roll back semantic evidence or the already-approved contributor-
fusion family.

## Verification cadence

- **Discovery through slice 15:** cheap semantic/type tests, one exemplar,
  one-worker Chromium, and the smallest preservation checks. Do not build a
  cross-browser certification matrix for an unapproved aesthetic.
- **After exemplar approval:** lock motif-specific regressions, then pressure
  the existing add-zero caller.
- **After second-caller approval:** run cross-browser, promotion, generation,
  bundle, and release gates.
- Use `npm run verify:impact -- --path <changed-path>` to select inner-loop
  checks. Stable recurring browser work must be exposed through the scoped npm
  command added in slice 13.

## Explicit deferrals

- Like-denominator addition and final fraction arithmetic choreography.
- Punctuated substitution, result reception, operator aperture, and expanded
  calculation.
- Matrices, code, Graph2D, and Graph3D generation expansion.
- Learner layouts, curricula, SRS, tutoring, Public Web, and public authoring.
- Repair of unrelated user-owned economics or documentation changes.
- Any universal renderer ownership graph or repository-wide equation rewrite.

## Contract stop conditions

- The new family changes approved contributor-fusion behavior.
- Carrier identity must be inferred from glyph text or native geometry.
- A caller needs timing, coordinates, opacity schedules, DOM nodes, or another
  clock.
- A visual checkpoint is reached without explicit human approval.
- Existing add-zero truth, IDs, static output, or direct URL would change.
- A test failure cannot be classified as introduced, pre-existing, or
  unrelated.
- Work expands into another family, fraction arithmetic, layout, or domain.
- Unrelated dirty work would be overwritten or reformatted.

## Estimated envelope

Twenty-seven slices, approximately 8–12 hours of implementation and focused
verification across the complete contract. The first autonomous tranche ends
at slice 15 after approximately 3–5 hours with a mandatory visual checkpoint.
The second caller ends at slice 21 with another mandatory checkpoint. Slices
22–27 run only after both exemplars are explicitly approved.
