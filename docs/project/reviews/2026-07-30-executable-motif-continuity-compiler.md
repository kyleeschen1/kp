## Decision

Continuity now compiles beneath the sealed executable motif program. It may
constrain paint during a program-owned phase, but it cannot accept, add,
remove, reorder, or rename phases.

The closed compatibility matrix is:

| Executable program | Compatible continuity topology |
| --- | --- |
| `operation-evaluation` | `bounded-semantic-contact-co-presence` |
| `identity-fission` | `identity-preserving-branch-co-presence` |
| `identity-fusion` | `identity-preserving-gather-co-presence` |

The operation-evaluation entry is the topology selected from measured paint in
slice 12. The fission and fusion entries encode their already sealed identity
lineage; their production promotion and browser evidence remain later work in
slices 20 and 21.

## Authority Boundary

The compiler accepts only:

- one minted executable motif program;
- the perceptual contract minted for that exact program and version; and
- one closed topology discriminant.

It derives forward phase bindings directly from the program and derives rewind
as the exact reversed binding sequence. Callers cannot supply phases, semantic
roles, timing, opacity, paths, geometry, selectors, DOM, or endpoint handoff.
The result retains the exact minted program and contract objects, and the
compiler adds its own non-serializable authority.

Each program phase receives a continuity obligation:

- orient preserves native source paint and unclaimed context;
- evaluation convergence maintains required-role ink;
- evaluation recognition co-presents contributors and result at certified
  contact;
- identity branch/establishment co-presents source and descendants;
- identity gather/establishment co-presents contributors and ancestor; and
- settle reaches exact native target paint while preserving context.

These obligations describe what paint must preserve. They do not prescribe
renderer topology, coordinates, motion paths, or timing.

## Zero-Area Boundary

`shared-zero-area-junction` is an explicit rejected compiler input. Every
currently sealed motif program declares intentional vanish forbidden, and
every current perceptual contract requires continuous visible ink. Therefore
the compiler cannot emit a zero-area continuity program or reintroduce the
blank pulse as fallback.

The historical paint-continuity route still exists in production until the
operation-evaluation exemplar is replaced in slice 15. It remains diagnostic
legacy evidence and cannot pass through the new compiler.
