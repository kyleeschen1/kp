# Animation Catalogue Bounded Curation Record

Date: 2026-08-01
Status: complete

## Outcome

The catalogue shell and solve-x presentation direction passed human review.
Ordinary asset selection now stays inside one persistent shell, and compatible
equation surfaces share the approved centering rule without inheriting
solve-x-specific dimensions or compact-step styling.

This curation pass does not add visible filters, an ontology, disposition
controls, or another navigation layer. All 33 concrete loadable assets remain
catalogue members, and every human disposition remains `Unreviewed`. Approval
of the catalogue UX is not approval of each asset's content or promotion
status.

## Direction After Curation

Catalogue work returns to maintenance and later batched human review. The next
active exemplar is the stable rank-3 Supply and demand equilibrium shift,
followed by constant-force work-energy as a structurally different
synchronized-model caller. Programming trace work, public presentation,
curriculum, and broad LLM editorial work remain outside the active contract.

API consolidation and motif standardization wait until both domain callers are
human-approved. At that point KP may extract only invariants both callers
demonstrate and prune only paths whose final caller has migrated.

## Preservation Boundary

- Preserve semantic models, transformation identities, deterministic clocks,
  renderer and paint ownership, direct seek/rewind, accessibility, review
  history, and authoring contracts.
- Preserve one flat concrete-asset catalogue with progressive disclosure.
- Keep human dispositions distinct from derived host health and editorial
  roles.
- Make any future retirement an independently reversible last-caller change.
