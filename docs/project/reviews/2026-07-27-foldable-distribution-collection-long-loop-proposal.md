# Foldable Distribution And Collection Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-07-27
Status: approved
Approved by: user

## Objective

Ship one canonical learner animation for:

```text
3(x + 2) + 2(x - 1)
-> 3x + 6 + 2x - 2
-> (3x + 2x) + (6 - 2)
-> 5x + 4
```

The animation must project expanded, collapsed, automatic, and pinned views
from one immutable transformation/evaluation tree. Folding may change visible
detail and timing, but never the trace, result, dependencies, checkpoints,
accessible explanation, or export truth.

## Canonical Presentation Requirement

The equation must communicate each operation through proper semantic visual
motifs. Whole-equation fade replacement and generic source-out/target-in
crossfades are not acceptable for the canonical exemplar.

- distribution visibly fans each factor into its descendant products;
- product evaluation preserves contributing-entity lineage;
- signed terms visibly gather into stable like-term groups;
- coefficient and constant collection visibly fuse exact contributors into
  their results;
- structural fission and fusion do not fade;
- persistent context reflows or stages before focal structure changes;
- exact native KaTeX owns every settled checkpoint.

A fade-only fallback, simultaneous legacy and canonical paint, unexplained line
jump, font swap, baseline jerk, overlap, or clipping fails the visual
checkpoint.

## Canonical References

- the distribution-area reader for fan-out clarity and geometric pedagogy;
- fraction split/merge for structural fission/fusion without fading;
- radical succession for paint-space typography and endpoint continuity;
- solve-x for native settlement, responsive behavior, and exact rewind;
- the Animation Library for one lazy host and persistent review capture.

## Preservation Boundary

Preserve the immutable semantic trace, existing solve-x/distribution/fraction/
radical behavior, one runtime clock, one canonical renderer-session path,
native KaTeX settlement and accessibility, annotations, hover, Cloze, static
steps, exports, and explicit compatibility status for unmigrated animations.

The loop may add the exact semantic fixture, transformation tree, fold
projection, deterministic timing/layout projection, one reader integration,
one library entry, governed-intent proof, focused reusable helpers, and stable
verification commands.

## Disallowed Work

- no second tree runtime, animation runtime, clock, or compositor;
- no global equation-family migration;
- no operation-specific routing geometry;
- no new lifecycle or scheduler category;
- no simultaneous legacy and canonical paint;
- no LLM-authored mathematics, DOM, pixels, arbitrary timing, or keyframes;
- no unrelated arithmetic, matrix, graph, publication, atlas, or UI work;
- no compositor-core change without stopping and presenting the failed generic
  invariant for separate approval.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every visible entity has explicit semantic lineage.
2. The two distributions may be parallel or staged without changing truth.
3. Collection preserves signed-term identity and complete many-to-one lineage.
4. All four fold modes share the exact result, dependencies, and checkpoints.
5. Direct seek and rewind are exact for every fold mode.
6. Collapsed work is disclosed in visual, narrated, static, and accessible
   projections.
7. Wide and phone layouts have no overlap, clipping, font swap, endpoint jerk,
   or unexplained line change.
8. Phone presentation uses deterministic staging or folding rather than
   operation-specific avoidance.
9. The selected library host owns at most one structural WebGL lease and the
   fixed Review launcher remains accessible.
10. No compositor, clock, lifecycle, scheduler, or runtime category is added.

## Stop Conditions

accessible truth; existing compositor relations cannot express the motifs;
compositor-core or a new lifecycle/scheduler category appears necessary;
layout requires operation-specific routing; canonical paint falls back to a
whole-equation fade; native settlement loses typography continuity; a
preservation gate regresses; scope expands; or the human checkpoint is not
approved.

Approval authorizes contract creation and execution through slice 23. It does
not waive the required human checkpoint. Slices 24-26 resume only after
explicit visual approval.
