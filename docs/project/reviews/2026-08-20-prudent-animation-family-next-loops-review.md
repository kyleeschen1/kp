## Context

Equation convergence has produced a canonical compiler, approved homomorphic
decomposition, balanced operations, ink-knot contributor fusion,
carrier-preserving identity simplification, Native KaTeX mechanism
conformance, and bounded mixed-family generation. Continuing to polish every
older equation caller now has lower marginal value than proving that KP can
add new families without recreating infrastructure.

The desired pace is neither catalogue-wide expansion nor one more indefinite
consolidation phase. Alternate one visual exemplar with one pressure/promotion
loop, then change families.

## Recommended First Six Loops

### Loop 1 — Exponential homomorphism exemplar

Build `b^(x + y) → b^x b^y` through the shared homomorphism law and a new
Native KaTeX power-application realization. Preserve `x` and `y`; treat target
bases and wrappers as derived syntax; replace the additive connector with
multiplicative structure. Stop for visual review.

### Loop 2 — Exponential quotient pressure and narrow promotion

After approval, pressure with `b^(x - y) → b^x / b^y`. Extract the shared
homomorphism duality and power-application roles; keep quotient construction
and Native KaTeX optics local where the two callers differ. Do not claim that
power/scalar transport or `exp(log(x))` cancellation is thereby solved.

### Loop 3 — Root branching exemplar

Build the real-domain `x² = 9 → x = ±3` transition with explicit branch
identity, parity and domain evidence, native radical/power roles, and exact
endpoints. Stop for visual review before generalizing.

### Loop 4 — Root inversion pressure and narrow promotion

Pressure the candidate with an odd-power or inverse-direction caller. Extract
only the branch/domain semantics and presentation boundary both callers prove,
then register governed natural-language and ordered-LaTeX discovery.

### Loop 5 — TypeScript refactor frontend closure

Use the approved free-shipping refactor as the visual reference. Add a
TypeScript-owned frontend for AST identity, bindings, source ranges, refactor
legality, and exact correspondence. Preserve the approved choreography and
stop if frontend truth requires code-renderer or caller-specific authority.

### Loop 6 — Python pressure and code-family promotion

Pressure the same causal recipe through Python-owned AST and binding truth.
Promote only the request, semantic-lineage, and sequencing boundary shared by
both languages. Parsing, syntax, indentation, highlighting, and renderer
optics remain language-owned.

## Cadence After These Six

Loops 7–8 establish finite summation and pressure the binder seam with finite
product. Integral notation follows as its own semantic family. Loops 9–10 then
build and pressure one bounded Graph3D rotation or projection. Re-rank matrix,
geometric dissection, Graph2D function transformation, and the
difference-quotient/tangent bridge afterward.
