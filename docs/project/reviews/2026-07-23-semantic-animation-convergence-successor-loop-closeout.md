## What Was Simplified

- Compiler-stage ownership is executable and monotonic instead of implicit.
- Semantic and renderer-neutral animation modules have zero frozen rendering
  import exceptions.
- Promoted equation assets author one typed versioned presentation profile;
  legacy metadata reads are centralized behind a deterministic decoder.
- Runtime frames share one normalized envelope and one clock, with closed
  equation, graph/diagram, and program-trace payload families.
- Semantic-animation, presentation, and product-manifest fields have exact,
  non-overlapping projection ownership and shared immutable identity.
- Catalog and flashcard consumers normalize aggregates at their boundary
  instead of carrying dashboard/export/product state into the runtime kernel.
