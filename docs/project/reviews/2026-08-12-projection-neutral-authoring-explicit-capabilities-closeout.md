## Outcome

The 30-slice convergence tranche is complete. It restored an honest broad
baseline, moved economics whole-file Article editing above layout ownership,
separated the two retained scroll projections, and replaced algebra's
import-time choreography registration with explicit immutable capabilities.

intact. The presenter-local passage buffer, modal lifecycle, 304-line Svelte
editor, and 190-line dedicated stylesheet are deleted.

independently. Shared geometry, responsive tokens, and the cue hook have
projection-neutral names. Fresh reachability evidence says to retain both;
the tranche deliberately does not pick a public default or change appearance.

The lazy algebra pack now returns its nine serializable animation assets beside
four frozen runtime capabilities: fission/fusion, distribution, factoring, and
canonical reverse choreography. Playback carries those capabilities explicitly
through loader, session, equation frame, and renderer boundaries. Eight
registration/runtime modules and their mutable slots are deleted. Four
clean-process import permutations, repeated pack creation, and concurrent lazy
loads produce the same catalog, capability keys, and eight reverse plans.
