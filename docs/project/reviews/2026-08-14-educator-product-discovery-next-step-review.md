# Educator Product Discovery Next-Step Review

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: accepted direction; cohort and pilot content pending grill-me review

## Problem

KP has accumulated substantial semantic, animation, publication, authoring,
and layout evidence, but product demand remains unvalidated. Continued layout
iteration cannot answer whether tutors, teachers, or learners experience a
valuable job that Khan Academy, YouTube, ordinary diagrams, and worksheets do
not already satisfy.

The refined eigenvector attentional surface is materially better, but its
human checkpoint is mixed: it is useful as a research artifact while the
layout remains short of a professional default. That result should not trigger
another open-ended presentation loop.

## Accepted Direction

Pause feature, layout, infrastructure, curriculum, and generalized authoring
development. Run a minimal-build educator product-discovery phase instead.

Test this falsifiable product hypothesis:

> A tutor or teacher will reuse or send a KP artifact because it preserves and
> reactivates conceptual machinery that their ordinary explanation, video
> link, static diagram, or worksheet does not.

The candidate product center is not “animated lessons.” It is an inspectable,
manipulable, and retrievable conceptual object shared by educator and learner.

## Discovery Boundary

Use existing artifacts wherever possible. Permit only corrections required to
let an educator conduct a real session or share a stable link. Do not add a
generic pilot platform, analytics system, account model, scheduler, curriculum,
layout framework, or new animation family.

The strongest evidence is behavior rather than stated enthusiasm:

- an educator names a recurring conceptual obstacle;
- they choose to try the artifact with a learner;
- the learner predicts, reconstructs, or explains something differently;
- the educator reuses or sends the artifact without prompting;
- either participant wants to return to the exact conceptual state; and
- the educator would notice if the artifact disappeared.

## Decision Gate

The grill-me review must still choose the first reachable educator cohort, its
recurring teaching problem, the smallest suitable existing artifact, the
session protocol, and the evidence threshold. After observed sessions, classify
the hypothesis as supported, mixed, or unsupported before resuming product
development.

- Preserve normal-matrix Proof Memory as architecture evidence, not the active

- Historical layouts, curriculum expansion, Public Web, advanced editor work,
  tutor/LLM systems, and SRS infrastructure remain paused.
- The existing development Pages directory and isolated-route consolidation
  are useful future operational work, but are not prerequisites for interviews
  or observed educator sessions.
