## Authority boundary

The model may select only:

- verified semantic source references;
- pinned registered operations and role bindings;
- explicit semantic correspondence and salience;
- named cadence, normal form, explanation depth, and compression intent; and
- epistemic status backed by external source authority or an explicit
  provisional/historical branch.

The compiler owns operation-pack resolution, canonical composition, laws,
witnesses, motif requirements, pacing units, provenance, stable
fingerprinting, and typed repair gaps. Native measurement and the canonical
renderer session own paint atoms, fragments, geometry, timing, styles,
typography, DOM, and clone-to-native settlement.

Both authoring schemas now reject nested attempts to provide fragment, paint,
geometry, rectangle/bounds, timing-table, style, computed-style, font, DOM, or
renderer authority. Unknown operations and invalid mathematics remain typed
repair or epistemic branches; neither can silently choose animation behavior.
