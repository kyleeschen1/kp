# KP Semantic Explanation Differentiation Proof Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: approved for execution through the first human checkpoint
Target: `next-action.kp.semantic-explanation-differentiation-proof-v1`
Contract: `run-contract.kp.semantic-explanation-differentiation-proof-v2`

## Objective

Prove the product claim provisionally titled **The Equation Remembers**: KP can
coordinate prose, semantic references, animation, direct navigation, and
responsive presentation around one persistent mathematical object. The proof
uses the recovered fraction-composition lesson, `2/3(x + 6) = 10`, and its
existing canonical equation session. It does not invent a new animation.

The differentiator under test is semantic explanation architecture, not a
novel page arrangement. Authors identify what deserves attention, what action
occurs, and what explanatory framing a beat requires. A projection decides how
to stage those relationships without changing object identity, animation
meaning, or the underlying clock.

## Canonical Reference And Acceptance

The canonical motion reference remains `/reader/fraction-composition/`; the
canonical document reference is the recovered fraction-composition Article.
The first proof projects one bounded explanation beat as an austere attention
stage over the same retained session, renderer, structural anchors, semantic
objects, native KaTeX endpoints, and deterministic clock.

At the first checkpoint, the exemplar must visibly establish that:

1. prose can address exact equation objects through focus, hover, pin, and a
   reconstructible URL without taking ownership of time;
2. the equation is recognizably the same object before, during, and after its
   canonical motion;
3. one screen contains the text needed to understand the staged beat at the
   preferred text size, with ordinary document flow as the large-text or short-
   viewport fallback;
4. the composition remains spatially stable and austere, with the order
   `stage`, `passage`, `progress`, `transport buttons` and accessible Back and
   Forward controls in a constant location;
5. the server-rendered lesson remains complete, searchable, indexable, and
   meaningful without JavaScript;
6. direct state restoration performs one seek rather than replaying prior
   motion.

The proof should make the learner's current primary target and necessary
context obvious. It must not claim that one visual treatment is already the
universal KP layout.

## Preservation Boundary

Preserve Article v1 syntax and source authority, the fraction-composition
semantic trace, evaluation tree, animation asset, renderer, compositor,
canonical stage, session lifecycle, full-timeline clock, native endpoints,
editor/save behavior, and existing reader appearance. Preserve economics,
Lisp, the catalogue, unrelated layout experiments, and all user-owned dirty or
untracked files.

The proof may add a projection adapter, semantic framing data derived from
existing Article IR, a bounded route or selectable view, focused tests, and
scoped presentation styles. It may not add a renderer, clock, animation model,
parallel equation DOM, framework-owned animation state, Article directive, or
responsive geometry to author syntax.

## Verification Cadence

Discovery follows the accepted hybrid cadence in
`../decisions/2026-08-10-kp-hybrid-experiment-verification-cadence.md`.
Semantic identity, time and lifecycle authority, deterministic seeking,
accessibility, no-JavaScript meaning, and known regressions are test-first.
Provisional spacing, choreography, emphasis, and composition receive only the
smallest focused unit coverage and one Chromium smoke before human review.

No responsive matrix, cross-browser matrix, dense screenshot sampling, shared
type family, or catalogue rollout belongs before the first checkpoint. Those
become valuable only after the user accepts the exemplar's visual language.

## Rollback And Promotion

The independently reversible rollback unit is the algebra proof projection,
its scoped adapter and styles, and its focused smoke coverage. Removing it must
leave the canonical Article, reader, animation, authoring grammar, and editor
unchanged.

Slice `s08` is a mandatory `HUMAN_CHECKPOINT`. Slices `s09`–`s17` remain locked
until the user approves the visual and attentional exemplar. Slice `s17` is a
second human checkpoint for compact reuse and alternate prose. Slices
`s18`–`s23` remain locked until that reuse boundary is accepted. Approval of
either checkpoint does not authorize catalogue-wide promotion.

## Stop Conditions

Stop immediately if the proof requires a new renderer or clock, changes the
canonical reader visually, alters Article v1 syntax, duplicates the live
equation stage, uses replay for direct restoration, moves responsive geometry
into authored content, overlaps unrelated user files, or cannot keep the
preferred stage text within one screen without changing its semantic meaning.
Stop at slices `s08` and `s17` until the named human judgment is available.
