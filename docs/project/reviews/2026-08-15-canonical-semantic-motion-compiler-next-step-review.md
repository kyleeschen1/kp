Date: 2026-08-15

This incremental-migration recommendation is superseded by the accepted
exhaustive equation-domain convergence decision in
`../decisions/2026-08-15-kp-exhaustive-symbolic-equation-convergence.md`.

## Outcome

The next repository tranche should establish one canonical semantic-motion
compiler front door. It should preserve the approved quotient, distribution,
and cancellation motifs, route all three through one validated authority
boundary, then prove lower marginal authoring cost with one new animation.

The recommended clean-path pressure caller is logarithm product expansion:

```text
ln(xy) -> ln(x) + ln(y)
```

This is deliberately near the approved evidence. It exercises one-to-two
operator derivation, argument role splitting, structural-shell lifecycle,
connector introduction, provenance, native KaTeX settlement, and exact rewind
without requiring a new renderer or clock. It is a credible test of whether a
new animation now consists mainly of semantic content and bindings.

## Recommended Tranche

1. **Freeze the public boundary.** Define one input over semantic endpoints,
   operation/role bindings, lineage, pedagogical intent, and salience; define
   `ready`, `repair-required`, `explicit-static`, and `human-review` outcomes.
2. **Route the three approved callers.** Adapt quotient fusion, distribution
   fan-out, and cancellation through the entry point without changing their
   reviewed choreography, timing, endpoints, URLs, or renderer ownership.
3. **Enforce authority.** Reject asset-authored concrete recipes, raw motif
   selection, local timing tables, renderer inference from glyph text, missing
   roles, false identity, incomplete lifecycle, and silent visual fallback.
4. **Connect governed drafts.** Let the existing LLM draft path submit only
   semantic states, operation/role bindings, lineage, teaching intent, and
   salience. KP retains recipe, timing, geometry, and renderer authority.
5. **Build the log-product caller.** Stop at a human checkpoint and measure
   files touched, renderer/runtime changes, repair count, authoring time, and
   whether semantic content dominates the diff.
6. **Decide from evidence.** If marginal cost falls and no competing authority
   appears, inventory paths as `canonical`, `adapter-backed`,
   `legacy-bespoke`, `static-only`, or `unsupported`. Otherwise repair the
   boundary before any breadth or migration.

## Acceptance Criteria

- One compiler call is the only supported front door for all four callers.
- The seven promoted invariants are validated before presentation resolution.
- The three approved animations remain visually and semantically unchanged.
- Unsupported or incomplete work fails closed with a typed outcome.
- Direct seek, rewind, reduced motion, native endpoint settlement,
  accessibility, and exclusive paint ownership remain deterministic.
- The new caller adds no clock, scheduler, renderer branch, or silent fallback.
- The new caller reaches a human-reviewable result mainly through semantic
  declarations and bindings.

## Preservation Boundary

Preserve the approved quotient, distribution, cancellation, and corrected
log/exponent outputs; their URLs, asset identities, clocks, presentation-local
geometry, native KaTeX authority, and accessibility behavior; the framework-
neutral animation boundary; and all current user-authored economics changes.

Do not reopen public layout, curriculum, SRS, advanced editor, SvelteKit,
Graph3D, or broad catalogue migration during this tranche.

## Rollback Units

1. compiler contract and fail-closed outcome;
2. each approved caller adapter independently;
3. governed-draft adapter;
4. new log-product caller;
5. architecture ratchets and compatibility retirement paired with their final
   migrated callers.
