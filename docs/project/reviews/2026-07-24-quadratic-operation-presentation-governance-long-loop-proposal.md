## Why the checkpoint was rejected

The graph, plus-minus branching, review inbox, semantic authorities, exact
roots, and shared-clock behavior are accepted. The two algebra methods are not
accepted for publication: their generic checkpoint transitions substitute
whole KaTeX states for operation-specific manipulation. As a result,
continuants arc as if they were operands, compound arithmetic disappears
inside jumps, and the visible solution-set reunion interrupts the more useful
branch-to-graph handoff.

The rejected checkpoint is represented by review notes
`review-note.13.mrzhovqp` through `review-note.20.mrzhxsef`. The notes require:

- persistent context to reflow before focal material moves;
- completing-square arithmetic, factoring, square-root, plus-minus, and
  isolation operations to remain causally visible;
- formula substitution, powers, products, subtraction, radical collapse,
  numerator work, and division to retain their actual operation motifs; and
- method branches to continue directly into the graph without a standalone
  visible solution-set scene.

## Canonical model

Compound algebra has one canonical atomic semantic trace. The default lesson
may present that trace as a compressed causal chain, but every operation
remains visible in dependency order and uses its operation-specific motif.
An optional “Show steps” drill-down expands the same trace, pauses the parent
clock, and returns to the exact parent frame.

The current quadratic animation is the representative exemplar. No family
generalization or Workbench publication is authorized by this contract.

## Observable acceptance criteria

1. Every authority operation is represented exactly once and in dependency
   order, unless a typed compound segment names the covered operations.
2. Every visible material entity has a total role: focal operand, continuant,
   introduced, eliminated, copied, merged, or structural.
3. Continuants reserve space and reflow before focal action begins.
4. Operation types select registered motifs; generic checkpoint motion cannot
   animate a transformation.
5. Compressed compound work shows distinct ordered operations rather than
   replacing endpoints, and drill-down uses the identical atomic trace.
6. Completing square visibly shows balance, addition on both sides, perfect
   square factoring, square root, plus-minus branching, and isolation.
7. The formula visibly shows coefficient substitution and dependency-ordered
   power, product, subtraction, radical, numerator, and division work.
8. Branches hand directly to the corresponding graph roots. Canonical
   solution-set truth remains available to semantics, accessibility, and
   inspection without requiring a visible reunion scene.
9. Direct seek, rewind, reduced/static motion, responsive layout, review
   capture, native KaTeX settlement, accepted graph behavior, and accepted
   branch identities remain intact.
10. The renewed contact sheet and live route receive explicit human approval
    before publication or generalization.

## Smallest rollback unit

Each slice is one independently reversible commit. Presentation certificates,
trace compilation, each method segment, drill-down, graph handoff, and route
ratchets remain separate rollback units. Rejecting the presentation does not
roll back the exact semantic authorities or accepted graph/branch semantics.

## Stop conditions

- **H1 HUMAN-EXEMPLAR:** stop at slice 20 for explicit visual approval.
- **H2 MATH:** stop on unverified algebra, inexact canonical results, or
  presentation-owned mathematical truth.
- **H3 CAUSALITY:** stop if a visible transformation lacks total operation
  coverage, material roles, phase order, or a registered motif.
- **H4 COMPRESSION:** stop if contextual speed-up omits an operation or if
  drill-down diverges from the canonical trace.
- **H5 PRESERVATION:** stop on accepted graph, branch, route, review,
  accessibility, native settlement, seek, rewind, or baseline drift.
- **H6 OWNERSHIP:** stop if a renderer must invent operations,
  correspondence, timing authority, or semantic identity.
- **H7 RADICAL-ISOLATION:** stop if the quadratic fix requires the radical
  animation's WebGL material path or changes its known residual.
- **H8 SCOPE:** stop before Workbench publication, family-wide
  generalization, another roadmap domain, a CAS, or broad renderer redesign.
- **H9 USER-OVERLAP:** stop if required work cannot be separated from
  unrelated user-authored changes.
- **H10 VERIFICATION:** stop if a required gate fails without a bounded
  in-scope repair.
