# Eigenvector Attentional Surface Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: approved for execution

## Objective

Build one isolated three-to-five-minute eigenvector experience at
`/learn/math/eigenvectors/` that tests whether KP can preserve a reusable way
of seeing while the learner never has to decide where to look.

The canonical content is the approved nine-beat sequence:

1. Most vectors change direction under a linear transformation.
2. A fan of vectors transforms under
   `A = [[2, 1], [1, 2]]`.
3. `v = (1, 1)` stretches without leaving its line.
4. The geometric relation becomes `Av = 3v`.
5. `3` generalizes to `lambda` in `Av = lambda v`.
6. The learner predicts which related vector must also survive direction.
7. `2v` transforms to `6v`, confirming the prediction.
8. Scalar manipulation reveals `span(v)` and the eigenspace distinction.
9. The experience compresses to the cue: the line of direction survives;
   `lambda` records what happens along it.

## Approved Ordinary Abundance Adaptation

The persistent transformation plane plays the role of the apartment: one
stable spatial memory surface throughout the experience. Ordinary readable
passages remain fully opaque in document flow. Crossing a stable reading line
selects one discrete semantic beat, runs at most one bounded transition, and
settles completely. Scroll does not continuously scrub ordinary animation
time.

On desktop the persistent plane and passage column may coexist because only
one owns detailed attention at a time. Reading beats keep the plane quiet;
motion beats use short passages and let the visual transition dominate. On
phones the compact plane pins above the passage. Fixed Back, Next, and compact
progress controls remain alternate navigation and direct-seek affordances.

All passages are emitted as searchable static HTML from the same local content
authority. Enhancement controls attention, motion, and navigation; it does not
create the explanation.

## Observable Acceptance Criteria

- One stable vector identity persists across SVG arrow, labels, KaTeX tokens,
  prediction option, scalar manipulation, and eigenspace cue.
- Each beat names exactly one dominant attentional owner; required context
  remains legible without competing.
- Scroll selects discrete states. Each transition settles without requiring
  precise scrolling, scroll lock, or hidden autoplay.
- Back, Next, scrub, keyboard, direct URL, reload, reverse, and interruption
  reach deterministic states without replaying prior motion.
- The prediction holds the scene until the learner acts and emits a semantic
  learner event rather than a pixel-derived event.
- Scalar manipulation distinguishes nonzero eigenvectors from the eigenspace,
  which includes zero.
- Static/no-JavaScript output contains the complete searchable explanation,
  server-rendered math, and a meaningful initial figure.
- The stage and active passage fit at 390 CSS pixels without horizontal
  overflow or geometry shift.
- Reduced-motion, forced-color, keyboard, and screen-reader paths preserve the
  mathematical meaning.
- The route imports no catalogue, editor, Svelte, Three.js, Graph3D, economics,
  or normal-matrix implementation closure.

## Preservation Boundary

- Preserve user-owned economics source and generated-publication edits.
- Preserve approved TypeScript, Python, Scheme, algebra, fraction-composition,
  normal-matrix, Graph2D, and catalogue behavior.
- Reuse the shared deterministic clock, salience intent, semantic scene,
  cross-view attention, native KaTeX, SVG, publication, and URL seams where
  they fit; do not modify shared contracts merely for naming consistency.
- Do not create a generic `Beat`, vector, matrix, layout, scrollytelling, or
  authoring framework from one caller.

## Rollback Unit

The feature is removable as one isolated route and its local semantic,
projection, SVG/KaTeX, publication, interaction, style, test, capture, Vite,
TypeScript, Playwright, and package-script files. Each verified slice is a
focused commit; shared changes require a demonstrated hard gap and otherwise
trigger a stop.

## Verification And Stop Conditions

Use focused tests in discovery, scoped type/build/browser checks at route
boundaries, and broad route closure only before the human checkpoint. Commit
each independently reversible slice with its Theseus evidence.

Stop if implementation requires a second clock, a new shared state store,
generic beat/layout authority, glyph-derived identity, model-authored geometry,
replay-based restoration, hidden searchable text, modification of protected
economics work, or a broad shared migration. Stop at s20 for human judgment;
no second caller or shared promotion is authorized.
