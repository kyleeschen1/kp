The user approved tracing and repairing the last-step overlap while preserving

## Responsible boundary and repair

Canonical artifact: the fractional linear equation animation asset. Host:
`/reader/solve-fractional-linear/`. Mathematical authority remains
`src/semantic/fractional-linear-equation-asset.ts`. The registered evaluation
presentation is compiled by the equation render plan and executed by the native
KaTeX scene compositor.

The host descriptor selected that compositor only for the two cancellations.
Both arithmetic simplifications already compile a verified `successor-synthesis`
plan with `kp.executable-program.operation-evaluation`, but the host bypassed
that authority and sampled the legacy fading material path instead. The violated
integration invariant is that these registered evaluation plans must actually
execute through their designated compositor.

The descriptor now selects the two arithmetic evaluations as well. The change
does not select every transition: the balanced introductions currently compile
explicit static checkpoints for the canonical path, so those retain their
existing animated implementation. The cancellation programs, semantic objects,
flat depth, typography, Nocturne palette, and shared motif definitions are
unchanged. Rollback unit: the two added descriptor selections and their local
regression expectations; no shared renderer migration is involved.

## Observed result

Before the repair, at overall progress 970 the old 2 and 4 each painted at
opacity 0.259259 while the incoming 8 painted at opacity 0.121230, overlapping
in the material layer. After the repair, the registered standard program keeps
opaque carriers and contracts the old factors. At 970 the old factors have
less than one pixel of width, with a full-sized opaque 8. At 980 only x, equals
and 8 remain painted. At 1000 the native solution owns the paint.

This is not a newly invented fade-out/reveal sequence. Standard evaluation
permits a bounded period of geometric contact between contributors and result;
the test does not forbid that contact. No retained derivation-trail snapshots
have been implemented.
