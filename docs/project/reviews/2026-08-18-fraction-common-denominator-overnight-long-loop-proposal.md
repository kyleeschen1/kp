# Fraction Common-Denominator Overnight Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: approved
Contract: `run-contract.kp.fraction-common-denominator-overnight-v2`

The `v1` control record was superseded before production work because its
multiword CLI fields were tokenized incorrectly. `v2` is the exact executable
contract for this reviewed proposal.

## Objective

Close the development-dock dark-theme gap, establish governed common-
denominator fraction semantics and authoring, and finish with one conservative
larger-expression fraction-equivalence caller for human review.

The loop deliberately front-loads objective semantic, authoring, compiler,
coverage, architecture, and theme work. Subjective visual work is limited to
the final caller and may not promote a shared motif before human approval.

## Canonical reference and exemplar

- Canonical reference: the approved explanatory and compact fraction-
  equivalence presentations.
- Pressure caller:
  `1/3 + 1/6 -> (2*1)/(2*3) + 1/6 -> 2/6 + 1/6`.
- Equivalence and numeric evaluation remain separate verified operations.
- The addition sign and untouched `1/6` retain stable semantic identity.

## Allowed work

- Development-dock theme tokens, reserved-area paint, toolbar styling, and
  focused route/browser evidence.
- Fraction semantics, correspondence, assumptions, typed repair, declaration,
  governed binding, planner vocabulary, deterministic series compilation, and
  fixed model-neutral corpus.
- Registered common-denominator alignment and like-denominator combination
  capabilities without claiming animated parity.
- One local presentation plan and native-KaTeX pressure caller composed from
  already approved operations and motifs.
- Catalogue registration, exact URL state, focused capture tooling, project
  memory, and Theseus evidence.

## Disallowed work

- Changing or globally promoting approved fraction-equivalence choreography.
- Inventing the common-denominator addition animation before human review.
- New clocks, renderers, compositor branches, universal fraction/CAS schemas,
  or silent fallbacks.
- Roots, matrices, code, graphs, layout, curriculum, public-product, or live-
  model work.
- User-owned economics changes or unrelated untracked documents.

## Verification and cadence

Use focused tests during semantic and compiler work, one-worker browser checks
at host boundaries, and the affected broad gate only at s21 and s26. Do not run
a live-model benchmark. Commit at the named boundaries and store slice status,
context receipts, and verification in Theseus.

## Done contract

The loop ends as `HUMAN_CHECKPOINT` when all objective work is green and the
dark/light, wide/narrow, forward/seek/rewind contact sheet is ready. Common-
denominator capabilities remain `Registered`, and shared motif promotion
remains forbidden until explicit human approval.
