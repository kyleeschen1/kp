## Reproduced matrix defect and candidate boundary

`presentation.ts` derives `settled` from the completed beat, then sets every
`data-reveal` group to either zero or one. Thus incoming column brackets and
entire evaluated rows wait until the endpoint and appear abruptly. The scalar
distribution beat already has material owners and bracket reflow; preserve it.

Canonical artifact: column-combination scene. Host:
`/experiments/matrix-column-combinations/`. Renderer: local native KaTeX plus
inert material occurrences. Semantic source: the existing matrix product and
column-combination objects. Candidate selector: `?motion=continuous`, limited
to the ordinary column-combination example. Baseline and other interpretations
retain their existing motion.

The candidate reuses the function-wrap reception profile for vector enclosure
entrance and the dot passage's contributor-fusion projection for multiplication
and addition. Copies preserve the original calculation as readable history;
results remain derived values. This local optical reuse is not certification
of the canonical native compositor. No universal renderer seam is introduced.

Acceptance: columns arrive before enclosure reception completes; evaluations
show contributors resolving into derived entries; no complete line suddenly
appears at its endpoint; scalar distribution remains intact; rewind, direct
seek and resize reproduce the same paint; exact final values remain 4 and 10.
Timing and legibility require human review. The smallest rollback unit is the
candidate selector and local continuity adapter; semantic contracts remain intact.

## Maintenance and delivery cost

One local optional adapter composes existing profiles; no semantic framework,
new page or universal motion API was added. It allocates 18 inert contributor
occurrences for this 2×2 exemplar, reused across frames and rebuilt on resize.
The existing material layer owns their disposal. The baseline does not load the
candidate adapter: it is dynamically imported only by the selected URL.

The production build reports the optional continuity chunk at 8.22 kB minified,
2.80 kB gzip, plus the reusable fusion chunk at 4.68 kB / 1.76 kB gzip. Those
two chunks total 12.90 kB / 4.56 kB gzip before caching; these are artifact sizes,
not an end-to-end network measurement. No cost ceiling was raised. Native KaTeX,
fonts and existing shared dependencies remain the dominant shared assets.

The baseline/candidate branch is temporary review scaffolding. After visual
acceptance, the approved integration package should select one default and
remove the comparison switch, subject to the changed-input preservation checks.
Keeping both indefinitely is not the proposed convergence outcome.
