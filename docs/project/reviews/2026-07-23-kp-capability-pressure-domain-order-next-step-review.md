## Ordered capability waves

### Wave 1: Continuous cross-view convergence

**Canonical exemplar:** two points on `y=f(x)`, a finite secant, a KaTeX
difference quotient, and continuous `h -> 0` convergence to the tangent and
`f'(a)`.

**New reusable contracts:**

- continuously parameterized semantic state;
- one derived quantity projected into KaTeX and SVG;
- finite-to-limit convergence;
- secant/tangent role continuity;
- exact seek and rewind across renderers;
- learner math overlays that never fall back to plain SVG math text.

**Promotion evidence:** wide and narrow learner projections, searchable static
review, exact sampled laws, deterministic captures, and human approval.

### Wave 2: Solution-set branching and equation/graph roots

**Canonical exemplar:** complete the square for a quadratic, branch through
`±`, settle two solutions, and synchronize them with the parabola's roots.

**New reusable contracts:**

- semantic regrouping without identity loss;
- balanced introduction used to create a perfect square;
- one-to-many solution-set branching;
- branch reunion into a set-valued answer;
- equation-root correspondence.

**Promotion evidence:** both branches remain inspectable and rewind exactly;
the graph roots and symbolic solutions share stable identities.

### Wave 3: Continuous accumulation

**Canonical exemplar:** accumulation under a curve with a moving upper bound,
KaTeX accumulation expression, and exact area readout.

**New reusable contracts:** continuous aggregation, persistent region growth,
bound provenance, finite partition views, and later derivative/accumulation
duality.

### Wave 4: Higher-order composition

**Canonical exemplars:** dot product as geometric projection, then
matrix-vector multiplication as row-wise dot products synchronized with a
linear map.

**New reusable contracts:** indexed traversal, repeated subanimation,
projection geometry, higher-order composition, and coordinated
symbolic/geometric result assembly.

### Wave 5: Exact arithmetic quantity and algorithms

Run this as one exemplar-gated lane, not one monolithic implementation.

| Order | Canonical exemplar | Required synchronized views | New motif or contract |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | `1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2` | KaTeX, partitioned circles, fraction bars/area, number line | Partition refinement, common-denominator alignment, exact part merge |
| 5.2 | `278 + 156 = 434` | KaTeX/column form, place-value table, base-ten quantities | Exchange ten lower-place units for one higher-place unit; carry provenance |
| 5.3 | `402 - 178 = 224` | KaTeX/column form, place-value table, decomposed quantities | Inverse exchange, borrowing provenance, exact rewind |
| 5.4 | `23 × 14 = 322` | KaTeX/column form, partial products, area grid | Two-dimensional distribution, ordered partial products, regrouping |
| 5.5 | `732 ÷ 6 = 122` | KaTeX/long form, grouping or area view, quotient trace | Iterative consumption, quotient-place settlement, remainder contract |
| 5.6 | Ratio/rate/percent scale | KaTeX, double number line or table, optional area model | Typed units, proportional scale, conversion provenance |

Partitioned-circle views are required evidence for appropriate proper-fraction
examples, but the semantic model is partition/refinement rather than
“pie-chart animation.” This keeps the same operation usable for improper
fractions, number-line reasoning, and area models.

Arithmetic acceptance requires:

- exact integer or rational state throughout;
- visible conservation during exchange, partition, or grouping;
- no unexplained digit teleportation;
- the written algorithm and quantity model on one clock;
- direct seek, exact rewind, reduced/static alternatives, and spoken semantic
  descriptions;
- composition from reusable exchange, partition, traversal, merge, and
  settlement motifs.
