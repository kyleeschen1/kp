# Catalogue Curation And Cross-Domain Promotion Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-08-01
Status: approved in conversation; executable order lives in Theseus

## Why This Loop Is Current

The catalogue simplification loop proved that KP does not have a large hosting
port backlog: 31 of 33 concrete assets already paint through native adapters,
two stop honestly at the absent programming adapter, and none require an
iframe. The next useful work is therefore not a renderer migration. It is a
short curation and catalogue-usage pass, followed by the first two unresolved
cross-domain promotions.

This order deliberately lets real economics and physics callers pressure the
shared seams before KP declares API tiers or standardizes motifs. It also keeps
pruning adjacent to the migration that makes it safe. The user approved this
order in conversation on 2026-08-01:

1. simplify and curate the internal catalogue experience;
2. build a bounded supply-and-demand equilibrium exemplar;
3. build a bounded constant-force work-energy second caller; and
4. consolidate only the APIs and motifs those callers prove, then remove only
   adjacent obsolete paths.

The typed Theseus run contract is the sole owner of the exact slice order,
live status, evidence, and stop state. This proposal owns rationale, scope, and
the human visual checkpoints without duplicating the slice ledger.

## Catalogue Curation And UX Boundary

The current approved catalogue shell around
`animation.linear-solve.solve-x` is the canonical reference. Its flat rail,
frameless stage, compact transport, linear inspector, inline KaTeX, compact
headings, and lower-left review capture remain stable.

The next exemplar changes three observable behaviors:

- selecting another catalogue row updates the URL and selected asset inside
  the existing shell rather than causing a document navigation;
- the rail query, rail scroll, focused control, inspector choice, review draft,
  and surrounding shell remain present while only selected-asset ownership is
  replaced;
- equation content is centered inside a bounded stage envelope where its
  surface contract permits it, and solve-x's visual step strip is substantially
  shorter without changing semantic beats or animation duration.

Full document navigation remains the progressive fallback when JavaScript is
unavailable, the link is modified, the destination is external, or the
selection cannot be resolved safely. Browser history and direct artifact URLs
remain canonical and Back/Forward restore the selected asset.

### Catalogue preservation boundary

- semantic objects, transformations, selectors, lineage, and authored assets;
- deterministic runtime, clock, play, pause, seek, and exact rewind;
- renderer and paint ownership, font settlement, and surface adapters;
- review-note history, screenshot capture, and production erasure;
- keyboard and accessibility behavior;
- the one-row-per-concrete-asset catalogue identity law; and
- named editor, workbench, dashboard, reader, and diagnostic routes.

### Catalogue rollback and promotion

The smallest rollback unit is one independently committed behavior: shell
navigation, stage geometry, or editorial catalogue metadata. The solve-x
visual change stops for human review before its centering or compact-step rule
is generalized to sibling equation assets.

Promotion requires all of the following:

- no document load during an ordinary catalogue-row selection;
- stable rail, inspector, and review-composer state across selection;
- correct direct URL and Back/Forward restoration;
- unchanged semantic frames, timing, seek/rewind, and paint ownership;
- a visually centered solve-x stage with a markedly shorter step strip at the
  accepted desktop viewport; and
- explicit human approval of the live exemplar.

Catalogue curation must remain simple. It may record evidence-backed editorial
roles and dispositions, but it must not add a filter dashboard, ontology tree,
or another visible toggle bank. The user's specific concern about the
Jacobian/Hessian comparison authorizes treating it as a diagnostic/retirement
candidate, not deleting the semantic fixture or its tests before their unique
contract value is replaced. Other human dispositions remain unassigned unless
the user reviews them.

## Economics Exemplar Boundary

The first domain promotion is one bounded linear supply-and-demand equilibrium
shift, not an economics engine. The canonical semantic example uses quantity
on the horizontal axis and price on the vertical axis, with deterministic
linear curves and exact equilibrium identity. A demand-intercept shift should
preserve named supply, demand, equilibrium, surplus/shortage-side, parameter,
and narrative correspondences across all frames.

The exemplar should reuse the catalogue host, shared runtime clock, graph
surface, inline mathematical annotation, and review capture. Bespoke economic
parameters belong behind the existing Parameters disclosure; the default stage
still shows only Play/Pause and the scrubber. A small exact model owns economic
truth. The renderer samples its frames and does not solve economics.

Acceptance requires exact model tests, deterministic parameter restoration,
direct seek and rewind, one canonical paint owner, accessible graph labels and
nonvisual state, synchronized equation/graph/narrative evidence, and one stable
catalogue visual capture. The economics exemplar stops for human review before
its choreography or visual motifs are promoted.

The rollback unit is the new economics asset pack entry, its exact semantic
model and presenter, catalogue metadata, and focused checks. It must not alter
existing algebra, graph, or diagram callers to make the exemplar pass.

## Physics Second-Caller Boundary

After economics approval, one constant-force work-energy exemplar pressures the
same synchronized-model-view environment with different domain semantics. The
bounded story relates force over displacement, rectangular area under the
force-position graph, work, and kinetic-energy change with explicit units. It
does not authorize a mechanics engine.

The second caller should reuse only seams already justified by economics or
existing platform contracts. Its semantic model, unit laws, and domain
correspondence remain subject-native. Acceptance mirrors economics: exact
identity tests, deterministic frames and parameters, seek/rewind, exclusive
paint ownership, accessibility, synchronized views, catalogue capture, and a
human exemplar checkpoint.

The rollback unit is the physics asset, presenter, metadata, and focused tests.

## API, Motif, And Pruning Boundary

Only after both cross-domain callers pass their human checkpoints may the loop
consolidate shared code. The audit must compare existing algebra/graph/diagram
callers, economics, and physics before changing a public boundary.

Allowed consolidation is deliberately narrow:

- classify stable authoring facades, internal compiler/reader/rendering APIs,
  and experimental surfaces from observed callers;
- extract a shared synchronized-model projection or lifecycle contract only
  when both new callers express the same invariant;
- create a versioned motif implementation only for an intentional shared
  visual redesign approved through both exemplars; and
- retire compatibility metadata, full-reload catalogue selection code, or an
  obsolete presentation route in the same reversible change that proves its
  last real caller moved.

The loop must not invent a universal renderer, scene graph, economics solver,
physics engine, universal parameter schema, universal motif registry, public
website, curriculum, learner system, or live LLM editorial service. It must not
delete the Jacobian/Hessian semantic fixture merely because its catalogue
presentation is demoted, and it must not convert unreviewed catalogue rows into
human dispositions by inference.

## Verification Cadence

Inner-loop verification follows changed-path impact, focused unit or browser
proof, and the stable `npm run visual:animation-catalogue` command. Each
subjective exemplar receives only the smoke and preservation checks needed to
reach human review.

At promotion boundaries, run the touched subsystem tests, typecheck,
architecture checks, production review erasure, promotion-memory validation,
and relevant stable visual command. The broad test/build/browser/Theseus gate
runs only after the approved second caller and consolidation boundary, or when
a shared integration change makes it necessary earlier.

## Stop Conditions

Stop immediately when:

- a visual exemplar reaches its required human checkpoint;
- work would change semantic truth, clocks, renderer ownership, or established
  authoring contracts merely to simplify presentation;
- shell persistence requires parallel state, a second review history, iframe
  fallback, or an asset-specific shared-shell exception;
- economics or physics needs a general solver/engine rather than its bounded
  exact model;
- API or motif consolidation is not supported by at least two approved,
  structurally different callers;
- pruning would remove unique semantic, conformance, compatibility, or
  historical evidence; or
- verification evidence is missing, contradictory, or cannot be recorded
  durably.

## Explicit Deferrals

- programming execution-trace semantics and its missing surface adapter;
- the public KP website and publication presentation;
- full curriculum, learner accounts, teacher tooling, and phone polish;
- generated-session and LLM editorial milestones;
- later vector, matrix, calculus, and breadth promotions;
- broad dashboard/workbench deletion; and
- catalogue-wide visual restyling beyond an approved, pressure-tested rule.
