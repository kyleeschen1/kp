# Semantic presentation continuity long-loop proposal

Date: 2026-07-24

Status: approved for execution through the distribution checkpoint

## Goal

Prevent compound expressions from losing internal coherence or changing
geometry when presentation ownership transfers. The first two exemplars are:

1. distribution as an intra-owner relative-geometry failure; and
2. radical rewind as an inter-owner presentation-equivalence failure.

The general result must protect any declared compound presentation group rather
than hard-coding the first product, horizontal algebra, or the `1/2` exponent.

## Canonical references

- Distribution: the accepted lesson choreography at
  `/reader/distribution-area/`.
- Radical rewind: untouched native KaTeX `x^{1/2}`.
- Native KaTeX remains final typography and spacing authority.

## Authorization boundary

Allowed work:

- typed choreography groups and settlement contracts;
- relative-geometry, rendered-ink, velocity, rewind, and ownership laws;
- distribution card and Workbench settlement;
- radical WebGL-to-native settlement;
- focused promotion diagnostics, tests, and stable visual tooling;
- project-control evidence needed for this exact loop.

Disallowed work:

- new animation families or curriculum implementation;
- semantic algebra changes;
- KaTeX modification, Three.js, or renderer replacement;
- unrelated roadmap, thread, and exploratory files;
- broad visual changes to other families before promotion evidence.

## Preservation boundary

- Preserve exact seek and rewind.
- Preserve semantic identity, correspondence, and factor lineage.
- Preserve independent motion before cohesion lock.
- Preserve native KaTeX endpoints.
- Preserve lesson routes, card identities, accessibility modes, and review
  provenance.

Each implementation slice owns one independently reversible commit with its
Theseus evidence. The two human checkpoints stop execution before the next
subjective tranche.

## Promotion criteria

Distribution passes when every preserved product settles without a late
relative-gap correction across arbitrary term positions, widths, seek, and
rewind.

Radical rewind passes when the outgoing WebGL `1/2` and incoming native KaTeX
`1/2` are visually coincident before ownership changes, with no scale,
position, or ink-bound adjustment hidden by crossfade.

The general law may be promoted only after both checkpoints pass. Existing
animations receive explicit audit results rather than silent visual rewrites.

## Done contract

The loop is complete only when:

1. declared compound presentation groups have enforceable intra-owner and
   inter-owner continuity laws;
2. distribution passes its arbitrary-product settlement contract;
3. radical rewind passes true rendered-output/native equivalence;
4. future compound settlement cannot promote without a group contract or
   typed exemption;
5. all release evidence and both human checkpoints are complete.
