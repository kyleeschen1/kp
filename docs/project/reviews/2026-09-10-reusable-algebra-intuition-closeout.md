## Limits and hindsight

This loop was worthwhile because it delivers a complete reusable explanation
instead of another isolated movement demo. It also cost more than composing
existing motif names suggested: semantic equivalence did not settle native paint
ownership between whole and member representations. That repair now has real
executable coverage, but this is not universal KaTeX certification.

Supported algebra is deliberately narrow: two nonnegative integer counts of the
same ordered binary sum, exact count evaluation, distribution, and optional final
constant-product evaluation. No arbitrary LaTeX chain, general CAS, unrestricted
symbolic evaluation, universal intuition ontology or automatic prose correctness
is claimed. No fresh model trial or learner study ran in this loop. Predict and
Reconstruct are self-checks, not automatic reasoning graders. WebKit coverage is
not physical-device Safari testing. Static editions are readable immutable output,
not animated export or deployment. Edited URLs alone do not preserve custom JSON.

The shared runtime increase deserves attention in future affected work. Keep
native validation intact; optional-loading work must retain issued authority and
measure real closures. Do not start a generic optimization or infrastructure loop
as a prerequisite to the next explanation without evidence that it blocks it.

## Recommended next proposal: one geometric intuition

Question: **Why does the gradient point across contours in the direction of
greatest local increase?** Explain local change, not a full multivariable course.
At a regular point of a smooth scalar field, movement along its level contour has
zero first-order change; the gradient is normal to that contour, and its direction
maximizes directional derivative among unit directions when nonzero. Explicitly
exclude the zero-gradient point from a unique-direction claim.

The next proposal should begin by locating the exact accepted surface-to-contour
artifact, host, renderer and semantic expression authority. Do not infer canonical
identity from an asset label or assume the algebra renderer governs geometry.
Use the existing Graph3D/diagram owners as applicable, with shared explanation
and interaction contracts only where they already fit.

Suggested bounded sequence for that future proposal: question/answer/evidence
brief; preserve the existing contour exemplar; one reversible linked-view
explanation; human checkpoint; source-only changed field/location; one meaningful
independent sub-explanation and exact return; existing reading/self-check/static

an across-contour move, then a comparable set of unit directions. Keep the point
and geometric referents identifiable while the representation changes.

Review the observable understanding question, not only motion polish: can a reader
predict which equal-distance small move increases height most and explain why
