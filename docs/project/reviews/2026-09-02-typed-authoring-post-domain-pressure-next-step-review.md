## Recommendation

Do not promote the experimental supply-demand model or connect it directly to
the visible supply-tax card yet. First restore the two known broad health
gates. Then pressure the repeated unit-map authoring mechanics with one
test-owned nonlinear geometry caller, `radius -> circle area`. Extract one
optional unit-aware scalar-map builder only if that caller repeats the market's
space, derivative, law-evidence, and runtime-unit boilerplate.

After that decision, reconcile the typed market with the existing
exact-rational supply-tax semantics before adding a developer-visible scenario
inspector. KP should not acquire two independent sources of economics truth
merely because both currently return the same fixture values.

## Ordered Next Steps

### 1. Restore the broad health baseline

- Inventory the Focus Deck log-exponent clock through the existing ownership
  mechanism without changing its timing or visible behavior.
- Attribute the nine fixtures added since the repository-wide inference
  budget recorded 21 fixtures, then remeasure its current 30-fixture closure.
- Rebaseline only after attribution shows no accidental public-barrel or
  framework closure. Keep the focused typed-math budget independent.

Done means `npm run check:architecture` and `npm run check:inference` pass for
the reasons they claim to measure. The clock inventory and inference record
are separate rollback commits.

### 2. Add one structurally different unit-scalar caller

Author a test-owned circle measurement object with typed radius and area
units, `A(r) = pi r^2`, and the point-dependent derivative
`dA/dr = 2 pi r`. Use the current authoring and unit packs as they exist; do
not create a helper first.

This caller is deliberately nonlinear. It tests whether the market's repeated
plumbing is a genuine unit-aware differentiable-map seam rather than an
artifact of affine economics. It also repeats the unresolved question of
declared versus derived product units without requiring a unit algebra.

### 3. Make one evidence-gated helper decision

Compare the market and circle implementations. If both repeat the same
mechanics, introduce one optional experimental builder roughly shaped like:

```ts
defineKpAuthoredUnitScalarMap(author, {
  path,
  domainUnit,
  codomainUnit,
  evaluateMagnitude,
  derivativeMagnitudeAt
});
```

The caller still authors the mathematical rule. The builder may derive stable
space and map IDs, unit guards, derivative-map wrapping, and named law
evidence. It must not guess a derivative, unit product, basis, equality, or
proof. Migrate only the two pressure callers, measure author-line and inference
cost, and stop if the helper merely moves complexity into generics.

If the mechanics do not repeat cleanly, record that result and keep both
callers local. No abstraction is also a successful outcome.

### 4. Establish one economics source of truth

Compare the typed market's baseline and seller-tax snapshots with the existing
canonical exact-rational supply-tax model. Choose an explicit adapter or shared
formula boundary so authoring convenience cannot diverge from runtime
economics authority. Add parity and negative tests for units, tax incidence,
welfare, and source identity. Do not replace the current animation, renderer,
clock, or Article source in this slice.

The price-floor scenario remains experimental unless the canonical economics
model gains matching semantics and an explicit rationing contract.

### 5. Make the result visible through one reversible inspector

Only after semantic convergence, add a developer-only read-only scenario
inspector for baseline, seller tax, and price floor. The canonical visible
reference is the existing supply-tax Focus Deck card; the inspector's purpose
is to expose typed inputs, derived values, and stable entity IDs, not to invent
new animation choreography.

Acceptance criteria are deterministic scenario switching, direct URL recovery,
legible quantities and welfare, and no mutation of the approved Focus Deck.
Use a dedicated experimental route as the rollback unit and stop for human
review before connecting the model to animation or Article authoring.

## Subsequent Pressure

After the inspector checkpoint, ask an LLM to author several valid variations
and several intentionally unsupported ones through only the curated imports.
Measure compile success, diagnostic usefulness, manual repair count, and
whether unsupported requests remain typed gaps. Do not let model output supply
law evidence, unit identities, domain formulas, renderer choices, or timing.

## Preservation Boundary

- Preserve the existing supply-tax, log-exponent, Graph3D, and TypeScript
  Focus Deck cards and their clocks, renderers, Article sources, and reviewed
  behavior.
- Preserve the current public authoring names until two callers show a reason
  to change them.
- Keep matrices basis-dependent and derivatives coordinate-free.
- Keep exact-rational domain authority exact; do not silently lower it to
  floating point for adapter convenience.
- Do not add a universal typeclass hierarchy, economics ontology, derived-unit
  engine, CAS, registry, Article grammar, or automatic animation generation.

## Approval Boundary

Approval of this review authorizes the two health repairs and the isolated
circle pressure caller. The helper remains conditional on observed repetition.
Economics convergence and the visible inspector are later independently
reversible slices, with the inspector ending at a human checkpoint.
