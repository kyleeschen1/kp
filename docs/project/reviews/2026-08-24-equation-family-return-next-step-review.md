## Finding

Build one bounded indefinite-integration power-rule exemplar next:

\[
\int x^2\,dx
\longrightarrow
\frac{x^{2+1}}{2+1}+C
\longrightarrow
\frac{x^3}{3}+C.
\]

This is the best next family because it introduces genuinely new semantics
while reusing mechanisms that have just passed equation, generation, and
Native KaTeX pressure. It also turns the operator-scope presentation question
into a concrete cross-caller decision instead of an abstract framework task.

The gallery run already repaired the two objective drifts named in the prior
family review: its second slice restored exponential-homomorphism pressure
fixtures and its third slice restored generated linear-solve parity. No
separate stabilization loop is required before the exemplar.

exercise existing replacement and wrapping transformations. Inequality
reversal is a strong later family because it changes a relation under a
verified operation, but it does not settle operator scope.

## Canonical Exemplar Contract

- **Canonical artifact:** one governed integration animation asset for the
  exact three-state sequence above.
- **Host:** the canonical generated equation session in the Internal Studio
  Catalogue.
- **Renderer:** Native KaTeX through the shared equation renderer adapter.
- **Semantic source of truth:** a verified integration trace and explicit
  lineage, never model-authored LaTeX or explanation prose.

Observable acceptance criteria:

1. The integral operator owns a named integrand scope, and `dx` identifies the
   integration variable rather than acting as decorative paint.
2. The source exponent contributes to both introduced `2 + 1` expressions
   through provenance without pretending that one paint occurrence is cloned.
3. `+C` appears as required introduced mathematical structure.
4. Both `2 + 1 -> 3` evaluations use the certified ink-knot contributor-fusion
   mechanism rather than generic replacement.
5. Operator emphasis is only a salience change. For this exemplar, recommend
   the renderer-owned `salience-only` scope treatment; retain `outline` for the
   already approved derivative and keep `none` available without caller edits.
6. Direct seek, reverse, replay, theme response, native endpoints,
   accessibility, and typed unsupported cases remain deterministic.

## Generation and Explanation Benchmark Fit

Make the exemplar benchmark-ready without opening a separate infrastructure
loop. Its teaching goal and verified trace should enter the governed canonical
construction request; the compiler should select registered operations and
presentation profiles; explanatory claims should bind to the trace; and every
unsupported variant should return a typed repair gap.

Do not make live-model repetition or broad corpus expansion a prerequisite for
the first visual checkpoint. After the choreography is approved, this exemplar
can become the first new-family case in the recurring prompt-to-reviewed-
artifact benchmark. This keeps subjective discovery ahead of broad
certification while still building on the production authoring path.

## Bounded Execution Sequence

1. Declare the exact integration semantics, roles, lineage, assumptions, and
   typed unsupported cases.
2. Extract the renderer-owned operator-scope policy as `outline`,
   `salience-only`, or `none`, preserving the derivative's current appearance.
3. Compile only the canonical three-state exemplar through the governed
   construction entrance and canonical Catalogue host.
4. Add focused semantic, lifecycle, and Native KaTeX mechanism checks plus the
   smallest stable visual review sheet.
5. Stop for human review before a second caller, family promotion, arbitrary
   exponents, definite integrals, substitution, area, the Fundamental Theorem,
   or a live-model benchmark expansion.

The preservation boundary is the approved derivative, completed gallery
artifacts, mathematical authority, compiler-owned motif selection,
deterministic clock, native endpoints, and typed-gap behavior. Independently
reversible rollback units are the semantic integration declaration, the scope
presentation policy, and the single exemplar route.

## Follow-on If Approved

After human approval, pressure the family with a structurally different
caller before promotion. A sum-rule case such as
`integral (x^2 + 1) dx` is a better second caller than another monomial because
it tests scope, linearity, multiple persistent terms, and one final constant
without jumping to substitution or definite integration.
