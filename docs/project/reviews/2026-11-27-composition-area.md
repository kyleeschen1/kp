## Why this medium

The obstacle is reading BA as “B first,” or assuming equal determinants imply
equal transformations. The static baseline is two product equations and endpoint
parallelograms. Motion lets the reader track the same ordered corners through
each application; paired equal-scale diagrams keep order comparisons available.
Scrubbing isolates the intermediate image and supports reversal without scrolling.
Reflection asks what a negative sign means; collapse asks whether the second map
can restore lost area. Text explains the determinant law and remains readable
without motion. Animated geometry illustrates these claims; it is not their proof.
No learner-transfer or generic animation-authoring claim follows from tests.
