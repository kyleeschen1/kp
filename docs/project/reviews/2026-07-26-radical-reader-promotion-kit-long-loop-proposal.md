# Radical Reader Promotion Kit Long-Loop Proposal

Date: 2026-07-26
Status: proposed; awaiting explicit approval
Mode: long

## Objective

Adopt one radical-succession reader transition through the released canonical
construction path, preserve the current reader and native semantic contracts,
and extract a promotion kit whose reuse is proved by a no-product-route
unit-exponent dry run.

Canonical exemplar:

```text
x^(1/2) -> sqrt(x)
```

## Boundaries

- Canonical reference: the released fraction split/merge reader transition and
  its canonical renderer session.
- Acceptance: correct native size and baselines at both endpoints; no snap,
  fade, double paint, overlap, or lifecycle gap; exact seek/rewind; responsive,
  accessibility, static/reduced-motion, export, and governed-construction
  preservation.
- Preservation: existing semantic traces, lesson contracts, annotations, hover,
  focus, Cloze behavior, URLs, and unmigrated compatibility-only animations.
- Rollback unit: the radical route registration, its lesson/descriptor wiring,
  and directly associated tests. The compositor core may receive only generic
  repairs demonstrated by the exemplar.
- Exemplar checkpoint: after slice 15. No promotion or generalization continues
  until the user reviews the browser exemplar.
- Promotion criterion: the unit-exponent dry run needs no compositor-core,
  lifecycle-category, scheduler-category, notation-geometry, or runtime-artifact
  addition.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and report `HUMAN_CHECKPOINT` after slice 15. Stop earlier with
`STOP_CONDITION` if any fix requires operation-specific geometry, a second
runtime or clock, a new lifecycle/scheduler category, false semantic identity,
simultaneous legacy/canonical paint, or changes outside the preservation
boundary. Stop at slice 18 if the unit-exponent cost ratchet fails; do not
disguise the failure as promotion-kit success.

## Completion

Completion means the radical reader exemplar is approved and canonical, the
promotion kit is evidenced rather than speculative, the unit-exponent dry run
passes the cost ratchet, and the next proposed product loop is foldable
distribution/collection. It does not authorize radical-family rollout.
