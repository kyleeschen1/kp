## Outcome

The approved autonomous tranche is complete through its final objective slice.
It leaves one economics-local quiet stacked exemplar for human judgment and
does not promote that presentation into shared grammar.

The work that does not depend on that judgment is now durable:

- the public lesson is complete, searchable route HTML with stable semantic
  identities, server-rendered KaTeX HTML/MathML, an initial SVG stage, and a
  useful no-JavaScript reading path;
- progressive enhancement adopts the published document rather than replacing
  it, and one retained runtime plus one clock owns graph behavior;
- the default learner route excludes the optional presenter and CodeMirror
  closures, while direct presenter and editor entry remain available;
- tutorial foundation, graph surface, dimensional continuity, economics, and
  lesson-projection styles have explicit ownership;
- the whole lesson has one typed Markdown-like CodeMirror/Vim buffer, one
  undo/redo history, last-valid preview, semantic completion and diagnostics,
  reversible passage commands, save/recovery, and native `:q`, `:w`, and `:wq`;
- the published hierarchical TOC includes sections and motion-passage starts,
  not scrub moments, and remains useful as native light-DOM links;
- URLs, TOC clicks, history restoration, previous/next, skip, settle, and exit
  use one state-before-viewport navigation transaction without replay;
- checkpoint-level graph descriptions, keyboard navigation, visible focus,
  reduced motion, forced colors, mobile disclosure, and no-JavaScript behavior
  have dedicated verification.

## Human Checkpoint

Review only the provisional quiet stacked choreography and choose **approve**,
**refine**, or **reject**. In particular, inspect:

1. whether the stage and page sharing one background makes the argument feel
   continuous rather than like an embedded player;
2. whether the initial passage is already in its correct settled state;
3. whether upcoming prose is quiet enough and the focus transition has a clear
   but restrained scale-and-brightness pop;
4. whether the stage-to-progress spacing, eye path, and overall amount of
   movement feel calm on desktop and phone.

Approval permits motif-specific regression checks, pressure from a
structurally different caller, and then split-projection parity. Refinement
changes only this exemplar. Rejection rolls back only the economics-local
animation-station presenter, styles, and preservation tests.

## Preservation And Non-Claims

Regardless of the visual decision, preserve static publication, semantic IDs,
the retained SVG session, one shared clock, direct state restoration, the
whole-lesson buffer/editor, semantic navigation, accessibility, and route
performance work.

This closeout does **not** approve a shared station grammar, split-layout
parity, final floating-TOC geometry, a release, an algebra caller, 3D parity,
SvelteKit, or a catalogue-wide migration. The animation-library promotion
rank remains unchanged.
