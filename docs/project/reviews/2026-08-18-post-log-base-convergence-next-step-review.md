## Why This Order

Opening fraction work before this slice would have left a nearly complete
family stranded one authoring step short of the project's stated goal. That
seam is now closed. Measuring a live model immediately afterward
reveals whether KP's next bottleneck is vocabulary, repair feedback, semantic
coverage, or motif coverage. That evidence is more useful than adding another
family speculatively.

Educator discovery remains the highest product-validation activity, but it is
an external parallel track rather than a reason to reopen layout or public-site
work in this repository.
