## Result

The canonical native-KaTeX renderer is behaviorally healthy but its ownership
boundary is no longer sealed. The four canonical owners remain under their
fixed source ceiling. Their exact twenty-file direct dependency inventory is
14,245 bytes over its unchanged ceiling.

This is the frozen pre-refactor baseline. Tranche A must reduce the dependency
closure through clearer ownership; it may not raise a ceiling, conceal moved
source, add a renderer or clock, or change accepted paint and motion.
