one precise claim: the first `2` remains the result while `\times 1` yields.
It does not ask whether carrier preservation should yet be promoted or applied
to other expressions.

Please inspect natural playback, direct scrubbing, reverse, and phone width:

1. The surviving `2` should remain visually continuous, opaque, and unscaled.
2. `\times` and `1` should remain at their native pose and fade together,
   without scaling, translation, or feeding into result paint.
3. There should be no blank interval, duplicate complete equation, terminal
   snap, or ambiguity about which glyph survives.
4. The motion should contrast clearly with contributor fusion in

5. The treatment should remain legible in dark/light themes and at phone width.

back or retunes the Native KaTeX realization while preserving the semantic
