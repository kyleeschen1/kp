# Material Continuity And Artifact Morph Loop Closeout

Date: 2026-07-16

Run contract:
`run-contract.kp.animation.material-continuity-and-artifact-morph-v0`

## Outcome

The approved 24-slice repair loop is complete. KP now treats visible equation
parts as persistent visual material when their semantic or representational
identity survives a rewrite. Linear solving and rational-exponent-to-radical
rewriting no longer depend on adjacent source/target fades or independent
whole-expression transforms.

The implementation adds:

- stable material owners that survive state replacement, direction changes,
  direct seek, and accessibility projection;
- multi-step choreography envelope bridges that carry attention and motion
  continuously across operation boundaries;
- explicit material fragments and shared reconciliation bundles for
  representational succession;
- continuity budgets for owner identity, boundary displacement and velocity,
  attention, bundle convergence, native settlement, and structural
  deformation;
- continuity-aware gestalt identities and structural exemptions;
- live editor diagnostics for ownership, bundle state, native handoff, and
  motion eligibility;
- generated-animation promotion evidence that includes forward and rewind
  material-continuity proof.

## Linear Solve

The four-state solve sequence now uses one stage-owned material layer.
Persistent equation parts move to their next measured positions before native
state content takes over. Balanced terms are introduced only after space is
reserved. Inverse terms meet at nonzero size before collapse, and constant
operands approach the derived result on independent arcs.

The operation boundaries share one semantic sequence clock and bridged
attention envelope. Browser sampling enforces the same owner on both sides of
each boundary, sub-pixel boundary displacement, bounded velocity change, and
nonzero attention. Rewind samples the same material pose in reverse.

## Radical Succession

Rational exponent notation is decomposed into independent numerator, fraction
rule, and denominator fragments. Those fragments collapse toward one measured
opposite-corner bundle, then unfold as radical hook, overbar, and index
geometry. The base persists as the radicand rather than disappearing and being
redrawn.

The temporary fragments remain stage-owned until native KaTeX geometry is
aligned. A short native handoff crossfades coincident geometry, after which
temporary fragments are visually retired. Structural notation is exempt from
nonuniform organic deformation. The same material succession and bundle are
used under rewind.

## Gestalt And Editor Inspection

Organic token realization now derives its signature from a stable semantic
motion identity instead of the transient rendered-state id. Persistent linear
roles and the radical base/radicand therefore retain one micro-motion character
while moving between states. Path-relative realization preserves local
movement orientation, and thin fraction/radical structures receive restrained
micro-motion with zero squash/stretch.

The existing **Choreography & style** inspector now reports:

- persistent material-owner count and semantic motion-identity count;
- artifact-fragment count and the active reconciliation bundle;
- whether the frame is in material-fragment, native-handoff, or native-geometry
  settlement;
- material-continuant and structural-exemption counts.

These diagnostics update from the mounted equation stage without remounting it.

## Generated Authoring And Promotion

New generated animations remain semantic-authoring products: models specify
operations, lifecycle, lineage, salience, traversal, and disclosure rather than
coordinates or keyframes. Promotion now additionally requires:

- at least one measured material boundary or reconciliation sample;
- forward and rewind sampling;
- live continuity inspection;
- zero material-continuity quality violations.

This gate is intentionally independent of mathematical correctness. Invalid or
incorrect instructional states remain supported when their epistemic status
and disclosure policy are explicit; they must still animate with honest object
and material continuity.

## Remaining Work

- Persistent material ownership is complete for the canonical linear and
  radical proof cohorts. Other generated equation families still need
  operation-specific owner and bundle declarations where their lineage
  requires them.
- The promotion contract accepts measured continuity evidence, but an arbitrary
  LLM-authored animation still needs a general sampler that derives the
  relevant boundary and reconciliation probes from its compiled lifecycle.
- Path-relative organic realization currently uses equation-DOM geometry. The
  same semantic identity and eligibility contract should be implemented by SVG,
  canvas, and WebGL renderers.
- A paused frame during radical bundle collapse is necessarily compact and
  abstract; playback, direct seek around the bundle, and the inspector make the
  representational succession legible. Further style packages may choose a
  wider or slower bundle without changing the semantic choreography.
- Existing generated catalog entries remain warning-first legacy assets until
  each has complete governed promotion evidence.
