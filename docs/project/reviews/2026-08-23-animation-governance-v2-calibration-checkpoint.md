## Purpose

This checkpoint asks whether one candidate set of boundary and arrival laws is
visually coherent across three structurally different equation callers before
those laws become shared policy. It is evidence, not a migration or a claim
that the three families now use one presentation implementation.

The canonical reference is the governed log-product equivalence frame. The
derivative power-rule evaluation and finite-sum expansion are comparison
callers because they pressure, respectively, descendant provenance/evaluation
and large-operator typography/persistent relation context.

## Questions For Human Review

1. In the log equivalence middle phase, does the source remain a stable witness
   while the two factor continuants clearly cross the equality boundary?
2. Do the factor routes, receiving parentheses, derived `ln` operators, and
   connector arrive as one intelligible event, without collisions or a blank
   perceptual gap?
3. Does the derivative middle phase preserve the identity of the source
   exponent while making its two descendant roles understandable rather than
   merely decorative?
4. Do the finite-sum bounds remain attached to the summation operator, and do
   generated terms read as an expansion on both wide and narrow stages?
5. Across all three callers, do dark/light and wide/narrow variants preserve
   visual hierarchy, native endpoint typography, and a single dominant action?

## Acceptance And Preservation Boundary

Approval authorizes slice 23 to promote only the boundary-crossing and
arrival-cohort laws demonstrated by the reviewed evidence. It does not approve
derivative migration, finite-sum migration, a universal renderer, new motion
depth, timing changes, or any new animation family.

Semantic operations, correspondence identities, URLs, deterministic clocks,
native KaTeX endpoints, and existing approved family choreography remain fixed.
The rollback unit for this checkpoint is the capture command and this review
record; the governed log implementation remains the separately committed
candidate exemplar.
