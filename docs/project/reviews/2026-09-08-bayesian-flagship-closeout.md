## What is integrated

The canonical [Bayes Focus Card](http://localhost:8000/experiments/bayesian-reasoning/)
is available on the existing shared port-8000 server; HTTP 200 was checked at
closeout. Artifact `animation.probability.flagged-ticket-bayes` is distinguished
by its authored revision, not its legacy name. The immutable binary joint model,
exact arithmetic and issued trace own meaning. The shared Focus Card clock and
input drive the bounded SVG tree and canonical native KaTeX evaluation.
The diagnostic native-probe page is not the product reference.

One applied source now drives:

- Seven stops: population, first partition, joint tree, marginal, conditional,
  restored population and reordered tree. Buttons and keyboard visit stops;
  gestures and the slider retain continuous control between them.
- Atomic replacement of the tree, native equation, bound prose, count and
  projections. Invalid drafts retain the entire last-valid revision.
- Full and compact Article readings, a denominator reason with exact return,
  revision-pinned URL/history restoration, and prediction/reconstruction prompts.
- Download of the applied source and verified immutable local static editions,
  with no-JavaScript SVG/math/context and shared style/font snapshots.
- A second original urn problem with different numbers, reversed initial tree
  order and opposite posterior direction, using the same implementation.

The [authoring packet](../authoring/bayesian-reasoning-packet.md) is the operational
entrypoint: obtain an example, check JSON, apply it in the card, inspect the
derived views, download the applied source, and build/verify its local edition.
Neither an unapplied textarea draft nor a stale revision becomes publication
authority. Old distributed editions remain immutable; shared form changes take
effect when new editions are rebuilt.

## Capability boundary

This is exact two-event binary probability with two declared input forms and
the lesson query P(A|B), not arbitrary event algebra or general Bayesian networks.
Zero outcomes are supported; zero conditioning mass and unsupported numeric
display sizes return typed gaps. Tree order does not infer independence or
causation. Labels and explanatory prose are editorial, not empirically proved.

No arbitrary LaTeX-to-proof generation, CAS, continuous probability, automatic
free-prose grading, blanket motif/API promotion or curriculum coverage is claimed.
The host is development-only; local static publication does not deploy a new
public Bayes route. Code/Graph3D obligations and the tabled matrix asset retain
their status. No merge, deployment, accounts or scheduler was added.

## Was this the right loop, and what follows?

Yes, as a bounded domain-integration proof. The second problem primarily changes
source while preserving semantic truth across motion, reading, exact return,
practice and publication. The work also exposed real shared compiler and
accessibility-ownership costs. Those findings justify targeted consolidation;
they do not require replacing the semantic architecture.

The next recommendation remains **a bounded R4 frontend-convergence proposal**,
not another broad mathematical expansion or a universal authoring abstraction.
Start from a real author task and compare the existing TypeScript, bounded LaTeX
and model-draft entrypoints. Measure specialist intervention and repair, preserve
domain-owned code/Graph3D pressure, then promote only demonstrated common seams
and retire adjacent obsolete paths. Incorporate the deferred live trial when its
actual task and external payload are approved. The very small inference headroom
belongs in the initial cost baseline, not an end-of-run surprise.

Keep the minimal-check-in policy: one consolidated scope approval for the next
run, visual review only for genuinely new or materially changed treatment, then
autonomous nonvisual hardening. R4 and the M1–M6 horizons are **not activated** by
this closeout. The [reconciled horizon](2026-09-07-reconciled-authoring-loop-horizon.md)
retains their order and constraints.
