## Outcome

The approved 28-slice exemplar-first run is complete. The user approved the
economics demand-shift treatment at its mandatory checkpoint, and the accepted
role, state, theme, and treatment seam then passed a structurally different
native-KaTeX fraction-composition caller.

The promoted boundary is small and framework-neutral:

- `src/animation/semantic-visual-salience.ts` is the portable facade;
- `src/animation/semantic-visual-treatment.ts` resolves complete pure
  treatments from semantic visual inputs;
- economics remains the first SVG adapter and owns its graph geometry;
- `src/reader/app/fraction-composition-salience-adapter.ts` binds resolved
  treatments to authored native-KaTeX wrappers without recompiling math or
  taking layout authority;
- `src/rendering/semantic-visual-dom-theme.ts` projects the shared theme into
  DOM custom properties without creating a domain-local palette matrix.

Attention and preservation are explicitly different lineages. A term may
remain semantically present while instructional attention follows an
introduction, role change, fan-in, fan-out, cancellation, or removal. One
reader clock and one presentation revision determine the painted endpoint, so
direct links, direct seek, rewind, and restored checkpoints converge on the
same treatment.

## Proven Standards

- Authors select semantic roles, identity families, salience states, and
  presence rather than raw CSS colors or opacity values.
- Renderer adapters remain medium-specific and consume one pure resolved
  treatment.
- Dark and light themes preserve semantic hierarchy through separately tuned
  optical values.
- Native KaTeX owns settled glyphs, metrics, MathML, accessibility, and layout.
- CSS may project endpoint variables but does not own semantic timing or add an
  independent transition clock.
- Forced-colors uses system colors and a non-hue underline cue; reduced motion
  and no-JavaScript publication retain meaningful semantic checkpoints.
- Stable presentation revisions permit projection caching and atomic no-op
  paint skips.
- Framework hosts may compose the UI but do not own semantic state, runtime
  clocks, treatments, or renderer ports.
