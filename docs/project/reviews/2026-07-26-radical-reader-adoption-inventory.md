Date: 2026-07-26

## Finding

KP already has a verified radical semantic trace and a working generic
native-KaTeX compositor proof. It does not yet have a radical equation-reader
lesson, route, descriptor, or product paint selection. The approved work is
therefore an adoption through existing boundaries, not a new mathematical
operation or renderer.

The current editor radical remains a separate compatibility/reference surface.
It uses the older WebGL-assisted material treatment and retains the named
cross-renderer handoff residual. The new reader exemplar must not modify,
generalize, or share paint with that path.

## Verified proof already available

The governed fixture compiles
`transform.generated.radical.square-root-as-power.rewrite-power-as-root`
through the canonical v2 construction with the strict rational-exponent law.
The native compositor browser fixture already observes total source and target
paint, stable permutation-independent reconciliation, generic structural
tracks, and exact forward/reverse lifecycle coverage:

- forward: one persistence, one introduction, and three eliminations;
- reverse: one persistence, three introductions, and one elimination;
- no radical-named paint kind or lifecycle category;
- native KaTeX owns both settled endpoints.

These proofs make the reader adoption plausible. They do not prove the
learner-facing lesson, route, accessibility, responsive geometry, or exclusive
product paint ownership.

## Missing reader seam

The current equation-reader registry has no radical variant, and the route
manifest has no radical reader route. Canonical session eligibility is limited
to the solve-x cancellation and every transition in the numerator split/merge
lesson.

The smallest adoption path is:

1. annotate the existing radical source and target LaTeX with shared structural
   selector machinery;
2. add one radical lesson model/compiler and descriptor;
3. register one route and its native review checkpoints;
4. make the radical lesson's single transformation eligible for the existing
   canonical reader session;
5. in the same rollback unit, prove compatibility material frames are empty
   whenever that session owns paint.

## Preservation boundary

The adoption must preserve:

- the existing radical animation, transformation, correspondence, law,
  operation-pack pins, and governed request;
- exact native source `x^{1/2}` and target `\sqrt{x}` semantics and typography;
- reader annotations, hover/focus, semantic links, Cloze behavior, transcript,
  canonical URLs, responsive projection, reduced/static behavior, and exports;
- direct seek and rewind from the same semantic clock;
- the existing editor/WebGL radical, its diagnostics, and its named residual;
- all other reader routes and their frozen payload budgets.

## Rollback unit

The independently reversible unit is the new radical lesson content, compiler,
descriptor, route registration, canonical eligibility branch, and their
focused tests. Removing that unit restores the current reader registry without
touching the semantic asset, canonical compositor core, or existing editor
radical.

## Stop implications

Stop rather than adopt if implementation requires:

- radical-, glyph-, character-, KaTeX-class-, route-, or viewport-specific
  compositor geometry;
- a new paint kind, lifecycle, scheduler, renderer mode, material layer, clock,
  or serialized renderer session;
- glyph matching in place of the existing semantic lineage;
- simultaneous compatibility and canonical paint;
- mutation of native settled DOM, mathematics, typography, accessibility, or
  the existing editor/WebGL exemplar.
