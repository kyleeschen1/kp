## Decision

The native-KaTeX scene renderer wins bounded canonical promotion.

The approved solve-x cancellation transition now has exactly one moving-paint
implementation: `KpNativeKatexRendererSession`, loaded through the reader's
canonical equation session bridge. Its compatibility material frames are not
constructed and its compatibility layer is cleared before canonical paint
ownership. The temporary query selector and peer route are gone.

This is not a global equation-family rollout. Non-migrated reader transitions
keep their existing material path as explicit compatibility coverage until each
passes its own exemplar migration. The editor equation host remains active
because it owns broader catalog capabilities and was not part of this reader
migration.

## What structurally changed

- Native source and target DOM remain the only settled typography and semantic
  authority.
- Moving paint is ephemeral, inert, and stripped of focus, annotation, link,
  role, selector, MathML, Cloze, and interaction authority.
- One renderer session selects native continuity, atom transit, or checkpoint
  settlement from semantic lineage and measured capability.
- The scene vocabulary remains fixed at five paint kinds and six lifecycles.
- Fraction merge/split and radical succession use the same observation,
  reconciliation, sampling, ownership, and settlement mechanics.
- Generic target-style paint realization removes the late `(x+y)/2` size and
  baseline snap without character, fraction, plus, route, viewport, or
  KaTeX-class exceptions.
- Lineage-backed fission and fusion do not fade. Structural rule length and
  multiplicity change through geometry.
- The 7−3 successor presentation now preserves the complete ordered source
  expression on one readable inline band before continuous native settlement.
- Clone sanitation is centralized in `computed-style-clone.ts`; every moving
  clone loses semantic and interactive authority through the same low-level
  contract.
- Four governed provider responses compile to compiler-owned canonical traces
  and the same renderer session while provider-authored DOM, fragments,
  geometry, timing, style, fonts, renderer choice, and unverified mathematics
  are rejected.
