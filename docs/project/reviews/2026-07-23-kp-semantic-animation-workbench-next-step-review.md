Date: 2026-07-23

## Recommendation

Finish the current derivative checkpoint, then make a 5-7-slice Semantic
Animation Workbench control-plane tranche the first work in the successor
contract. Do not begin quadratic breadth until the workbench exemplar passes
human review.

| Candidate | Authoring | Reliability | Reuse | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Continue breadth immediately | 1 | 2 | 2 | 4 | Defer |
| Build the complete polished editor first | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Too broad initially |
| Build Workbench control-plane v0 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 2 | Do next |

## Why This Is Current

The project already has catalog descriptors, live previews, promotion metadata,
Theseus adapters, review capture identity, and a picker protocol proof. The
missing capability is a canonical derived index that reconciles those
authorities. A bounded v0 therefore pays down current tracking risk without
turning into a speculative editor rewrite.

The derivative, approved power-to-radical exemplar, and planned quadratic
provide the smallest useful lifecycle cohort:

- approved and playable;
- active and awaiting promotion;
- planned and not yet playable.

If those states are not understandable in one search-and-preview surface, the
model is not ready to generalize.

## Promotion Criteria

At the human checkpoint, the reviewer must be able to:

- find all three seed items from one fuzzy search field;
- see one canonical result rather than projection duplicates;
- play every concrete result in the Semantic Editor;
- understand what the current review is checking;
- distinguish roadmap state from maturity and review state;
- reach item-specific current and historical review evidence;
- switch among supported representations without changing animation identity.

## Estimated Envelope

The control-plane v0 is expected to take 5-7 focused slices. A complete
workbench with representation convergence, keyboard workflow, planned-item
navigation, validation, responsive behavior, and polish is expected to take
12-16 slices total. The successor long-run contract should preserve a human
checkpoint after the control-plane exemplar before quadratic implementation or
catalog-wide generalization.

## Deferrals

- Agenda redesign;
- a new review storage protocol;
- per-animation review rounds;
- manual duplication of Theseus state;
- wholesale branch merging without revalidating the picker adapter;
- broad animation-family migration before the exemplar checkpoint;
- integral, vector, matrix, arithmetic, or later curriculum breadth before the
  quadratic/workbench sequence is proven.
