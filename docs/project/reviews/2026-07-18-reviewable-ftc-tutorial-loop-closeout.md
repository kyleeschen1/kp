# Reviewable FTC Tutorial Loop Closeout

Date: 2026-07-18
Run contract: `run-contract.kp.tutorial.reviewable-ftc-v0`
Status: complete at `reviewable`; hard-stopped before gold

## Outcome

KP now has one real hermeneutic tutorial exemplar available in both contexts:

- editor: load the **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus** launcher at the top of
  the rendered-asset panel;
- learner: open `?view=ftc-tutorial`.

The module establishes accumulated area as a whole, isolates a finite added
strip, relates that strip to `ΔA` and a difference quotient, narrows guided
`Δx` bounds, and reintegrates the views through the continuity-qualified limit
and `A'(x)=f(x)`. A later net-change scene reuses the same accumulator and area
identity as `A(b)-A(a)=∫ₐᵇf(t)dt`.

The artifact is declared `reviewable`, with novelty `new-combination` and
`humanReviewRequired: true`. It is not gold.

## FTC-Specific Delivery

- three curated continuous exact lenses: `t²`, `1+t`, and `1+sin(t)`;
- stable axes and full curve, persistent accumulated-area material, and a
  movable upper-bound role;
- a finite added strip with exact area change and true lower/upper rectangle
  and quotient bounds, including the sine lens's interior maximum;
- four strictly decreasing guided `Δx` levels, all labeled finite
  approximations;
- notation ordered as strip → `ΔA` → finite quotient → limit → derivative
  identity;
- one optional, unscored, non-gating finite-versus-limit probe with an explicit
  intentional-invalid choice;
- pure direct sampling, exact mirrored rewind, order-independent seek, and
  branch rejoin laws;
- stable SVG semantic selectors, KaTeX in a fixed rail, full motion by default,
  and no nested animation scroll containers.

## Known Reviewable Gaps

- The equation rail is geometrically stable within a claim, but notation
  boundaries still replace authored KaTeX states. Human review should decide
  where token-level semantic morphing is required for gold.
- Organic motion is intentionally restrained to persistent graph material and
  focus breathing. It has not received perceptual tuning equivalent to the
  gold equation cohort.
- Narrated accessibility currently means synchronized textual narration, not
  voice synthesis. No human screen-reader session has been recorded.
- The three curated function lenses deliberately exclude arbitrary function
  entry and do not prove a general plotting capability.
- The net-change continuation is one later scene, not a complete second
  interpretive cycle.
- No BFS, economics, physics, upload/LLM generation, learner modeling,
  gamification, or universal scene infrastructure was started.

## Hard Stop And Next Action

Stop here. The next action is human review of the delivered FTC exemplar. That
review should produce a bounded change list and an explicit gold decision.
Only after it should KP reuse the proven kernel in BFS/Dijkstra or promote new
cross-domain vocabulary.
