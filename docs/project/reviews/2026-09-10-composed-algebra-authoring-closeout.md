## What this adds

One bounded authored chain now combines compound factoring with canonical
ink-glyph evaluation. The primary is
`2(x+3)+3(x+3)` → `(2+3)(x+3)` → `5(x+3)`.
Its product-shaped, opposite-orientation caller is
`(x*y)*2+(x*y)*4` → `(x*y)(2+4)` → `(x*y)*6`.
The repeated compound expression remains a coherent group, not independently
traveling glyphs. Both callers use operation-owned complete presentations,
authenticated adjacency, the same native KaTeX compositor and one semantic clock.
The second caller adds source JSON, not a bespoke renderer or timing plan.

The author can check, edit, Apply and export one verified revision. Its Focus
Card has three reading stops and two animated transitions, continuous scrubbing,
animated one-step controls and synchronous position/prose. Full/Compact readings,
prediction/reconstruction prompts, exact-position return and immutable local
static publication use that revision. Invalid, superseded or unprepared edits
retain the last valid displayed card; download does not export an unapplied draft.

The source supplies bounded ordinary LaTeX, real scalar declarations and editorial
explanation—not geometry, a renderer, timing or proof flags. Private proof and
presentation capabilities, exact endpoint checks, type-negative fixtures and
runtime authentication prevent ordinary callers from substituting an unrelated
animation. Actual paint/lifecycle tests establish the exercised compositor facts;
matching an asset ID alone does not.

## Repairs, hindsight and assurance limits

The accepted primary at `1b3a862d0` preserves compact motif character rather than
inflating arcs to eliminate rectangle overlap. Factoring inspects its complete
factor/context paint, coordinates receiver space, resolves contributors to the
same position/scale/emphasis, and releases emphasis after material settlement.
Transit contact is diagnostic; native endpoints and ownership remain strict.
The user accepted this treatment before second-caller promotion.

Pressure testing then found genuine shared defects: grouped numeric juxtaposition
did not round-trip through the scalar parser; a collapsed viewport could reach
native preparation; CommonMark emphasis could consume multiplication in display
math; and introduced glyphs skipped ink normalization when no source frame existed.
Each was repaired at its owning parser, lifecycle, Article or compositor seam
with deterministic regressions. The latter two exposed missing product-reading
math and a 0.35875-pixel WebKit delimiter seam. Neither the 0.25-pixel tolerance nor
accepted choreography was weakened. The generated exact reachability inventory
was refreshed through its owning generator rather than disabling freshness.

This is useful integration progress: a different compound shape and orientation
now reuse one verified mixed-operation path and its projections. It was expensive
relative to the narrow mathematical result. Much of that effort was uncovering
incomplete canonical integration, typography/lifecycle defects and release debt,
not creating a new factoring animation. Further work should reduce marginal glue
and maintenance risk, not use that investment to justify indefinite infrastructure.

Supported scope is exactly three states: collect two nonnegative safe-integer
multiples of the same compound sum/product in explicit left or right order, then
evaluate only their integer sum. Order and surrounding structure are preserved;
zero is allowed. Fractions, powers, functions, negative coefficients, arbitrary
chains, general polynomial factoring and a complete CAS are not supported here.
Valid mathematics outside supported presentation returns a repair gap. Editorial
prose is not proof. There is no new subject curriculum, deployed site, external
model trial, learning-outcome claim or universal compositor certification.
