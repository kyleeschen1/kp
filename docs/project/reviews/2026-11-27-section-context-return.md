## Explanation and medium

Question: when y=c becomes y=−c with d fixed, does the parabola change in its own
x/z coordinates? Assume the learner can substitute a number and square it;
explain that fixing y selects points in a plane. Since (−c)²=c², the resulting
expression for z is identical for every x. The planes differ when c≠0; at c=0
they coincide. Same planar curve does not mean the same points in 3D.

The static baseline is this substitution plus ordinary prose. Retain it in the
answer, including with WebGL unavailable. One existing section player lets the
reader compare the two planes and inspect them head-on without new choreography.
The prediction precedes the reveal and is a self-check, not automatic grading.
A changed-sign/zero case pressures the distinction; this does not establish
retention or learner transfer. Keep inherited typography and theme.
