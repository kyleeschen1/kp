# Finite Binder Expansion Long-Loop Proposal

Status: approved
Approved: 2026-08-21
Proposed contract: `run-contract.kp.finite-binder-expansion-v1`
Target: `kp.finite-binder-expansion`

## Objective

Establish a narrow, governed finite-binder expansion capability beginning with
the canonical sum

```latex
\sum_{i=1}^{3} a_i \longrightarrow a_1+a_2+a_3
```

and stop for human visual review before applying any presentation treatment to
the structurally different product caller

```latex
\prod_{k=0}^{2} x_k \longrightarrow x_0x_1x_2.
```

The capability must make bound-variable scope, inclusive limits, ordered body
instantiation, and one-to-many semantic lineage explicit. The source body is a
template; its three target occurrences are derived instances rather than one
persisted glyph cloned three times.

## Why This Is Current

The bounded TypeScript and Python generation frontends are closed. The accepted
post-root sequence selects one finite sum/product binder exemplar next because
it pressures semantics and paint that existing equation families do not:
scope, capture avoidance, repeated instantiation, limits, and large-operator
ownership. It also creates the narrow foundation needed by later sequence and
calculus work without treating integrals or derivatives as summation syntax.

## Canonical Reference And Acceptance

The semantic reference is the accepted Packet 4 decision in
`docs/project/decisions/2026-08-20-prudent-animation-family-breadth-cadence.md`.
The technical references are the canonical Native KaTeX compositor, immutable
endpoint ownership, one deterministic sampled clock, exact URL state, and the
Catalogue Review pipeline. There is no previously approved finite-binder
choreography; the sum exemplar is intentionally the visual discovery surface.

At the human checkpoint:

- the exact native source and target settle without correction;
- values `1`, `2`, and `3` instantiate `a_i` in unmistakable order;
- the binder, limits, body template, instances, and connectors retain correct
  semantic roles and lineage;
- source and target paint do not overlap, snap, or imply false persistence;
- direct seek, rewind, reduced motion, URL restoration, and Review capture
  remain deterministic; and
- the learner has one dominant action to follow.

Promotion requires an approved sum exemplar plus the product pressure caller.
Only caller-proven scope, iteration, and lineage may be shared. Addition and
multiplication semantics and choreography stay operator-owned. The broad
historical binder/calculus plan row may not become Direct from this evidence.

## Allowed Work

- Narrow the planned capability to finite binder expansion without claiming
  calculus coverage.
- Add typed sum/product binder roles, range and scope legality, expansion
  operations, lineage, case coverage, diagnostics, and corpora.
- Build one reversible Native KaTeX sum exemplar and its Catalogue checkpoint.
- After human approval, add the product pressure caller and promote only proved
  cross-caller infrastructure.
- Add governed discovery, authoring, readiness, and exact release evidence for
  the narrow family.

## Disallowed Work

- No infinite sums, sequence evaluation, recurrences, integrals,
  differentiation, Taylor series, arbitrary substitution, or arithmetic
  evaluation.
- No universal binder AST, generic calculus family, inferred semantic truth,
  or generic animation fallback.
- No changes to existing animation choreography, public lessons, reader
  layouts, graph families, code frontends, or live-model behavior.
- No product presentation generalization before the sum checkpoint is
  explicitly approved.

## Verification Cadence

Focused semantic checks run during discovery. Typecheck and Theseus validation
run at standard boundaries. Browser, responsive, cross-engine, architecture,
bundle, and broad compositor gates wait for the exemplar checkpoint, promotion,
and release boundaries. Stable scope-specific `npm run` commands will own any
recurring browser checks.

## Preservation And Rollback

Each slice is one independently reversible commit. The sum presentation asset
and adapter are the smallest visual rollback unit; semantic vocabulary and
typed gaps can survive rejection of its choreography. Existing assets,
semantic IDs, timelines, routes, and user-authored dirty files remain outside
the change boundary.

## Stop Conditions

Stop on ambiguous scope truth, accidental capture, false identity persistence,
duplicate paint, terminal snapping, a second clock, bundle/compiler leakage,
or any required change to existing approved choreography. Stop after `bf16`
until the user explicitly approves the finite-sum visual exemplar.
