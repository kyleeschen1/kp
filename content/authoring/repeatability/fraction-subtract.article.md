# Subtract in shared twelfths

Three quarters and one sixth count differently sized parts. Express both in
twelfths so that the subtraction compares counts of the same unit.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Keep the order of the subtraction as both fractions change their notation.
:::

Nine twelfths minus two twelfths leaves seven twelfths. This chain stops there;
there is no common integer factor to remove.
