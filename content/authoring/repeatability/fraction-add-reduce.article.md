# From tenths to halves

Two fifths and one tenth name differently sized parts. First express both counts
in tenths; then add those counts before simplifying the result.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Follow the unchanged quantity through alignment, addition and reduction.
:::

Five tenths and one half describe the same amount. The intermediate five tenths
makes the addition visible before the final change of unit.
