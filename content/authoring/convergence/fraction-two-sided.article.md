# Combine sixths and quarters in twelfths

A sixth and a quarter are differently sized parts. To add their counts, first
express both quantities in a shared unit.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Use twelfths: each sixth contains two, and each quarter contains three.
:::

Two twelfths plus three twelfths is five twelfths. Neither original quantity
changed when its notation changed.
