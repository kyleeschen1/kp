# Count eighths, then reduce

Three eighths and one eighth already share a unit. Splitting each part in two
gives another way to express the same sum and makes the value-preserving
renaming explicit.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Track the same quantity as the parts become smaller and their count increases.
:::

Six sixteenths plus two sixteenths is eight sixteenths. Group those eight parts
together: they occupy half of the whole. The extra alignment is deliberate
practice in equivalent fractions; it is not necessary for this addition.
