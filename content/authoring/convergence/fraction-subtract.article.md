# Subtract quarters from sixths in their written order

Five sixths and one quarter count differently sized parts. Twelfths let us
subtract counts of the same unit.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Rename both fractions while keeping the first quantity first.
:::

Ten twelfths minus three twelfths leaves seven twelfths. Reversing the terms
would change the sign, so their order is part of the reasoning.
