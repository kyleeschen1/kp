# Subtracting more than we started with

One sixth is smaller than three quarters, so their difference will be negative.

:::kp-passage{#counting-parts}
Express both quantities in twelfths before subtracting their counts.
:::

Two twelfths minus nine twelfths is negative seven twelfths. The order of the
subtraction determines the sign.
