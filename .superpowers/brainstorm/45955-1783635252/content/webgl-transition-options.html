Where this helps

  • Exponent or fraction notation collapsing into a radical index/glyph.
  • Square brackets leaving after entries settle, then parentheses arriving later.
  • Non-persistent operators fading as visual material instead of pretending to preserve identity.

Guardrails

  • Do not use pixel morphing for semantic identity unless the semantic plan says identity is preserved.
  • Keep DOM fallback and reduced-motion paths.
  • Test rendered pixels/boxes, not just style strings.
